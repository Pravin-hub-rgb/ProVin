# 1.5 Legal & Regulatory Framework (SEBI & Global Guidelines)

Pichli file mein tujhe poora flow mil gaya — code API se baat karta hai, broker exchange tak pahunchata hai. Matlab technically tu ab algo bana ke chal sakta hai. Lekin ruko — kya koi bhi algo bana ke market mein daal sakte ho? Nahi. Rules hain, aur yeh rules jaanna utna hi zaroori hai jitna coding. Agar future mein algo trader banna hai, toh regulatory landscape pehle samajhni padegi — warna baad mein problem hogi.

## SEBI Kya Regulate Karti Hai

India mein algo trading **SEBI** (Securities and Exchange Board of India) regulate karti hai. Iska primary goal hai — establish a strong regulatory framework for algos in the stock market.

SEBI ki definition se shuru karte hain, kyunki yehi decide karta hai ki tera system "algo" hai ya nahi:

> **"Algorithmic trading = any order generated using automated execution logic."**

Matlab agar koi bhi order automatic execution logic ke through generate hota hai, toh SEBI ke criteria mein wo ALGO ke andar aata hai. Tera simple moving average crossover bot bhi, aur Jane Street jaisi HFT firm ka system bhi — dono isi definition mein aate hain.

Is framework ke 3 core objectives hain:

1. **Protect investors** — investors ko protect karna
2. **Ensure market integrity** — market integrity ensure karna
3. **Prevent unfair trading practices** — unfair practices rokna

Aur regulate karne ka actual purpose kya hai? Market mein unregulated strategies chal rahi hain — jaise bid-ask spread pe chalne wali strategies, ya high-frequency firms ka kaam. In HFT (High Frequency Trading) players ko regulate karna hi SEBI ka main purpose hai. API-based trading ke rise ke saath oversight aur stricter hui hai — focus transparency, risk control, aur regulated strategies pe hai.

Abhi tak SEBI ke mota-moti 2 circulars hain algo trading ke regarding. Dono padh loge toh idea lag jayega ki market mein kaam karna hai toh kaunsa regulatory framework tere ALGO pe lagta hai.

## Algo Classification — White Box vs Black Box

SEBI ne ALGO ko do broad categories mein classify kiya hai:

**White Box ALGO:** User logic dekh sakta hai aur samajh sakta hai. Transparency hoti hai — rules visible hain. Simple definition: **white box = wo ALGO jo TU khud banate ho.**

**Black Box ALGO:** Rules aur logic tujhse hidden rakhe jaate hain. Wo kisi proprietary cheez hoti hai. Simple definition: **black box = wo ALGO jo dusre bechte hain, jiska logic tujhe nahi dikhta.**

Ab dono ko point by point compare karte hain:

**Logic:** White box mein transparent — tu dekh bhi sakta hai, modify bhi kar sakta hai. Black box mein hidden/proprietary — dekh nahi sakta.

**Retail use:** White box algo tu broker API ke through use karta hai. Black box commercial/subscription model pe milta hai — jaise kaafi websites hain jo subscription pe ready-made algos bechti hain, wo sab black box category mein aati hain.

**RA License (Research Analyst):** White box ke liye license required nahi. Black box ke liye mandatory — agar tujhe black box algo banana hai jo tu kisi ko bech sake, toh SEBI se Research Analyst ka license lena zaroori hai.

**Documentation:** White box mein lighter requirement. Black box mein logic ka paper banake rakhna mandate hai — aur wo paper SEBI ko submit bhi karna padta hai (research analyst license ke saath compliance mein).

**Exchange approval:** White box ke liye not required. Black box ke liye mandatory.

**Marketing:** White box mein marketing fair honi chahiye. Black box mein misleading performance claims bilkul nahi kar sakte — kisi bhi tarah ka false/gumraah claim nahi.

Ek real example se pakka karte hain. Maan le tujhe TradeTrun jaisi website banani hai — jahan tu apna algo dusro ko beche. Toh tujhe chahiye hoga: (1) Research Analyst ka license, (2) logic ke papers mandate karne padenge, (3) user ko jo logic becha wo likh ke rakhna padega — detailed documentation, (4) exchange approval lena padega.

Lekin agar tu apne liye khud algo banata hai — white box — toh in sab se pareshan hone ki zaroorat nahi. Bas broker API se connect karo aur chalao.

## Order Frequency Threshold — 10 Orders Per Second Rule

White/black box ek classification hai. Dusri classification hoti hai — order frequency ki. Aur yeh threshold SEBI ne clearly set ki hai:

> **10 orders per second**

Isi number se participants categorize hote hain, aur yahi decide karta hai ki kitni compliance chahiye:

```
                ┌──────────────────────────┐
                │  Order Frequency Check   │
                └────────────┬─────────────┘
          ┌──────────────────┴──────────────────┐
          ▼                                     ▼
< 10 orders per second                > 10 orders per second
     = LFT                                  = HFT
(Low Frequency Trading)            (High Frequency Trading)
```

### LFT — Low Frequency Trading

Tere normal strategies yahin aati hain. Indicator-based strategies, rule-based strategies, moving average crossover, Bollinger Bands strategy, Donchian Channel breakout — yeh sab LFT hai, kyunki inmein 1 second mein 10 orders wali baat hi nahi hoti.

LFT ke liye requirements:

- Exchange approval: **not required**
- Broker approval: **required** — broker tujhe tag kar sakta hai
- SEBI registration: **not required**
- RA license: **not required**
- Audit trail: **maintain karna padega**
- Client information: **zaroori hai**

Dekha? Retail algo trader ke liye yeh kaafi light hai. Audit trail aur client info maintain karo, bas.

### HFT — High Frequency Trading

10 orders per second exceed karte ho? Toh requirements bahut strict ho jaati hain:

1. ALGO ka registered + approved hona NSE/BSE se MUST
2. SEBI registration required
3. System audit mandatory — SEBI ka registered auditor tera audit karega
4. Regulatory scrutiny — SEBI and exchange inspections and stress tests conduct karte hain market resilience aur fairness ensure karne ke liye
5. SEBI/NSE kabhi bhi scrutiny kar sakte hain

HFT ke examples: bid-ask trading jisme ek second mein threshold ke upar orders dalte ho high speed pe, aur Jane Street jaisi HFT firms ka kaam.

Tu abhi beginner hai toh teri strategies LFT hi hongi — tension mat le. Par boundary pata honi chahiye, kyunki jab strategy fast banegi toh yeh line cross hoti hai.

## Compliance Pathway — Jab Bhi Naya Algo Banaye

Future mein jab bhi koi ALGO create karega — apne liye ya client ke liye — yeh 3-step pathway follow karo:

```
STEP 1: Define Your ALGO
        │
        ├── White Box hai?  → Transparent logic (jo tumne banaya)
        └── Black Box hai?  → Proprietary logic (dusro ka becha hua)
        │
        ▼
STEP 2: Check Your Order Frequency
        │
        ├── Low frequency?   → < 10 orders per second
        └── High frequency?  → > 10 orders per second
        │
        ▼
STEP 3: Us Category Ki Compliances Follow Karo
        │
        ├── Low frequency  → LFT wali compliances
        └── High frequency → HFT wali compliances
```

Step by step samajh:

1. Sabse pehle check karo — mera ALGO white box hai ya black box?
2. Uske baad check karo — meri frequency 10 orders/sec se zyada hai ya kam?
3. Low frequency hai toh LFT ki compliances list dekho
4. High frequency hai toh HFT ki compliances list dekho
5. Yahi decide karta hai ki regulatory framework mein tu kya-kya kar sakta hai

## Nutshell

SEBI ki definition yaad rakh: **automated execution logic se generate hua koi bhi order = algo trading.** Apna algo classify karo do axes pe — **White Box / Black Box** (logic dikhta hai ya nahi) × **LFT / HFT** (10 orders/sec se neeche ya upar) — aur us category ki compliances follow karo. Retail ke liye white box + LFT hi normal raasta hai.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Har algo ke liye SEBI registration nahi chahiye.** Sirf black box (bechne wale) aur HFT (>10 orders/sec) walon ke liye heavy requirements hain. Apna white box LFT algo bina registration ke chala sakte ho.
- ❌ **White box matlab sasta/weak algo nahi.** White box sirf matlab hai ki logic transparent hai jo tu khud banata hai. Jane Street level strategy bhi white box ho sakti hai agar logic visible ho.
- ❌ **Regulation sirf bade players ke liye nahi.** Audit trail maintain karna aur client information dena — yeh retail LFT trader ke liye bhi applicable hai.
