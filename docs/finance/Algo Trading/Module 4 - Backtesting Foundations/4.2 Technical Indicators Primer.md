# 4.2 Technical Indicators Primer

## Indicator Kya Hota Hai?

Raw candlestick data sirf numbers deta hai: open, high, low, close, volume. Par "RELIANCE ka close 2456.30 hai" dekh ke koi decision nahi hota. Decision ke liye **context** chahiye:

- Price apne average se upar hai ya neeche?
- Move kitna tez hai ya slow?
- Volatility badh rahi hai ya ghat rahi hai?

**Indicator = raw price/volume se derive kiya gaya calculation jo context deta hai.** Jaise cricket mein sirf runs nahi dekhte — strike rate, average dekhte hain. Woh sab batsman ke "indicators" hain.

Indicators ke 3 families hain:

| Family | Kya Measure Karta | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Trend** | Market kis direction mein hai | EMA, Super Trend |
| **Momentum** | Kitni speed se move ho raha | RSI, MACD |
| **Volatility** | Kitna hil raha hai | Bollinger, ATR |

Chalo shuru karte hain sabse simple se.

## SMA — Simple Moving Average

Sabse basic indicator. **Pichle N candles ke close ka simple average.**

5-day SMA of closes [18, 17, 16, 18, 17]:

```
(18 + 17 + 16 + 18 + 17) ÷ 5 = 86 ÷ 5 = 17.2
```

Naya candle aaya? Window slide karo, purana drop karo, naya add karo, phir divide by 5.

**Kya batata hai:** Current price 19 hai aur SMA 17.2 → price apne recent average se upar hai → uptrend ka hint.

**Problem:** Purani candles ko utna hi weight milti hai jitni nayi ko. Kal ka move aur 5 din purana move — dono barabar count. Par trading mein **fresh information zyada important** hoti hai. Isi problem ka solution mila: EMA.

## EMA — Exponential Moving Average

EMA bhi average hai, par **nayi candles ko zyada weight** deti hai. Iska formula recursive hai:

```
EMA_today = α × Close_today + (1 − α) × EMA_yesterday

jahan α (smoothing factor) = 2 ÷ (N + 1)
```

Example: 9-period EMA ke liye α = 2 ÷ (9+1) = **0.2**. Matlab har nayi candle ka close 20% weight laata hai, aur kal ka EMA 80% weight rakhta hai — jismein pichli saari information compressed hai.

⚠️ **Yahan ek classic galti hoti hai jise abhi hi pakad lo:** Log EMA calculate karne ke chakkar mein bas N candles ka simple average nikal lete hain — "last 9 candles ka average = 9 EMA" likh dete hain. **Woh SMA hai, EMA nahi!** Sahi EMA recursive hota hai — har nayi value purani EMA ke upar build hoti hai. Agar tu manual loop se EMA banayega aur `talib.EMA()` se compare karega toh numbers match NAHI karenge agar tune SMA nikala hai. Yeh confusion bahut students ko backtest galat karwata hai — kyunki unki "EMA strategy" asal mein SMA pe chal rahi hoti hai!

**SMA vs EMA quick check:**

| | SMA | EMA |
|---|---|---|
| Weighting | Sab candles equal | Recent candles heavy |
| Reacts to new moves | Slow | Fast |
| Formula | Sum ÷ N | Recursive with α |

**Crossover concept (bahut important):** Do EMA lagao — fast (9) aur slow (21). Jab fast wali slow wali ko **upar cross** kare → bullish signal. Neeche cross kare → bearish signal. Yeh "EMA crossover" duniya ki sabse famous beginner strategies hai — agla-backtest isi pe hoga.

Aur ek precision point jo code likhte waqt kaam aayega: **crossover EVENT vs crossover STATE** alag cheezein hain.
- **State:** 9 EMA > 21 EMA hai (abhi is moment pe upar hai)
- **Event:** Pichli candle mein 9 ≤ 21 tha **aur** is candle mein 9 > 21 ho gaya (abhi-abhi cross hua!)

Strategy design karte waqt yeh farq profit-loss decide karta hai. Code mein event detect karne ka pattern:

```python
crossed_up = (fast_ema[i] > slow_ema[i]) and (fast_ema[i-1] <= slow_ema[i-1])
```

## RSI — Relative Strength Index

**Momentum oscillator, 0 se 100 tak ki range mein.**

Idea: Pichle N candles mein kitna movement **upar** hua vs kitna **neeche**? Upar wale moves zyada hain → buyers strong → RSI high.

- **RSI > 70:** Overbought — price bahut tez chadha hai, thakan aa sakti hai (pullback possible)
- **RSI < 30:** Oversold — bahut gira hai, bounce possible

Formula ka core: `RSI = 100 − (100 ÷ (1 + RS))` jahan `RS = avg gain ÷ avg loss` over last 14 candles. Math ratne ki zaroorat nahi — library karegi (agli file). Intuition yaad rakh: **speedometer of buying/selling pressure.**

**Use case:** Mean-reversion strategies ("RSI 30 se upar cross hua toh buy") ya momentum confirmation ("price bhi upar, RSI bhi 60+ — strong trend").

## VWAP — Volume Weighted Average Price

**Volume-weighted average price = (Price × Volume) ka cumulative sum ÷ cumulative volume**, typically din ke start se.

Simple average sab candles ko barabar treat karta hai. VWAP kehta hai: *"jahan zyada volume hua, wahi price zyada matter karta hai."* Jis level pe sabse zyada trading hui, woh institutional traders ka reference level ban jaata hai.

- Price **VWAP ke upar** → buyers control mein
- Price **VWAP ke neeche** → sellers control mein
- VWAP often acts as support/resistance during the day

**Use case:** Intraday traders ka benchmark — "main VWAP se achhe rate pe khareeda?" Institutions apne executions ko VWAP se compare karte hain. Isliye VWAP ke paas price react karti hai — self-fulfilling prophecy.

Note: VWAP **roz reset** hota hai (din ke start se) — isliye yeh intraday indicator hai, swing trading mein use nahi hota.

## MACD — Moving Average Convergence Divergence

Teen cheezon ka combo, teeno EMA-family se:

```
MACD Line   = 12-EMA − 26-EMA        (fast minus slow)
Signal Line = MACD Line ka 9-EMA
Histogram   = MACD Line − Signal Line
```

Intuition: MACD Line batata hai **do trends ka gap**. Fast EMA slow se upar hai → gap positive → uptrend. Gap badh raha → momentum badh raha.

Signals:
- **MACD crosses Signal upward** → bullish
- **MACD crosses Signal downward** → bearish
- **Histogram shrink ho raha** → momentum kamzor pad raha (trend thak sakta hai)

Basically EMA-crossover ka packaged version with extra smoothing — trend-followers ka favourite.

## Bollinger Bands — Volatility Ka Puri

Ek SMA (usually 20-period) ke upar-neeche **±2 standard deviation** ki bands:

```
Middle Band = 20-SMA
Upper Band  = SMA + 2 × StdDev
Lower Band  = SMA − 2 × StdDev
```

Standard deviation (2.5 file mein seekha tha — spread of data) yahan volatility ka proxy hai. Volatility badhi → bands phaili. Volatility ghati → bands sikud gayi (**squeeze** — badi move ka warning).

Intuition: Statistics kehti hai normal data ~95% time ±2 SD ke andar rehta hai. Toh:
- Price **upper band touch kare** → statistically expensive (short-term stretched)
- Price **lower band touch kare** → statistically cheap

**Use case:** Mean-reversion ("band touch → revert expected") ya breakout trading ("squeeze ke baad band break = new trend").

## ATR — Average True Range

**Volatility ka thermometer — kitne rupaye ka average move ho raha hai per candle.**

True Range = sabse bada inmein se:

```
1. Aaj High − Aaj Low
2. |Aaj High − Kal Close|
3. |Aaj Low − Kal Close|
```

(Doosre do cases gap handle karte hain — kal close 100, aaj open 105 → woh gap bhi volatility hai.)

ATR = True Range ka N-period average (typically 14).

**Use case:** Stop-loss placement! Fixed ₹10 SL Reliance ke liye chhota hai par MRF ke liye pagalpan. ATR-based SL: `SL = entry − 2 × ATR` — har stock/timframe ke liye automatically adjust. Risk management (Module 2) ka favourite tool.

## Super Trend

Ab woh indicator jo 4.1 mein example tha. **Super Trend = price ke upar/neeche chipka hua trend-line jo ATR use karta hai.**

Kaam kaise karta hai (conceptual):
1. Ek base line banao using price ± multiplier × ATR (typical: 3 × ATR on 10-period ATR)
2. Price base ke **upar** rahe → line neeche rehti hai, **green** (uptrend)
3. Price base ke **neeche** jaaye → line flip ho jaati hai upar, **red** (downtrend)
4. Flip hone pe hi signal milta hai — "Buy jab green ho, Sell jab red"

Fayda: trend follow karta hai, sideways mein whipsaw kam deta (ATR filter), aur visually crystal clear hai — isliye beginners se advanced tak sab use karte hain. Exact calculation thodi technical hai; jab zaroorat padegi code mein, library function use karenge — intuition kaafi hai.

## Cheat Sheet — Konsa Indicator Kab

| Indicator | Family | Ek Line Mein |
|---|---|---|
| SMA | Trend | Simple average — baseline |
| EMA | Trend | Weighted average — fresh moves matter |
| Crossover | Signal | Fast/slow EMA cross = trend change |
| RSI | Momentum | 0–100 speedometer; 70/30 levels |
| VWAP | Benchmark | Volume-weighted fair price (intraday) |
| MACD | Momentum/Trend | Two EMAs ka gap + signal line |
| Bollinger | Volatility | SMA ± 2SD bands; squeeze = warning |
| ATR | Volatility | Average rupee-move per candle; SL sizing |
| Super Trend | Trend | ATR-based flipping trend line |

## Nutshell

Indicators = raw data se derived context. Teen families: **Trend** (SMA, EMA, Super Trend — direction), **Momentum** (RSI, MACD — speed), **Volatility** (Bollinger, ATR — range). Sabse critical technical point: EMA recursive hai (α = 2/(N+1)), SMA nahi — yeh galti mat karna. Crossover mein event ≠ state. ATR stop-losses ke liye, VWAP intraday benchmark ke liye. Har indicator ek lens hai — koi bhi akela perfect nahi.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Indicator = prediction machine nahi.** RSI 70 pe "girna pakka" nahi — sirf probability ka hint. Strong trend mein RSI hafton tak 70+ reh sakta hai.
- ❌ **Zyada indicators = better analysis nahi.** Paanch indicators lagao jo sab same underlying data (price) se bane hain — woh 5 confirmations nahi, 1 confirmation 5 baar hai. Redundancy ko diversity samajhna classic trap hai.
- ❌ **EMA/SMA interchangeable nahi hain.** Same window pe bhi values alag hongi — signals alag din aayenge, backtest results alag aayenge. Jo strategy likho, usi indicator ke saath test karo.

**In Your Own Words**

1. EMA aur SMA mein kya farq hai, aur α = 2/(N+1) kya karta hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** SMA sab candles ko equal weight deta hai (sum ÷ N). EMA recursive hai: `EMA = α×Close + (1−α)×EMA_prev` — nayi candles zyada weight paati hain, isliye fresh moves pe faster react karti hai. α = 2/(N+1) smoothing factor hai jo weight set karta hai: 9-EMA ke liye α=0.2 matlab aaj ka close 20% contribution, kal ka EMA 80%. Common trap: log N candles ka simple average nikal ke usko EMA bol dete hain — values talib.EMA se match nahi karenge.

</details>

2. Crossover EVENT aur STATE mein farq? Detection logic kya hogi?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** State = abhi fast>slow hai (kabhi bhi cross ho sakta tha). Event = ABHI cross hua — pichli candle fast≤slow thi aur current mein fast>slow ho gaya. State-based entry har upar-wali candle pe re-enter kar sakti hai; event-based sirf crossing candle pe ek baar. Detection: `(fast[i] > slow[i]) and (fast[i-1] <= slow[i-1])` — dono candles check karna zaroori hai.

</details>

3. RSI 70/30 levels ka matlab kya hai, aur kya RSI 70 = guaranteed fall?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** RSI pichle ~14 candles mein buying vs selling pressure ka ratio hai (0–100 scale). 70+ = overbought (tez buying, thakan possible), 30− = oversold. Par guaranteed kuch nahi — strong trending market mein RSI hafton tak 70+ reh sakta hai jabki price aur chadhta hai. Isliye RSI ko kabhi akela signal nahi maante — confirmation ke saath use karte hain.

</details>

4. ATR-based stop loss fixed rupee stop loss se better kyun hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Har instrument ki volatility alag hai. Fixed ₹10 SL volatile stock ko noise mein hit karwa dega, aur calm stock ke liye unnecessarily bada risk hai. ATR = average per-candle move, toh `SL = entry − 2×ATR` automatically har stock/timeframe ke liye adjust hota hai — volatile ko jagah, calm ko tight stop. Risk per trade bhi predictable ho jaata hai (position sizing ke liye).

</details>

5. Bollinger Band "squeeze" kya batati hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Bands = SMA ± 2 standard deviations, aur SD volatility ka measure hai. Jab bands sikud jaati hain (squeeze) = volatility historically low = market compressed hai. Volatility mean-reverting hoti hai — low ke baad expansion aata hai. Isliye squeeze ko breakout traders "badi move coming" ka early warning maante hain (direction band break se pata chalta hai).

</details>
