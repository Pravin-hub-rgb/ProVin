# 4.1 Backtesting vs Forward Testing

## Do Phases Kyun?

Soch: tune ek strategy banayi — "jab yeh hota hai toh buy karo." Ab kya tu seedha real paise laga dega? Nahi. Pehle **test** karna padega ki yeh strategy paisa banati hai ya udati hai.

Testing ke **do major phases** hain:

| Phase | Data | Kya Test Hota Hai |
|---|---|---|
| **Backtesting** | Historical past data | Strategy ka past performance |
| **Forward Testing** | Future unseen data | Strategy ka real-time performance |

Aur ek interesting baat — insaan ka dimaag **short-term data bias** mein phans jaata hai. Tujhe pichle 15–30 din ya max 6 mahine ke moves yaad rehte hain, par market ke cycles **2–4 saal ke** hote hain. Isliye rule: edge confirm karne ke liye **2–4 saal ka data** backtest karo, phir future data pe forward test karo.

## Backtesting — Past Pe Test

**Definition:** Apni trading strategy ko historical market data pe apply karke evaluate karna ki woh kaisa perform karti — isi ko backtesting kehte hain.

Simple example se samajh:

- Rule: "**Buy jab Super Trend green ho, Sell jab red ho**"
  - (SuperTrend ek popular indicator hai jo price ke saath line banata hai — green matlab uptrend, red matlab downtrend. Agli file mein ise formulas ke saath seekhenge.)
- Step by step process:
  1. Past data CSV mein load karo (Open, High, Low, Close)
  2. Python + pandas se code likho — har candle pe rule check karo
  3. **3–5 saal ke data** pe chalao
  4. Result dekho: profit bacha? Haan → strategy mein **edge hai**

### Backtesting Kyun Zaroori Hai

- **Edge confirm** karna — 5 saal profitable rahi toh luck nahi, kuch hai strategy mein
- **Parameters identify** karna — Super Trend ki setting change karke dekho, kya better result aata hai
- **Risk, drawdown, profitability samajhna** — sirf profit nahi, kitne dukh ke saath aaya woh bhi
- **Losing strategy pe time waste na karo** — pehle hi din pata chal jaata hai
- **Long-term behavior samajhna** — trend following strategies mein poora saal drawdown phase ho sakta hai; yeh normal hai ya nahi, tabhi pata chalega

### Good Backtesting Ke Liye Kya Chahiye

1. **High-quality historical data** — intraday ke liye 1-min ticks, positional ke liye daily/EOD
2. **Proper coding logic** — no look-ahead bias (jaldi explain karunga)
3. **Realistic assumptions** — slippage, brokerage, stop-loss gaps include karo

### Metrics Jo Dekhne Hain

Yahan bas naam yaad rakho — har metric ka formula aur code **file 4.6** mein detail se aayega:

| Metric | Kaam |
|---|---|
| **CAGR** | Annualized return |
| **Maximum Drawdown** | Sabse bada loss phase |
| **Sharpe Ratio** | Risk-adjusted return |
| **Win Rate** | Kitne trades profit mein |
| **Profit Factor** | Gross profit ÷ Gross loss |
| **Avg Trade Return** | Per trade expected return |
| **Exposure & Holding Period** | Position kitni der open rehti hai |

Benefits: fast, free evaluation; saal ka data minutes mein test; bull/bear/sideways teeno regimes ka behavior dikh jaata hai.

## Backtesting Ki Limitations ⚠️

Backtest perfect nahi hai. Yeh 6 traps hain:

1. **Overfitting / Curve Fitting** — Strategy past data pe perfectly fit ho gayi par future mein fail. Example: Nifty 2020 crash ke baad upar gaya → tune "sirf long" strategy banayi = curve fitting. Past ≠ future.
2. **Survivorship Bias** — Sirf aaj ke stocks ka data use kiya; jo companies delist ho gayi (band ho gayi) unhe ignore kar diya. Unka data daala toh results girega.
3. **Data Mining** — Itne combinations try kiye ki kahin na kahin takle successful combination mil hi gaya. Woh skill nahi, coincidence tha.
4. **Execution Difference** — Backtest points calculate karta hai; real market mein slippage aati hai. Entry pe bhi slip, exit pe bhi slip.
5. **Ignoring Transaction Costs** — Sabse zyada log yahan marte hain. Calculation dekho: 500 trades × cost = **150% cost!** Matlab 50%-profitable strategy bhi −100% net ho sakti hai.
6. **Look-ahead Bias** — Aisi information code mein use karna jo us waqt available hi nahi thi (jaise din ke end ka close dekh ke subah ka trade "lena").

### Stop-Loss Handling Galat Hone Ki Problem

Backtest code aksar **closing candle pe SL check** karta hai. Real market mein SL **wick mein hit** ho jaata hai — aur specially options mein lagbhag **5% trades** aisi hoti hain jahan wick itna fast tha ki chart pe tick hi nahi dikhta! Isko realistically model karna aage seekhenge.

## Forward Testing — Future Pe Test

**Definition:** Strategy ko future unseen data pe test karna — paper trading ya walk-forward simulation ke through.

Kyun zaroori hai jab backtest already pass ho gaya?

- **Market structure badalta rehta hai** — jo past mein chalta tha, future mein na chale
- **Robustness check** — real conditions mein consistent profitable hai ya nahi
- **Overfitting pakadna** — false edges, high slippage strategies yahan expose hoti hain
- **Execution-sensitive strategies** — sirf real environment mein prove hoti hain

### Jo Cheezein Sirf Forward Test Dikhata Hai

| Factor | Backtest | Forward Test |
|---|---|---|
| Real spreads & order book impact | ❌ | ✅ |
| Live slippage | ❌ | ✅ |
| Real latency | ❌ | ✅ |
| Real-time volatility shock | ❌ | ✅ |
| Drawdown ke time ki psychology | ❌ | ✅ |

Woh last point sabse underrated hai — backtest mein −40% sirf number hai; live account mein −40% teri neend udati hai.

## Forward Testing Ke Do Tarike

| Tarika | Kya Hai | Pros / Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Paper Trading** | Live market conditions, virtual money | ✅ Real market test ❌ Emotions test nahi hote |
| **Walk Forward Optimization** | Data ko training set mein optimize karo, testing set pe validate karo — multiple slices pe repeat | ✅ Regimes-crossing stability check ❌ Thoda complex |

Walk forward ka basic idea: data do hisson mein baanto. Pehle hisse pe parameters optimize karo (training), dusre hisse pe validate karo (testing) — aur yeh kai slices pe repeat karo. Yeh check karta hai ki strategy alag-alag market regimes (bull, bear, range-bound, volatile, extreme volatile) mein survive karti hai. Iski poori mechanics hum **Module 5** mein detail se build karenge.

## Forward Testing Ke Metrics

Forward test ke dauran track karo:

| Metric | Kya Track Karna |
|---|---|
| **Win Rate deviation** | Backtest se kitna hat gaya |
| **Slippage per trade** | Real slippage kitni aa rahi |
| **Execution speed** | Order kitni jaldi fill ho raha |
| **Real DD vs Expected DD** | 40% expect kiya, 70% mila = problem |
| **Missed signals** | Kuch signals miss ho rahe hain kya |
| **Strategy stability** | Month-to-month consistent hai |
| **Expectancy per trade** | Consistent hai ya ghir rahi hai |

**15–20% Deviation Rule:** Backtest aur forward test ke numbers ke beech 15–20% ka farq common aur healthy hai — yeh easily aata hai. Par agar 50%+ deviation hai, toh live jaane se pehle ruk kar socho.

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Aspect | Backtesting | Forward Testing |
|---|---|---|
| Data | Past | New unseen live data |
| Speed | Fast | Slow |
| Cost | Zero | Low |
| Accuracy | Theoretical | Realistic |
| Overfitting risk | High | Low |
| Emotions | Nahi | Haan |

Dono zaroori hain — backtest skip kiya toh pata hi nahi chalega edge hai ya nahi. Forward test skip kiya toh pata nahi chalega real world mein chalegi bhi ya nahi. Aur haan — **backtest pass, forward fail** hona bhi possible hai (regime change, higher slippage, unexpected volatility).

## Nutshell

Testing ke do phases: backtest (past data, free & fast, edge confirm) → forward test (future unseen data, realism confirm). Backtest ke traps: overfitting, survivorship bias, data mining, slippage ignore, transaction costs (500 trades = 150% cost!), look-ahead bias, wick-stop problem. Forward test ke do tools: paper trading (virtual money) aur walk forward (train/test slices). 15–20% deviation healthy hai. Dono pass → tabhi live.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Backtesting = guarantee nahi.** Past performance future ka promise nahi hai. Backtest sirf batata hai ki strategy ne *kabhi* kaam kiya hai — kabhi kaam karna abhi bhi kaam karna nahi.
- ❌ **Forward testing = live trading nahi.** Paper trading mein real paisa nahi lagta. Woh ek rehearsal hai, performance nahi.
- ❌ **Profitable backtest = safe strategy nahi.** Curve-fitted strategy backtest mein hero, live mein zero hoti hai. Isliye forward test mandatory hai.

**In Your Own Words**

1. Backtesting aur forward testing mein fundamental difference kya hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Backtesting past ke historical data pe strategy chalata hai — fast, free, theoretical. Forward testing future ke unseen data pe chalata hai (paper trading ya walk-forward) — slow, low-cost, realistic. Backtest edge confirm karta hai; forward test real-world robustness confirm karta hai. Dono different questions ka jawab dete hain: "yeh kabhi kaam karti thi?" vs "abhi bhi kaam karti hai?"

</details>

2. Transaction costs ignore karne se kya disaster ho sakta hai? Numbers se samjhao.

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Har trade pe brokerage + slippage + taxes lagte hain. Agar 500 trades hue aur per-trade cost ~0.3% hai, toh total cost = 150% of traded capital. Matlab agar strategy gross mein +50% banati hai, net result −100% ho sakta hai — high-frequency strategies yahan sabse zyada maarti hain kyunki unke trades zyada hain. Isliye backtest mein costs model karna mandatory hai.

</details>

3. Look-ahead bias kya hai? Ek example do.

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Aisi information use karna jo us decision-time pe available hi nahi thi. Example: candle i ka close dekh ke candle i ke andar hi trade lena — close toh candle khatam hone pe hi pata chalta hai, toh real time mein woh entry impossible thi. Ya aaj ki knowledge se pichle saal ke news events ko strategy mein hardcode karna. Backtest unrealistically achha dikhta hai, reality mein woh entries exist hi nahi karti.

</details>

4. Paper trading mein emotions kyun test nahi hote?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Kyunki real paisa risk mein nahi hota. Virtual ₹50,000 loss hua toh dil dhak se nahi dhadakta — bas ek number kam ho gaya. Real account mein wahi loss neend udane wala hota hai, panic exits karwata hai, overtrading karwata hai. Psychology strategy ka hidden component hai jo sirf apne paise pe activate hoti hai — paper trading usko measure nahi kar sakti.

</details>

5. 15–20% deviation rule kya kehta hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Forward test ke metrics (win rate, returns, drawdown) backtest ke numbers se 15–20% tak deviate karna normal aur acceptable hai — real spreads, slippage, latency ka farq itna toh aayega hi. Is range ke andar strategy healthy maani jaati hai. 50%+ deviation red flag hai — matlab backtest assumptions galat the ya regime badal gaya — live jaane se pehle investigate karo.

</details>
