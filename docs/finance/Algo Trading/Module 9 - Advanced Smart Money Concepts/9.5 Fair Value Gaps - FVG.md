Order Blocks ne entry zones diye. Ab doosra footprint — **Fair Value Gap (FVG)**, jo imbalance ka game hai: jab price itni tez chalti hai ki beech ke levels pe "sahi trading" hui hi nahi. Yeh gaps price ko wapas kheenchte hain — aur humein entries dete hain.

# 9.5 Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

## Imbalance Kya Hota Hai

Normal market mein har price level pe buyers-sellers dono active hote hain — continuous auction. Par jab koi bada news/order-flow aata hai:

```text
Price 100 → 104 → 110 (teen candles, ek hi direction mein tez)

100-104 ke BEECH ke levels (101, 102, 103) pe
proper two-sided trading hui HI NAHI —
buyers itne desperate the ki unhone seedha upar khareed liya
```

Yeh **imbalance** hai: kuch price levels "fairly traded" nahi hue. SMC kehta hai — market aise gaps ko baad mein revisit karke "fair value" complete karti hai.

## FVG Ki Detection Rule — Teen Candle Pattern

FVG teen consecutive candles se detect hota hai:

```text
BULLISH FVG:
    Candle 1: high = 100
    Candle 2: badi green candle (98 → 108)
    Candle 3: low = 106
    
    GAP = Candle 1 HIGH (100) se Candle 3 LOW (106) ke beech
    Yeh zone kabhi "touched" nahi hua trading mein!
```

Rule ek line mein:

> **Candle 1 ka high aur Candle 3 ka low OVERLAP na karein → beech ka space = FVG**

(Bearish FVG mirror hai: candle 1 low vs candle 3 high.)

⚠️ Notice: **middle candle** gap banati hai par gap uske body se define nahi hota — sirf candles 1 aur 3 ke extremes se. Log yahan galti karte hain.

## Visual

```text
        |    |
      | |    |   ← candle 3 (low 106)
      | |  █ |   
      | |  █ |   ← candle 2 (big green)
    █ | |  █ |   
  → █ █ █  █ |

  candle 1     gap zone: 100–106
  (high 100)   = BULLISH FVG
```

Gap zone 100–106 mein price ne trade nahi kiya. Ab agar price 105 pe wapas aati hai — woh **untraded territory** mein hai.

## FVG Se Trade Kaise Hoti Hai

Template (bullish FVG, uptrend context):

```text
1. Context: bullish structure (BOS ho chuka)
2. Impulsive move ne FVG chhoda
3. Price pullback karti hai → FVG zone mein enter karti hai
4. Confirmation: zone ke andar bullish reaction candle
5. Entry: confirmation close
6. SL: FVG ke neeche (ya candle 1 ke low ke neeche — safer)
7. Target: next liquidity / recent high
```

**Confluence bonus:** agar FVG aur OB overlap karein (same area), zone double-strong — do independent footprints ek jagah.

## Fill Hona Mandatory Nahi — Par Tendency Hai

Honest framing: "market har gap 100% fill karti hai" — myth hai. Jo sach hai:

- Tez moves ke baad **pullback tendency** strong hoti hai (overextension fatigue)
- FVG zones act as **magnets/support** during those pullbacks
- Partial fills zyada common hain than full fills

Isliye trade plan mein FVG tap + reaction dekhna, blind limit order lagana nahi.

## Inversion FVG (IFVG) — Quick Advanced Note

Ek twist: agar price FVG ko **todh ke paar close** kar de, toh wohi gap apna role ULTA le leta hai — bullish FVG break = ab bearish resistance. Isko inversion kehte hain. Logic: jo support fail ho gaya, woh failed-buyers ka bagage ban jaata hai. Abhi bas naam yaad rakho; use advanced contexts mein hota hai.

## Nutshell

FVG = 3-candle imbalance: candle-1 high vs candle-3 low no-overlap zone (bullish). Matlab: levels fairly trade nahi hue. Use: impulsive leg ke baad pullback magnet/entry-zone; confluence with OB = strong. Full-fill guaranteed nahi — reaction-based entry lo. IFVG: broken gap flips role. Middle candle gap nahi banati — extremes matter.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **FVG ≠ opening/closing gap of price charts.** Daily chart ka "gap-up" (kal close vs aaj open) alag concept hai. FVG INTRADAY candle-to-candle imbalance hai — same word, different thing.
- ❌ **Har gap fill hota hai — nahi.** Strong trend momentum mein FVGs hafton unfilled reh sakte hain (woh exhaustion memory banti hain, tradeable nahi). "100% fill" statistics survivorship-flavored claims hain.
- ❌ **Gap size = strength nahi directly.** Bada gap bata sakta hai move aggressive thi — par tradeability location+context se aati hai (structure ke saath), size akela nahi.

**In Your Own Words**

1. Bullish FVG detection rule exact likho (candles ke terms mein).

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Teen consecutive candles lo. Agar CANDLE 1 ka HIGH < CANDLE 3 ka LOW, toh beech ka poora vertical space = bullish FVG. (Middle candle ne upar ki taraf itna gap create kiya ki sides overlap nahi hui.) Code form: `fvg_exists = c1.high < c3.low; zone = (c1.high, c3.low)`. Bearish mirror: `c1.low > c3.high`. Common mistake: middle candle ki body/wick se zone banana — galat, sirf 1 aur 3 ke extremes.

</details>

2. FVG "pullback magnet" kyun behave karta hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Do reasons: (1) Mechanical — impulsive move mein late-arrivers/institutions ke pending buy-limits un untraded levels pe rakhi jaati hain ("fair price pe lena hai"), toh pullback wahin rukta hai jahan demand orders baithe hain. (2) Psychological/technical — bahut traders same visible gap mark karte hain aur wahin orders daalte hain (self-fulfilling clustering — 9.4 ka principle). Net: untraded zone = natural demand shelf during retracement.

</details>

3. FVG + Order Block confluence kyun stronger hota hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Kyunki do ALAG logics ek jagah converge karte hain: OB = last-opposite-candle before impulse (order accumulation story); FVG = untraded imbalance within/beside the move (auction-gap story). Independent frameworks same price-area point karein toh false-positive probability dramatically girti hai — jaise do alag witnesses same baat bolein. Practical: overlap region ko refined entry zone maano, SL tight rakho, RR improve hota hai.

</details>

4. Blind limit order in FVG vs reaction-based entry — kaunsa better aur kyun?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Reaction-based better (shuruat ke liye). Blind limit = "price touch hote hi buy" — par gap-fill mandatory nahi; price zone ke THROUGH nikal sakti hai (trend too strong ya reversal aa gaya), tab tumhare paas falling knife milti hai. Reaction approach: zone mein AANE ke baad bullish rejection candle wait — market khud confirm karta hai ki demand activate hui. Cost: entry thodi worse (confirmation ke baad). Benefit: failure-rate bahut kam. Advanced traders hybrid karte hain: partial blind + partial confirmed.

</details>
