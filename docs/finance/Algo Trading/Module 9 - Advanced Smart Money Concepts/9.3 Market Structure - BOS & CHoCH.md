# 9.3 Market Structure: BOS & CHoCH

## First SWINGS — Building-Blocks!

To read market-structure, define SWING-POINTS:

```text
SWING-HIGH = candle whose BOTH sides'
             highs are LOWER!
             (local-MOUNTAIN ⛰️)

SWING-LOW = candle whose both sides'
            lows are HIGHER!
            (local-VALLEY 🏞️)
```

These swings create STRUCTURE

(Dow-theory's 100-YEAR-old core!):

| Pattern | Structure |
|---|---|
| Higher-Highs + Higher-Lows (HH+HL) | **UPTREND!** |
| Lower-Highs + Lower-Lows (LH+LL) | **DOWNTREND!** |

Simple, OBJECTIVE, codeable — three things we like! (5.3!)

## BOS — Break-of-STRUCTURE!

> **BOS = price BROKE LAST-swing-point in TREND-DIRECTION!**
>
> = CONTINUATION-signal! ✅

Example:

```text
UPTREND:
Swing-High: 100 → price fell to 96 → then CLOSED at 101!

100-BROKEN (upward!) = BULLISH-BOS!!

Meaning: BUYERS so strong they even ate old-high!
Trend-CONTINUATION evidence! ✅
```

After BOS expectation: trend continues on its path —

pullbacks are buying-opportunities!

(discount — 9.6's teaser! 👀)

## CHoCH — Change-of-CHARACTER!

> **CHoCH = price broke FIRST important SWING against TREND!**
>
> = first reversal-WARNING! ⚠️

Example:

```text
UPTREND running: HH-120 → HL-112 → HH-130...

Now price broke HL-112 and CLOSED below!!

= FIRST time in uptrend a swing-low BROKE!
= FIRST CRACK in bullish-structure!
= BEARISH-CHoCH!! ⚠️
```

Name itself tells: *CHARACTER changed!*

Reversal not CONFIRMED yet —

but "buy-the-dip" DEFAULT no longer!

After CHoCH:

```text
1. WATCHFUL-waiting / protect longs!
2. CONFIRMATION needed:
   if then LH forms + LL = downtrend-SHIFT-COMPLETE!
```

## Both SIDE-BY-SIDE!

| | **BOS** | **CHoCH** |
|---|---|---|
| Break-direction | WITH trend ✅ | AGAINST trend ⚠️ |
| Meaning | CONTINUATION | Possible-REVERSAL |
| Action-bias | FOLLOW trend! | ALERT-mode; protect trades! |
| Strength | STRONG-signal | EARLY-warning |

**Memory-trick:**

```text
BOS = "Boss is SAME!" (trend continues!)
CHoCH = "CHARACTER changed!" (wind-SHIFTED!)
```

## Internal-vs-EXTERNAL Structure (Quick!)

Advanced-traders see structure on TWO-levels:

```text
EXTERNAL = MAJOR-swings (whole-trend's TURNING-points!)
INTERNAL = small PULLBACK-swings within the leg!
```

Start with EXTERNAL focus!

Internal gives more NOISE!

Just know distinction EXISTS —

because YouTube people MIX them and CONFUSION happens!

## Nutshell

Swings (local-peaks/valleys!) → structure: HH+HL = UP, LH+LL = DOWN. **BOS** = trend-direction-break = CONTINUATION-proof! **CHoCH** = counter-trend-FIRST-break = reversal-WARNING (confirmation-pending!). Combo-with-liquidity: sweep + CHoCH = FADE-setup; break + BOS = CONTINUATION-setup! Focus on EXTERNAL-structure, internal later. This framework OBJECTIVELY-codeable — PYTHON-version in 9.7!

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **CHoCH ≠ GUARANTEED-reversal.** Only FIRST-warning — after CHoCH price can RESUME (fakeout-of-fakeouts!!)! Hence CHoCH = ALERT, not ENTRY; needs CONFIRMATION-structure!
- ❌ **Not every minor-level-cross = BOS.** Wick-TEMPORARY-cross vs BODY-CLOSE break — DIFFERENT-weight! And major-swing vs micro-swing difference (external-vs-internal!). Don't make NOISE into signal!
- ❌ **Structure-DRAWING ≠ exact-science.** Two-traders mark slightly-DIFFERENT-swings (timeframe/fractal-choice!). Hence FIX your RULES (minimum candles each side of swing?!) and SAME-rules backtest! **CONSISTENCY > PERFECTION!**
