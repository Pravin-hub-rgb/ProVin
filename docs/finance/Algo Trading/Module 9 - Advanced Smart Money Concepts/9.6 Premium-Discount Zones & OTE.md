OB aur FVG ne bataya KAHAN entry milegi. Par ek filter baaki hai jo average SMC trader ko pro se alag karta hai: **location quality.** Ek bullish OB downtrend mein milna = trap; uptrend ke discount mein milna = gift. Yeh file woh lens deti hai.

# 9.6 Premium/Discount Zones & OTE

## Range Ki Baat Karein

Kisi bhi significant move (swing low → swing high) ko lo. Us range ko do hisson mein baanto:

```text
Swing High: 120  ←─────────────────┐
                                   │  PREMIUM (upar wala aadha)
Equilibrium: 110  ─────────────    │  "mehenga" zone
                                   │
                                   │  DISCOUNT (neeche wala aadha)
Swing Low:   100  ←────────────────┘  "sasta" zone

Equilibrium (EQ) = range ka exact 50% = 110
```

| Zone | Price Kahan | Matlab |
|---|---|---|
| **Premium** | EQ ke UPAR | Range-relative mehenga — buyers late aa rahe |
| **Equilibrium** | Bilkul beech | Fair price |
| **Discount** | EQ ke NEECHE | Range-relative sasta — value buying area |

## Core Rule — Simple Aur Powerful

```text
BUY setups sirf DISCOUNT mein dhoondo
SELL setups sirf PREMIUM mein dhoondo
```

Logic khareedari ki dukaan jaisa hi hai: sale mein samaan lo, MRP pe mat lo. Bullish OB discount mein = institutions apni position saste bana rahe hain. Wahi OB premium mein = shayad sirf retail FOMO.

⚠️ Range selection hi skill hai: kaunsa swing-low-to-high leg lo? Answer: **impulsive structure-breaking leg** (wahi jo BOS diya). Chhote pullbacks ki range nahi.

## OTE — Optimal Trade Entry

Ab precision layer. Discount ke andar bhi poora area barabar nahi hota. Fibonacci retracement levels lagao (range ke 100% se wapas):

```text
Range: 100 → 120

Retracement levels:
  50%  = 110   (equilibrium)
  61.8%= 107.6 ←┐
  70.5%= 105.9  ├─ OTE ZONE
  79%  = 104.2 ←┘

OTE = 62%–79% retracement band
```

> **OTE = deep-discount sweet spot** — itna girna ki value ho gayi, itna nahi ki trend hi toot gaya.

Statistical logic: trends rarely reverse from 30-40% pullback (shallow), aur 85%+ pullback often means structure failed. 62-79% = "trend intact, maximum value" intersection.

## Sab Kuch Jodte Hain — Confluence Stack

Ab tak ke tools ka priority order:

```text
1. STRUCTURE     → direction (uptrend? BOS hua?)
2. LOCATION      → discount mein entry dhoondho (premium nahi!)
3. ZONE          → us region mein OB ya FVG tap ho
4. CONFIRMATION  → reaction candle zone ke andar
5. TARGET        → opposite liquidity pool
```

Har layer false-positive probability ghatati hai. Ek akela OB = okay trade. Structure + discount + fresh OB + confirmation = high-quality trade. Yeh stacking hi SMC ka asli method hai — koi single "magic pattern" nahi.

## Example Walkthrough (Numbers Ke Saath)

```text
1. NIFTY uptrend: HL 21,800 → HH 22,200 (BOS ✅)
2. Range: 21,800–22,200 → EQ = 22,000
3. Price pullback start... 22,000 cross kiya = DISCOUNT entered
4. 21,950 pe red candle (yeh impulsive down-leg se pehle wali thi)
   = bearish candle in discount + FVG bhi wahin
5. Confirmation: bullish engulfing at 21,960
6. Entry: 21,965 | SL: 21,900 (structure/OB low neeche)
7. Target: 22,200+ (old high liquidity)
   Risk 65 pts, Reward 235+ pts ≈ 1:3.6 ✅
```

Premium mein same setup milta toh SKIP — yeh discipline hi edge hai.

## Nutshell

Range ke 50% se upar premium (sell-hunt area), neeche discount (buy-hunt area). Rule: buys only discount, sells only premium. OTE = 62-79% fib band within discount = max-value-min-risk pocket. Range = impulsive structure-breaking leg. Full stack: direction (structure) → location (discount) → zone (OB/FVG) → confirmation → target (liquidity). Stacking = quality control.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Fibonacci magic nahi hai.** 61.8/70.5/79 numbers ka koi cosmic power nahi — yeh practical band hai jo "deep but not broken" retracement define karta hai. Value distribution-based reasoning hai, mysticism nahi.
- ❌ **Static zones nahi.** Range change hui toh premium/discount bhi shift — naye swing ke saath recalculate. Purani range ka discount nayi range ka premium ban sakta hai. Always current-leg based.
- ❌ **Discount = automatic buy signal nahi.** Location filter hai, trigger nahi. Discount + OB/FVG + confirmation teeno chahiye. Sirf "sasta dikha" pe entry lena falling knife pakadna hai.
