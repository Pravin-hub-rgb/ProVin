# 2.5 Statistical Thinking — Mean, Variance, Correlation & Distribution

Pichli file mein ek sawaal chhoda tha: strategy A aur strategy B dono ne 15% return diya — kaunsi better? Same return, par ek predictable thi aur ek roller-coaster. Yeh judge karne ke liye chahiye **statistics.** Aur sach yeh hai — finance pura ka pura data hai, aur bahut saare traders isliye fail hote hain kyunki statistics nahi aati unhe. Statistics tujhe teen cheezein deti hai: market behavior samajhna, risk measure karna, aur profitable system build karna.

Dar mat — Python mein yeh sab 2 minute mein calculate hota hai (agli file mein karenge). Pehle concept samajh, kyunki calculation toh computer karega, samajhna tujhe hi padega.

## Mean — "Itna Expect Kar Sakte Ho"

Mean = average = saari values ka sum ÷ values ki count.

```
Values: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
Sum = 15, n = 5 → Mean = 3
```

Trading mein mean ka matlab hai **expected value of returns** — average itna return expect kar sakta hai.

- Nifty ka daily return average ~**0.05%** hai — yeh expected daily return hai
- Ek algo trader ke 5 din ke returns ka mean nikala toh **0.46% per day** aaya

Yeh number kyun matter karta hai?

- **Expected profitability evaluate karta hai** — system mein dum hai ya nahi
- **Mean reversion strategy** isi pe chalti hai — price mean ki taraf wapas aayegi ka assumption (yeh strategies baad mein banayenge)
- **Strategies compare** karta hai — dono ka mean dekho, jo behtar expectation wali wohi
- **Expectancy per trade** nikalne mein help karta hai

Ek line mein mean: *"itna expect kar sakte ho"* — guaranteed nahi, par expectation setting hoti hai.

## Variance & Standard Deviation — Risk Ka Measure

Ab dusra sawaal — returns kitne spread hote hain apne mean se?

**Variance** batata hai data mean se kitna faile hue hai. Lekin ek problem hai — variance square terms mein aata hai (units squared), jo directly samajhna mushkil hai.

Isliye hum use karte hain **Standard Deviation (SD)** = variance ka square root. Simple units mein wahi spread.

Trading mein SD ka matlab:

- **Kitna volatile asset hai** — SD zyada = zyada volatility
- **Kitni risky strategy hai** — zyada SD = zyada risk
- **Return kitna uncertain hai** — profit bhi uncertain

### Strategy Comparison — Wahi Sawaal Jo 2.4 Se Laye The

Do strategies, dono ka mean same:

| Strategy | Returns | SD | Nature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 0.5, 0.4, 0.6 | Low | Predictable ✅ |
| **B** | 3, -2, 4 | High | Unpredictable ⚠️ |

Dono ka average similar hai. Par Strategy A har din consistent deti hai; B kabhi 3 deta hai kabhi -2. Ab jawab mil gaya pichli file ke sawaal ka — **same mean ho toh bhi low variance strategy better hai.**

Golden insight likh ke rakh:

> **High return + high variance < Moderate return + low variance**

Zyada SD = zyada volatility = zyada risk = kam chances of sustainable profit.

Practical uses: volatility measurement, position sizing (2.4 wala pillar 2!), stop loss levels, portfolio risk control, aur Sharpe ratio calculation — sab isi ek number pe khade hain.

## Correlation — Do Cheezon Ka Rishta

Correlation batata hai — ek cheez move kare toh doosri kaisa move karti hai. Range: **+1 se -1.**

- **+1:** Perfectly same direction — ek upar, doosra upar
- **0:** Koi relationship nahi — dono apni alag duniya mein
- **-1:** Perfectly opposite — ek upar toh doosra neeche

Real example: equity market aur Gold ka correlation negative hota hai — equity upar jaaye toh gold neeche (aur kabhi-kabhi ulta).

### Trading Mein Kyun Itna Important

Sabse bada use case: **multi-strategy portfolio.** Do strategies chalayi, dono ka correlation nikal:

- Correlation **0.9** = dono almost saath-saath chalti hain. Jab ek drawdown mein jaayegi, doosri bhi drawdown mein hogi → **drawdown increase**, risk concentrated
- Correlation **0.1** = dono independent. Ek drawdown mein ho toh doosri shayad profit mein ho → **drawdown dramatically reduce**

Yaad hai 2.4 wali story — 5 strategies, sabka drawdown ek saath aa gaya? Uski asli wajah yehi thi: wo strategies highly correlated thin, chahe instruments alag the. Correlation check karna hota, sirf instrument names nahi.

Baaki uses: hedging (opposite correlation wale assets jodo), pair trading (correlated pairs pe trade), market neutral strategies.

## Distribution — Data Ka Pattern

Distribution batata hai — data mein kitni baar kaunsi value aati hai. Frequency pattern.

Teen types trading ke liye zaroori hain:

### Normal Distribution (Bell Curve)

Symmetric pattern mean ke around. Iske famous rules:

- 68% data **1 SD** ke andar
- 95% data **2 SD** ke andar
- 99% data **3 SD** ke andar

Volatility models isi assumption pe bante hain.

### Fat Tail — Financial Data Ki Asli Sachchai

Yahan twist hai. **Financial data normal distribution follow NAHI karta — fat tail hota hai.** Matlab extreme events expected se ZYADA frequent aate hain.

Black Swan events — circuit lagana, gap-up/gap-down opening — ye theoretically rare hone chahiye the, par practically bahut aate hain. Consequences:

- Stop loss zyada hit honge jo tune socha tha
- Agar tera backtest normal distribution assume karta hai → **results over-optimistic honge**
- Gap risk real hai — isliye 2.4 mein overnight position ka risk gina tha

### Skewed Distribution — Asymmetric Pattern

Jab data ek taraf zyada weight rakhta hai:

| Type | Pattern | Example | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Negative Skew** | Small wins + occasional large loss | Option selling | Dangerous ⚠️ |
| **Positive Skew** | Small losses + occasional large win | Trend following | Excellent ✅ |

Yeh table poore trading career ka cheat code hai:

- **Trend following positively skewed hai** — chhote losses lagte rehte hain, par kabhi-kabhi bada trend pakad leta hai jo sab cover kar deta hai. Isliye trend following achhi strategy mani jaati hai.
- **Option selling negatively skewed hai** — roz chhota premium kamata hai (feel-good), par kabhi-kabhi itna bada loss aata hai ki mahino ka premium ek saath gaya.

Same average profit ho sakta hai dono ka — par skew dekh ke pata chalta hai kis strategy mein raat ki neend sukoon se aayegi.

## Nutshell

Statistics = data-driven decisions, feeling-based nahi. **Mean** = expected value (Nifty avg 0.05%/day) — expectation setting aur strategy comparison ke liye. **SD** = spread from mean in simple units — jitna zyada, utna risky; golden rule: moderate return + low variance > high return + high variance. **Correlation** (+1/0/-1) — multi-strategy portfolio mein correlated strategies (0.9) drawdown badhaati hain, uncorrelated (0.1) ghataati hain. **Distribution** — financial data fat tail hota hai (extreme events zyada, backtest over-optimistic ban jaata hai), aur skew dekho: positive skew (trend following) excellent, negative skew (option selling) dangerous.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Mean = guaranteed return nahi.** Sirf expectation hai. Ek din mean se 10x upar bhi ja sakta hai, neeche bhi.
- ❌ **Normal distribution ko financial data pe blindly apply mat kar.** Fat tail reality hai — extreme events normal assumption se zyada aate hain, isliye backtests over-optimistic bante hain.
- ❌ **High correlation sirf numbers ka coincidence nahi.** Do strategies ka correlation 0.9 hai matlab underlying logic similar hai — instruments alag hona kaafi nahi, risk phir bhi concentrated hai.
