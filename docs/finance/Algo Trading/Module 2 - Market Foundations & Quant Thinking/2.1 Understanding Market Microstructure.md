# 2.1 Understanding Market Microstructure

Module 1 mein humne dekha ki tera order kahan jaata hai — code se API, API se broker, broker se exchange. Ab ek level andar chalte hain. Jab tu button dabata hai (order place karta hai), toh us order ke execute hone tak aur uska PnL tere paas aane tak beech mein kya-kya hota hai? Yeh poora system kehlata hai **market microstructure** — aur algo trader ke liye isse samajhna must hai. Isko **engine of the market** bhi keh sakte hain — jaise engine gaadi ko A point se B point tak le jaata hai, waise hi microstructure market ke andar ki saari machinery hai.

Is file mein 4 parts cover honge:

1. Introduction to Market Microstructure
2. Order Types and Order Book Dynamics
3. Price Formation, Bid-Ask Spread aur Liquidity
4. Transaction Cost and Slippages (algo trading ke liye sabse important)

## Part 1 — Microstructure Kya Hai

Formal definition yeh hai: **"It is the study of how trades happen, how prices move, and how orders interact inside the exchange."**

Aur simple words mein — microstructure matlab button dabane se lekar order execute hone tak, aur PnL tere paas aane tak, jo bhi cheezein beech mein aati hain un sab ki understanding.

Kya-kya aata hai ismein? Dekh:

- Tera order execute kaise hota hai?
- Order mein timestamp hota hai, price stamp hota hai
- Do logon ka order same hai toh partially filled ko pehle milega ya fully filled ko?
- Hidden orders ko pehle preference milegi ya kis ko?
- Server kaise react karta hai?

Yeh SAB ki understanding = market microstructure ki understanding.

### Exchange Ka Kaam Kya Hai

Base samajhne ke liye exchange ka kaam dekhte hain:

1. Exchange sab logon se orders collect karta hai — bahut saare logon ne bids lagayi, bahut saare logon ne asks lagayi, bahut saari shares ke, bahut saare index ke orders
2. Ye sab collect karke ek **ORDER BOOK** banata hai
3. Order book mein ek taraf bid hoti hai, dusri taraf ask
4. Saari bids ko ekkatha karta hai, saari asks ko ekkatha karta hai
5. Phir unko match karta hai

Microstructure mein hum isi ke andar ki cheezein samjhenge — order matching, order time, iceberg order, hidden order, price formation, liquidity, order flow, total volumes, bid-ask spread, market participants ka behavior, impact cost.

### Algo Trading Ke Liye Ye Kyun Important Hai

Char solid reasons hain:

**1. Slippage determine karna:** Microstructure samjhega tab jaanega ki teri slippage kaisi hogi. Example — strategy mein agar market order dalne par bahut zyada slippage aa rahi hai, toh wahan limit order use kar sakte ho. BUT sab jagah limit order use nahi karna!

**2. Limit order ka trade-off:** Kyun nahi sab jagah? Kyunki limit order use karoge toh kuch trades chhoot jaate hain — 10% se 15-20% trades miss ho jaate hain. Aur un missed trades mein se agar koi badi trade chhoot gayi, toh poori dynamics/metrics kharab ho jayegi. Solution hai chhote-chhote differences pe limit order dalna — order book ki help se (yeh aage seekhenge).

**3. High probability entries:** Microstructure knowledge se hi high probability entry aur execution decisions le sakte ho.

**4. Directly affected strategies:** Scalping, option selling, intraday strategies — in sab pe microstructure ka direct effect hota hai.

## Part 2 — Order Types Aur Order Book

Theory mein bahut saare order types hote hain, par unhe samajhne ka koi matlab nahi jab tak real life mein use na ho. Hum sirf 3 major types samjhenge jo course mein aur real life mein helpful hongi:

1. Market Order
2. Limit Order
3. Stop Loss Order

Pehle order book basics samajh lo, kyunki teeno types isi pe based hain. Order book mein ek taraf buyer hota hai, dusri taraf seller. Buyer apna best price deta hai, seller apna best price deta hai.

- **Best Bid = highest buy price** (sabse zyada buy price)
- **Best Ask = lowest sell price** (sabse kam sell price)

Real example — Pakka Limited: buy side ka highest price (best bid) ₹116.16 tha, sell side ka lowest price (best ask) ₹116.42/₹116.43 tha.

### Type 1 — Market Order

Definition: *"Jo bhav mein ho, us bhav mein TURANT khareed do"* — koi price specify nahi karte, turant execution chahiye.

Isko samajhne ke liye ghar-boli analogy lete hain. Soch Pakka Limited ka share ek ghar hai jise tu khareedne ja raha hai. Us ghar ke andar boli lag rahi hai:

- Ek aadmi keh raha hai: *"yeh ghar 116 rupaye mein bikega"*
- Ek aaya: *"main yeh ghar bechunga 117 rupaye ka"*
- Ek aaya: *"116 rupaye assi paise ka"*
- Phir koi aaya: *"main yeh ghar bechunga 116 rupaye 43 paise ka"*

Toh sell side ki bids aise lag rahi hain. Ab:

**BUY market order dalte time:** Tu bolta hai "mujhe yeh ghar jis bhav mein ho, us bhav mein turant khareed do." Tujhe milta hai sell side ka LOWEST price (best ask) → ₹116.43. Rule yaad rakh: buy market order pe hamesha sell ki lowest price milti hai.

**SELL market order dalte time:** Tujhe milta hai buy side ka HIGHEST price (best bid). Example mein order book mein buyer side ka highest 116.16 tha → wahi milega. Chart pe jab sell market order dala toh 116.21 wala price mila — jo buyer side ka top price tha.

Practical check: jab buy kiya chart mein dikha sell ka sabse lowest price 116.42 — wahi mila. Matlab: **buy karte time sell side ki lowest bid milti hai; sell karte time buyer side ki highest bid milti hai.**

### Type 2 — Limit Order

Ab tu market ko apna bhav batata hai: *"mujhe ye cheez IS bhav mein khareedni hai; jab tak wo bhav na aaye, tab tak purchase MAT karna."*

Ghar analogy continue karte hain. Pehle main kamre mein gaya aur bola "jo bhav chal raha hai mere liye khareed do" — ye market order tha. Ab main aake bolta hun: "Main apna bhav batata hun — Pakka 116.16 ka hai, main isko 114 rupaye pe khareedunga, kyunki mujhe 116 mein value nahi dikh rahi."

Yeh 114 rupaye = limit bhav. Instruction market ko:

- Jab bhi koi seller 114 pe bechne ke liye tayyar ho jaye → tab execute karna
- Agar seller 114 pe bechne ko tayyar na ho → execute mat karna

Order book mein kya dikhta hai? Bid chal rahi hai 116.14 pe, 5 bids 116.07 pe dikh rahi hain. Maine limit order 114 rupaye pe laga diya. Wo order OPEN dikhta hai — apna price dikha raha hai 114. Jab bhi 114 rupaye aa jayega, mera order execute ho jayega.

### Type 3 — Stop Loss Order

Scenario: tere paas ek security padi hui hai (jo tune 116.16 pe khareedi thi). Tu nahi chahta ki ye girte-girte zero ho jaye. Toh tu kya karega? Ek predefined price market ko bata dega. Us price pe tera order execute karaya jayega.

> **Stop Loss Order = predefined price batana jis price pe order execute karaya jaye (loss control karne ke liye).**

### Order Matching — Price-Time Priority

Ab core logic — exchange decide kaise karta hai kaunsa order pehle execute hoga? Iska jawab hai **PRICE-TIME PRIORITY**, aur yeh itna core hai ki ChatGPT ya Google pe search bhi karoge toh wahi milega.

**Price priority:** Highest bid ko sabse zyada priority milti hai. Lowest ask ko priority milti hai.

**Time priority:** Agar same price pe do orders hain, toh jiska order PEHLE aaya, uski quantity PEHLE fill hogi. Baad mein aane wale ki baad mein.

Do examples se pakka karte hain:

**Example 1 (time priority):** Maine 9:16 pe 116.16 pe buy bid lagayi. Tune 9:17 pe 116.16 pe bid lagayi. Same price! → Mera pehle execute hoga, tera baad mein — kyunki mera pehle aaya tha.

**Example 2 (price priority):** Maine 9:16 pe 116.16 ka lagaya. Tune 9:16 pe hi 116.40 ka lagaya. Tera price better hai → tera PEHLE execute hoga. Price priority beats time.

Algo banate waqt yeh logic pata hona zaroori hai — kyunki tera bot bhi isi queue mein khada hota hai.

## Part 3 — Price Formation, Spread Aur Liquidity

### LTP Kaise Banta Hai

Jo hume candlestick chart aur line chart mein dikhta hai — wo price formation se banta hai.

Real example: Loopin. Market band ho chuka tha. Loopin ka final price ₹2080.99 tha. Kyun? Kyunki Loopin ki LAST TRADE isi price pe hui thi. Order book khologe toh sell price/best ask alag dikh raha tha (~2500 pe), but kisi ne last traded price pe transaction ki.

- **LTP = Last Traded Price** — jis price pe last transaction hui
- Kisi dusre bande ne transact kiya → LTP turant us price pe aa jayega

Recap: highest buyer = best bid, lowest seller = best ask.

### Bid-Ask Spread

> **Spread = Best Ask − Best Bid** (buyer aur seller ke beech ka difference)

**Example 1 — Pakka Limited (narrow spread):** Buyer khada hai 100 rupaye pe, seller khada hai 100.05 pe. Difference = 5 paise → NARROW SPREAD.

**Example 2 — Eco-Reco (wide spread):** Eco-Reco ek chhoti company hai. Buyer 100 pe, seller 101.50 pe. Bada difference → WIDE SPREAD.

Wide spread kyun banta hai? Kyunki wahan too many sellers NAHI hain. Jab sellers kam hote hain → price discovery achhe se nahi hoti.

Golden rules:

- **Narrow spread = HIGH liquidity**
- **Wide spread = LOW liquidity**

Derivatives note: Bank Nifty weekly options mein 3 PM ke baad spreads widen ho jaate hain — kyunki liquidity kam ho jaati hai.

Key line: **"Spreads are the HIDDEN COST of trading."** Jitna zyada spread, utni zyada cost teri trading mein lagegi. Isliye narrow spread achha hai — liquidity zyada, costing kam.

### Liquidity — 3 Forms

Liquidity ke 3 forms hote hain, ek-ek karke:

**1. Market Depth:** Different price levels pe kitne buy/sell orders waiting hain. Example — order book mein 101 pe, 100 pe, 100.10 pe, 100.20 pe har level pe orders pade hain. 100 pe 40 orders, 100.10 pe 50 orders... alag-alag price points pe pending buy/sell orders = market depth.

**2. Order Flow:** Real time mein market mein actual kitne orders execute ho rahe hain. Kitne market sell, kitne market buy ho rahe hain. Limit vs market order ka differentiation — kitne limit orders sell ho rahe hain, kitne market orders. Order flow ki alag se analysis hoti hai = **TPO (Time Price Opportunity)**. Market profile ki poori information TPO aur order flow se related hai.

**3. Trading Volume:** Us time pe total trading volume market mein kitna hai (subah se abhi tak ka).

### High vs Low Liquidity Ka Effect

High liquidity hone pe enter/exit karne ka impact cost ~0 hota hai. Low liquidity mein kya hota hai? Example dekh — buyers 1000 hain, lekin sellers sirf 20 available hain. Result: bahut saare failed orders, bahut si entries execute nahi hongi.

### Impact Cost — Reality Check

Yeh section sabse important hai, kyunki yeh backtest aur live trading ke beech ka asli gap batata hai.

Ek real story: ek trader strategy run karata tha jiska backtest result ~188%/150% dikha raha tha. Lekin real mein return ~60-70% KAM aata tha. Wo beech ka gap = **IMPACT COST**.

Impact cost kahan lagti hai:

- Enter karte time: 20-30 points lagte hain
- Exit karte time: 10-15 points lagte hain

Quantity ka bhi effect hai: 1 quantity execute karane ki impact cost ≠ 1000 quantity execute karane ki impact cost. Jaise-jaise quantity badhegi, impact cost badhegi.

Company comparison: Pakka Limited ki impact cost kam hogi (liquidity zyada). Eco-Reco ki impact cost zyada hogi — liquidity ki wajah se.

## Part 4 — Transaction Costs Aur Slippage

Transaction cost ke 2 types hote hain:

**Implicit costs** (security khud se generate karti hai): bid-ask spread, slippage, market impact cost.

**Explicit costs** (fixed/bahar se): brokerage, exchange fees, STT, GST, stamp duty.

### Slippage Kya Hoti Hai — Calculation Se Samjho

```
Algo ne order send kiya:      ₹200
Execute hua:                  ₹202
                              ─────
Slippage = 202 − 200 =         2 points

Money terms mein:
2 points × lot size 75 =     ₹1,500  ← yeh teri cost hai!
```

Sirf 2 points ka difference, lekin lot size multiply hone pe ₹1,500 ki cost. Ab samajh aaya slippage itni serious kyun hai?

### Slippage High Kyun Hoti Hai

Teen main reasons:

**1. High volatility:** Volatility high hone pe bid-ask spread WIDE ho jata hai. Spread wide = slippage bahut zyada.

**2. News events:** News event mein kya hota hai — saare log MARKET ORDER place karte hain. Sabko lagta hai "main sabse pehle react karunga." Itne saare market orders se spread wide ho jata hai → slippage bahut aati hai.

**3. Weekly expiry (~2:45 PM):** Weekly expiry ke around 2:45 baje market order use karne se slippage bahut aati hai.

### Slippage Reduce Kaise Karein

**Option 1 — Limit order use karo. BUT ultimate solution nahi!** Kyunki limit order mein apni dikkatein hain: aankh band karke limit order use karoge toh ~20% orders chhoot jayenge. Aur unhi 20% missed orders mein agar bada profit wala trade hua, toh poori metrics kharab ho jayengi.

**Option 2 — Smart execution techniques (asli solution):**

- Smart Order Slicing
- VWAP Execution

Inpe aage code mein kaam karenge — Python ke through tools use karke smart order slicing, taaki orders minimum impact cost mein execute hon aur slippage kam aaye.

## Nutshell

Microstructure = button dabane se order execute hone tak + PnL aane tak ka poora system. Exchange orders collect karke order book banata hai (bids vs asks), phir price-time priority se match karta hai. 3 order types: Market (turant, best available price), Limit (apna bhav, wait), Stop Loss (predefined exit). Narrow spread = high liquidity = kam hidden cost; wide spread = low liquidity. Impact cost backtest aur live ka difference kha jaati hai — 188% backtest live mein 60-70% kam aa sakta hai. Slippage volatility/news/expiry mein badhti hai — solution smart order slicing + VWAP execution.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Microstructure sirf theory nahi.** Yeh directly tere PnL pe affect karta hai — slippage, spread cost, impact cost sab isi se nikalte hain.
- ❌ **Limit order hamesha better nahi hota.** Haan slippage bachati hai, par 15-20% trades miss karwa sakti hai — aur bada profit wala trade miss hua toh poori metrics kharab.
- ❌ **Backtest return = live return nahi.** Backtest mein impact cost aur slippage properly model nahi hoti — isliye live results hamesha thode neeche aate hain.

1. Best bid aur best ask kya hote hain? Buy market order dalne pe kaunsa price milega?

2. Price-time priority kaise kaam karti hai? Ek example de ke samjhao.

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Do rules hain. Price priority: better price wala order pehle execute hota hai — highest bid, lowest ask top pe. Time priority: same price pe jo order pehle aaya uski quantity pehle fill hogi. Example: maine 9:16 pe 116.16 pe bid lagayi, tune 9:17 pe same 116.16 pe — mera pehle execute hoga (time priority). Lekin agar tune 9:16 pe hi 116.40 pe lagaya, toh tera pehle execute hoga (price priority beats time).

</details>

3. Narrow spread aur wide spread ka kya relation hai liquidity se?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Narrow spread = high liquidity. Wide spread = low liquidity. Kyunki jab buyers-sellers zyada hote hain (high liquidity), toh competition se best ask aur best bid paas paas aate hain — narrow spread. Jab sellers kam hote hain, price discovery achhe se nahi hoti — spread wide ho jaata hai. Aur spread hidden cost hai, isliye narrow spread wale stocks mein trading cost kam aati hai.

</details>

<details>

Isliye smart execution techniques (order slicing, VWAP) use karte hain.
