## Strategy Rules

Remember 4.4's EMA-crossover?

This is its UPGRADED version —

one extra FILTER added: **RETEST!**

| | Long | Short |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Bullish crossover (9 ↑ 33) | Bearish cross-under (9 ↓ 33) |
| Condition | **RETEST** comes after | Same mirror |
| Extra filter | Retest-candle **STRONG** (body ≥ 80%) | Same |
| Stop Loss | Previous candle's LOW | Previous candle's HIGH |

Flow in one line:

## Strong Candle Rule — Body ≥ 80%

Formula (candle-anatomy — familiar from 2.x!):

```text
Body   = |Close − Open|
Range  = High − Low
Body % = (Body ÷ Range) × 100
```

Worked example:

```text
High=100, Low=0, Close=80, Open=20

Range = 100 − 0 = 100
Body  = 80 − 20 = 60
Body% = 60 ÷ 100 × 100 = 60%

60% < 80% → ❌ NOT STRONG! (reject)
```

**Logic:** strong-body = decisive-move

(buyers/sellers controlled WHOLE candle).

Long wicks = indecision.

Retest followed by STRONG candle =

reversal CONFIRMED with conviction!

## What is Retest?

> **Retest = after crossover, price TOUCHES/KISSES EMA-zone again**
> — that's entry-trigger!

Code-level definition (must tell AI):

```text
LONG side: retest-candle's HIGH > both EMA values
(meaning candle touched EMA-zone and closed above!)

SHORT: mirror logic
```

⚠️ **OPPOSITE-CROSSOVER RULE (critical!):**

After bullish-crossover, looking for retest —

but if **cross-under** happens in between, signal **FAILS**, skip trade!

Tell AI clearly:

*"find retest AFTER crossover and BEFORE opposite crossover!"*

(This detail missed → AI gives WRONG signals — catches stale retests!)
