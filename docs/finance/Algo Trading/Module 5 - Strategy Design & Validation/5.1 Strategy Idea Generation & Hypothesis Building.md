# 5.1 Strategy Idea Generation & Hypothesis Building

## Guess Work vs Hypothesis-Driven Testing

Do tareeke hain trading karne ke:

| Guess Work | Hypothesis-Driven |
|---|---|
| "Lagta hai ab upar jayega" | "Agar X hota hai, toh historically Y hota tha" |
| Kabhi-kabhi profit | Measurable, repeatable logic |
| Har trade alag reasoning | Same rules har baar |
| Results explain nahi kar pata | Accept/reject data se |

Consistent trading sirf **rules-based** ho sakti hai — aur rules banne se pehle chahiye ek **clear trading idea + testable hypothesis**. Yeh course ki sabse important lines mein se ek hai:

> *"It starts with a clear trading idea and a testable hypothesis."*

Har achhi strategy ke peeche ek hypothesis hoti hai — market **on average** jo behaviour dikhata hai, usko capture karne wale rules.

## Idea Generation Kya Hai?

**Idea generation = market inefficiencies dhundhne ka process.**

Inefficiency matlab — jahan price discrepancy ya price difference hai, wahan opportunity hai. Classic example: **option aur spot ka pricing difference** — kabhi-kabhi option premium spot move ko perfectly reflect nahi karta; yeh gap hi tradeable opportunity ban sakta hai.

Poora flow:

```text
Market inefficiency dhundo
    ↓
Usse capture karne wala idea banao
    ↓
Idea ko hypothesis me convert karo
    ↓
Hypothesis pe rules design karo → Test karo → Deploy karo
```

Ek zaroori filter: achha idea wo hai jo **quantify** ho sake. "Market ajeeb behave kar raha hai" — yeh feel hai, idea nahi. "Jab VIX 10% ek din mein badhta hai..." — yeh numbers mein hai, idea ban sakta hai. Portfolio level pe bhi same rule: jo time ke saath measure ho sake, wahi system ban sakta hai.

## Ideas Ke Paanch Sources

Kahan se ideas aate hain? Paanch major sources:

| # | Source | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Market observation** | Sudden moves dekhna — price moves ke patterns notice karna |
| 2 | **Price action patterns** | Candlestick formations jo baar-baar repeat hote hain |
| 3 | **Technical indicators** | 4.2 mein jo seekha — Super Trend buy signal deta hai; us observation ko idea bana ke test karo |
| 4 | **Events & volatility** | Events (budget, results, Fed) ke around market ka behaviour |
| 5 | **Academic papers** 🔑 | Trend following pe sabse zyada tested research papers milti hain — seriously underrated source |

Do notes:

- Ek source pe mat atako — **multiple sources se ideas lo**, sab ko test karo.
- Sources se mile observations ko **actionable** banana padega — observation ≠ trading idea.

Aur haan — indicators wala source ab tere liye special hai. Pehle log RSI/Bollinger ke naam sunte the, tu unka math jaanta hai. Jab SuperTrend flip hota dikhe, poochh: *"kya flip ke baad N candles mein edge hota hai?"* — boom, hypothesis ready.

## Trading = Art + Science

> **"Trading is art plus science."**

| Part | Kya Hai | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| 🎨 Art | Idea generation | Creative — observations se ideas sochna |
| 🔬 Science | Hypothesis building & testing | Statistical — formulate, measure, accept/reject |

Pehla half creative hai (practice se aata hai), doosra half pure science (seekha ja sakta hai). Commerce background ho ya engineering — dono halves develop ho sakte hain. Science part toh tum already seekh rahe ho; art part market-time ke saath improve hota hai.

## Good Idea Kaise Judge Karein?

Har idea ko chaar sawalon pe judge karo — bas baith ke discussion mat karo, framework se evaluate:

1. **System ban sakta hai?** — Kya is idea ko trading strategy/system mein convert kiya ja sakta hai?
2. **Quantify hota hai?** — Time ke saath numbers mein measure ho sakta hai? Agar nahi, toh system impossible.
3. **Consistent hai?** — Baar-baar kaam karta hai ya sirf kabhi-kabhi?
4. **Actionable hai?** — Isse concrete buy/sell rules ban sakte hain?

Yeh 4-filter pass karne wale ideas hi high-quality trading ideas bante hain.

## Hypothesis Kaisi Hoti Hai — Examples

### Example 1 — Expiry-Day Behaviour

Expiry day pe ek cheez near-certainty ke saath hoti hai: **ATM call aur ATM put ka premium decay behaviour** (ATM = strike jo current price ke sabse paas hai). Us certainty ko pakdo aur uske around system build karo — re-entry rules add karo, stop loss rules add karo. Interesting hypothesis se poora system banta hai.

(Options ke basics 2.2 mein padhe the — premium, strikes. Expiry-day strategies ka detail hum case studies mein karenge.)

### Example 2 — Event-Based Volatility

```text
"Agar VIX ek single day mein 10% badhta hai,
toh agle 3 din intraday volatility higher hoti hai."
```

VIX kya hai? **Volatility Index** — market ka "dar ka meter." Jaise temperature batata hai garmi kitni hai, VIX batata hai market kitna dara hua hai (derived from option prices).

Ab dekho yeh hypothesis kitni solid hai:
- **Specific** — exact condition (VIX +10% in one day)
- **Measurable** — volatility numbers mein naapi ja sakti hai
- **Testable** — historical data leke backtest kar sakte ho; ✅ accept ❌ reject

Compare karo: *"market upar jayega"* — vague, unmeasurable, untestable. Woh hypothesis nahi, andaaza hai.

## Final Workflow — Idea Se Backtest Tak

```text
Observation
    ↓
Convert to Hypothesis
    ↓
Define Signal Rules
    ↓
Backtest          ← Module 4 ka engine!
    ↓
Accept ✅ / Reject ❌
```

Yeh loop hai — reject hua toh dukhi mat ho, naya idea lao, phir se. Reject hona failure nahi hai; **cheap failure** hai. Ek galat idea paper pe reject hua, real money bach gayi.

Progression hamesha yehi rahegi:

```text
idea generate → rules banao → backtest → trade → live trade
```

Chhote level pe test karo, phir scale karo — safe rasta yehi hai. Aur agli classes mein is workflow ke har step ki detail aayegi — time frames, entry/exit rules, optimization.

## Nutshell

Algo trading coding se nahi — **clear idea + testable hypothesis** se start hoti hai. Idea generation = inefficiencies dhundhna; sources: observation, price action, indicators, events, academic papers. Trading = art (ideas) + science (testing). Idea judge karne ke 4 filters: system-ban-sakta?, quantifiable?, consistent?, actionable? Achhi hypothesis specific + measurable + testable hoti hai ("VIX +10% → next 3 days higher vol"), vague statements nahi. Workflow loop: observe → hypothesize → rules → backtest → accept/reject. Guessing band, testing shuru.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Hypothesis = prediction nahi.** "X ke baad Y hota hai" future ka guarantee nahi — sirf historical pattern ka statement hai. Data use reject kar sakta hai; wahi toh value hai.
- ❌ **Zyada ideas ≠ zyada profit.** Bina test ke 50 ideas collect karna collection hai, research nahi. Ek tested-rejected idea untested-50 se zyada valuable hai.
- ❌ **Coding first step nahi hai.** Code tool hai — hypothesis ke baad aata hai. Pehle code likhne wala usually guess-work ko automate kar raha hota hai, strategy nahi.

1. Guess work aur hypothesis-driven trading mein fundamental farq kya hai?

**Jawab:** Guess work feeling-based hai — "lagta hai upar jayega," har trade alag reasoning, results explain nahi hote, kabhi jeet kabji haar. Consistency sirf doosre tareeke se possible hai kyunki wahi rules har baar apply hote hain aur performance measure ho sakti hai.

2. Paanch idea sources kaunse hain? Inme se do apne shabdon mein expand karo.

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** (1) Market observation — sudden moves/patterns dekhna, (2) Price action — candlestick formations, (3) Technical indicators — SuperTrend flips jaise signals ko idea banana, (4) Events & volatility — budget/results/Fed ke around behaviour, (5) Academic papers — trend-following research, sabse underrated source. Expansion examples: indicator source — main khud 4.2 ka math jaanta hun, crossover ke baad ka behaviour test kar sakta hun; academic source — free research papers mein decades-tested concepts milte hain jo retail traders ignore karte hain.

</details>

3. Achhi hypothesis ke 3 qualities? "VIX +10% → next 3 days higher volatility" inko kaise satisfy karti hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Specific + Measurable + Testable. Specific — exact trigger defined hai (VIX +10%, single day), koi ambiguity nahi. Measurable — dono sides numeric hain (% rise, volatility levels), quantify possible. Testable — historical VIX data fetch karke backtest ho sakta hai, clear accept/reject outcome. Contrast: "market darega ab" — koi trigger nahi, koi measure nahi, koi test nahi.

</details>

4. Idea judge karne ke 4 filters kya hain?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** (1) System ban sakta hai? — concrete rules mein convert ho sakta hai kya, (2) Quantify hota hai? — numbers mein track ho sake, warna evaluation impossible, (3) Consistent hai? — baar-baar kaam karta hai ya one-off, (4) Actionable hai? — actual entry/exit decisions generate kar sakta hai. Chaar filters pass kare tabhi backtest phase mein le jao.

</details>

5. Rejection ko "cheap failure" kyun kehte hain?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Kyunki hypothesis workflow mein idea paper pe reject hota hai — backtest data se — real money risk kiye bina. Cost: thoda computing time. Alternative (bina test ke live jaana): potential cost real capital loss hai. Matlab systematic testing ne failure ko cheap bana diya. Zyada cheap failures = zyada jaldi seekhna = eventually better systems. Yehi science half ka fayda hai.

</details>
