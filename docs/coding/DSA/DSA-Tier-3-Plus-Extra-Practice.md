## How this list was chosen
These are well-known interview problems that fill gaps in the 300-sheet: more tree and graph variations, more heaps and design problems, a lot more DP, and a wider greedy set. They come from the kinds of problems that popular lists (Blind 75, NeetCode 150, Top Interview 150) and interview reports repeat. I picked them from memory and checked for overlap with your 300 by script, so treat the selection as strong but not official.

## How to use
1. Don't rush through all of it. Aim to *understand patterns*, not to hit a number. Doing 30-40 of these well is better than ticking 150.
2. Prioritize the patterns you struggled with in the main 300.
3. Same method: try 20-25 minutes, read the solution, re-solve next day.
4. If a link does not open, search the title on LeetCode. Difficulty labels are best-effort.
