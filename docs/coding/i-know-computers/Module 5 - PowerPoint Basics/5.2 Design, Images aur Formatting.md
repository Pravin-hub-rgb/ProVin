## Chapter 5.2 — Design, Images & Formatting

Here's what we'll cover: first, **themes and design** — change the look of your whole presentation instantly. Then **adding images** and shapes. After that, **SmartArt** for professional diagrams. Finally, **alignment tools** to arrange items perfectly.

### Themes and Design

A **theme** is a pre-made set of colors, fonts, and effects that applies to your entire presentation.

**To apply a theme:**
1. Go to **Design** tab
2. Browse the **Themes** gallery (scroll through the options)
3. Click a theme to apply it — all slides change instantly
4. Use **Variants** (next to Themes) for different color versions of the same theme

> **💡 Tip:** Choose one theme and stick with it. Don't mix different design styles in one presentation — it looks unprofessional.

**Background formatting:**
  - Design → **Format Background** (right side panel opens)
  - Choose: Solid color, Gradient, Picture, or Pattern fill
  - To apply to all slides: click "Apply to All" at the bottom

---

### Adding Images

1. Go to **Insert → Pictures**
2. Choose **This Device** (a file from your computer) or **Stock Images** (built-in library)
3. Select the image → click Insert

**Image tips:**
  - **Resize:** Drag a corner handle — this keeps the image proportions correct
  - **Move:** Click and drag the image
  - **Delete:** Select the image and press Delete
  - **Crop:** Select image → Picture Format → Crop → drag the edges

---

### Shapes and Icons

**Insert → Shapes** gives you rectangles, circles, arrows, callouts, and more.

**To use a shape:**
1. Insert → Shapes → click a shape
2. Click and drag on the slide to draw it
3. **To add text:** Right-click the shape → Edit Text → type
4. **To format:** Select the shape → Shape Format tab → change fill color, outline, effects

> **💡 Tip:** Use shapes to highlight important information. A colored rectangle behind key text makes it stand out.

---

### SmartArt

SmartArt turns bullet points into professional diagrams.

**To add SmartArt:**
1. Insert → **SmartArt**
2. Choose a category: List, Process, Cycle, Hierarchy, etc.
3. Click a layout → OK
4. Type your text in the text pane (or click the shapes directly)

**Common office uses:**
  - **Process** — steps in a workflow
  - **Hierarchy** — organization chart
  - **List** — key points with icons
  - **Cycle** — repeating processes

---

### Alignment Tools

When you have multiple items on a slide, alignment matters.

1. Select multiple items (`Ctrl+click` each)
2. Go to **Shape Format → Align** (or Picture Format → Align)
3. Choose: Align Left, Center, Right, Top, Middle, Bottom
4. **Distribute Horizontally/Vertically** — spaces items evenly

> **💡 Tip:** Use the **gridlines** (View → Gridlines) and **guides** (drag out from the rulers) to position items precisely.
