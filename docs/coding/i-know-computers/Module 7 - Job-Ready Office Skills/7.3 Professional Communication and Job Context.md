## Chapter 7.3 — Professional Communication & Job Context

### Professional Email Tone

**Formal email (to bosses, clients, teachers):**

| Part | Example |
|---|---|
| **Opening** | `Dear Mr. Sharma,` / `Dear Sir/Madam,` / `Respected Sir,` |
| **Closing** | `Regards,` / `Warm regards,` / `Sincerely,` / `Best regards,` |

**Avoid in professional emails:**
  - "Hey!", "Sup", "Hii" — too informal
  - ALL CAPS — "PLEASE SEND THIS NOW" — sounds like shouting
  - Multiple exclamation marks — "Thanks!!!" — looks unprofessional
  - Emojis — unless the office culture is very casual

**Subject line - make it clear:**
  - Bad: "please check"
  - Good: "Invoice #1045 — Payment Pending — Action Required"

### Office Etiquette — Computer Related

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Lock your computer** (`Windows+L`) every time you leave your desk | Prevents unauthorized access — even if you step away for a minute |
| **Don't save passwords in the browser** on shared computers | Anyone who uses that computer can access your accounts |
| **Backup important files** weekly | If your computer crashes, you don't lose work |
| **Check company USB policy** before using USB drives | Some offices block USB for security |
| **Minimize personal use** of office computers | Keep social media and personal browsing for breaks |
| **Don't print unnecessarily** | Save paper and company resources |
| **Double-check numbers** during data entry | One wrong number can cause big problems |

### Basic Billing Software Concept

Many offices use dedicated billing software (Tally, Zoho Books, Busy, etc.). The exact software may differ, but the concepts are the same as what you've learned.

**Common features:**
  - **Invoice creation:** Customer name, items, quantity, rate, total (= Qty × Rate)
  - **Payment recording:** Cash, UPI, cheque, card
  - **Reports:** Daily sales, outstanding payments
  - **Inventory:** Track stock in and out

**How it relates to Excel:** Billing software is basically Excel tables with a nicer interface. You enter data, formulas calculate totals, and reports summarize everything. Your Excel skills make learning any billing software much easier.

### Data Entry Best Practices

  - **Double-check numbers** — enter, then read once more before moving to the next field
  - **Be consistent** — dates in the same format, names in the same case, everywhere
  - **Save regularly** — in Excel press `Ctrl+S` every few minutes
  - **Don't rush** — speed is important, but accuracy matters more. A wrong number can take hours to fix
  - **Ask if unsure** — if you don't know what data goes in a field, ask your supervisor. Don't guess
