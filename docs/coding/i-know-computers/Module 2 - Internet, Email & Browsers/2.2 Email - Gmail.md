## Chapter 2.2 — Email (Gmail)

### What is Email

Email (Electronic Mail) is a way to send messages and files over the internet. Unlike a phone call, the person doesn't need to be online at the same time — your message waits in their inbox until they check it.

  - **Email address format:** `username@domain.com` (e.g., `rahul.sharma@gmail.com`)
  - **Free email services:** Gmail (most popular), Outlook, Yahoo Mail

> **💡 Key insight:** In an office, your email is your identity. A professional email address like `rahul.sharma@company.com` looks much better than `coolguy456@gmail.com`.

### Writing a Professional Email

Every professional email has these parts:

<table>
  <tr><th>Field</th><th>What to Put</th><th>Example</th></tr>
  <tr><td><b>To</b></td><td>Recipient's email address</td><td><code>hr@company.com</code></td></tr>
  <tr><td><b>Cc</b></td><td>People who need a copy (Carbon Copy) — everyone can see who's on Cc</td><td><code>manager@company.com</code></td></tr>
  <tr><td><b>Bcc</b></td><td>People who get a copy but others can't see them (Blind Carbon Copy)</td><td>Use when sending to many people who don't know each other</td></tr>
  <tr><td><b>Subject</b></td><td>Clear topic line — tells the reader what the email is about</td><td><code>Invoice #1045 — Payment Confirmation</code></td></tr>
  <tr><td><b>Body</b></td><td>The actual message — keep it short and clear</td><td>See format below</td></tr>
</table>

#### Professional Email Format

```
Subject: [Topic] — [Action Needed / For Review / Request]

Dear [Name / Sir / Ma'am],

[Opening line — why you're writing]

[Main content — short paragraphs, bullet points if needed]

[Closing — next steps or thank you]

Regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Designation]
[Your Phone Number]
```

#### Real Office Examples

**Example 1 — Sending a report to your boss**

```
Subject: October Sales Report — For Your Review

Dear Sir,

Please find the attached October sales report for your review.

The key highlights are:
- Total sales: ₹2,45,000
- Top performer: North region
- 3 new clients onboarded this month

Let me know if you need any changes or additional data.

Regards,
Rahul Sharma
Sales Associate
+91-98765-43210
```

**Example 2 — Email to a client about an invoice**

```
Subject: Invoice #1045 — Payment Due on 15 Nov 2024

Dear Mr. Mehta,

Hope you're doing well.

This is a reminder that Invoice #1045 for ₹12,500 is due on 15th November 2024. The invoice details are attached as a PDF for your reference.

Payment options: Bank transfer, UPI, or cheque.

Please let me know if you have any questions or need the invoice in a different format.

Regards,
Priya Singh
Accounts Department
+91-98765-43211
```

**Example 3 — Internal team update**

```
Subject: Team Meeting — Friday 10 AM — Conference Room B

Hi Team,

This is a reminder about our weekly team meeting this Friday at 10 AM in Conference Room B.

Agenda:
- Project status updates (5 min each)
- Client feedback review (10 min)
- Upcoming deadlines (5 min)

Please come prepared with your progress updates. Let me know if anyone cannot attend.

Regards,
Amit Kumar
Team Lead
```

> **⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid:** No subject line, writing in ALL CAPS, using "Hey" or informal greetings in formal emails, forgetting attachments, and replying-all when not needed.

### Attachments — Sending Files

Most office emails include attachments — PDFs, Word documents, Excel sheets, images.

**Steps to attach a file:**

1. Click **Compose** to start a new email
2. Click the **paperclip icon** (Attach files) at the bottom
3. Select the file from your computer
4. Wait for the upload to finish
5. Click **Send**

> **⚠️ Gmail limit:** Files larger than **25MB** cannot be sent as attachments. Use Google Drive link instead (click the Drive icon next to the paperclip).

### Reply, Reply All, and Forward

Once you receive an email, you have three options:

| Action | What It Does | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Reply** | Send your response only to the original sender | Most common — when only the sender needs your answer |
| **Reply All** | Send your response to the sender + everyone on Cc | Use carefully — only when everyone needs to know your reply |
| **Forward** | Send the received email to someone new | When someone else needs to see this email |

> **💡 Office tip:** In most offices, use **Reply** (not Reply All) unless your response truly matters to everyone on the email. Reply All mistakes are very common among new employees.
