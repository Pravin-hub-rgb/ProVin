In this chapter, we'll work on typing speed and accuracy. In the next chapter, we'll learn professional file organization.

### Why Typing Speed Matters

In an office, you type all day — emails, data entry, reports, documents. Your typing speed directly affects how fast you work.

**Industry standards:**
  - 30-40 WPM (Words Per Minute) — basic office work
  - 50-60+ WPM — data entry specialist
  - Error rate below 1% — accuracy is as important as speed

> **💡 The goal:** 40 WPM with 95%+ accuracy is enough for most office jobs. You don't need to be a super-fast typist — just fast enough to work efficiently.

### Touch Typing Basics

**Touch typing** = typing without looking at the keyboard. Your fingers know where the keys are by muscle memory.

**Home row position:**
  - Left hand: **A S D F** (little to index finger)
  - Right hand: **J K L ;** (index to little finger)
  - **F** and **J** have small bumps — feel them with your index fingers
  - Thumbs rest on the **Space bar**

**How to practice:**
  - Start by placing fingers on the home row
  - Each finger moves up and down to reach its keys
  - Always return to the home row after pressing a key
  - Don't look at the keyboard — force yourself to look at the screen

### Number Row Practice (Top Row)

  The **number row** is the row of keys above the QWERTY letters — `1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 0`. In Excel and data entry work, you type numbers constantly — dates, amounts, quantities, percentages. Learning to type them without looking saves huge time.

  **Finger positions for the number row:**
  - Left hand: **1** (little), **2** (ring), **3** (middle), **4** (index), **5** (index)
  - Right hand: **6** (index), **7** (index), **8** (middle), **9** (ring), **0** (little)

  **Practice tips:**
  - Start slowly — focus on accuracy, not speed
  - Practice common patterns: dates like `15/06/2026`, amounts like `1,250.75`
  - Use typingclub.com lessons — they have dedicated number row exercises
  - 5 minutes of number row practice per day makes a big difference

  > **⚠️ Number row vs Numeric keypad:** The number row (top of keyboard) is for general typing — addresses, sentences with numbers. The numeric keypad (right side) is for fast data entry — long columns of numbers. Learn both.

---

### Numeric Keypad

  For data entry jobs, the numeric keypad (number pad on the right side of full-size keyboards) is very important.

  - **Home row for numbers:** 4, 5, 6 (middle finger on 5 — there's a bump)
  - Index finger: 7, 4, 1
  - Middle finger: 8, 5, 2
  - Ring finger: 9, 6, 3, .
  - Little finger: Enter, +

> **💡 Tip:** If you're on a laptop without a number pad, you can buy a USB numeric keypad for data entry work.
