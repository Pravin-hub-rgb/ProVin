## Strategy Rules

Humari 4.4 wali EMA-crossover yaad hai?

Yeh uska UPGRADED version hai —

ek extra FILTER add hota hai: **RETEST!**

| | Long | Short |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Bullish crossover (9 ↑ 33) | Bearish cross-under (9 ↓ 33) |
| Condition | Uske baad **RETEST** mile | Same mirror |
| Extra filter | Retest-candle **STRONG** ho (body ≥ 80%) | Same |
| Stop Loss | Previous candle ka LOW | Previous candle ka HIGH |
| Target | **1:3** (risk × 3!) | 1:3 |

Flow ek-line-mein:

*crossover hua → price wapas EMA ke paas aaya (retest)
→ strong candle bani → ENTRY!*

## Strong Candle Rule — Body ≥ 80%

Formula (candle-anatomy — 2.x se familiar!):

```text
Body   = |Close − Open|
Range  = High − Low
Body % = (Body ÷ Range) × 100
```

Worked example:

```text
High=100, Low=0, Close=80, Open=20

Range = 100 − 0 = 100
Body  = 80 − 20 = 60
Body% = 60 ÷ 100 × 100 = 60%

60% < 80% → ❌ STRONG NAHI! (reject)
```

**Logic:** strong-body = decisive-move

(buyers/sellers ne POORA candle control kiya).

Lambi wicks = indecision.

Retest ke baad STRONG candle milna =

reversal CONFIRMED with conviction!

## Retest Kya Hai?

> **Retest = crossover ke baad price WAPAS EMA-zone ko touch/kiss kare**
> — wohi entry-trigger hai!

Code-level definition (jo AI ko batani padegi):

```text
LONG side: retest-candle ka HIGH > dono EMA values
(matlab candle EMA-zone TOUCH karke upar hi bani!)

SHORT: mirror logic
```

⚠️ **OPPOSITE-CROSSOVER RULE (critical!):**

Bullish-crossover ke baad retest dhoondhna hai —

par beech-mein agar **cross-under** ho gaya toh signal **FAIL**, trade skip!

AI-ko clearly bolna padega:

*"find retest AFTER crossover and BEFORE opposite crossover!"*

(Yeh detail miss karne pe AI GALAT signals dega — stale retests pakdega!)

```text

   - crossover ke baad, OPPOSITE-crossover se PEHLE retest

   - Visual-signals entries/exits pe dikhe
```
