# 2.4 Position Sizing, Risk & Leverage

Pichli file mein crypto ka dark side dekha tha — 20% crashes, exchange failures, no circuit breakers. Wahan ek line thi jo ab poore weight ke saath samajhne ka time hai: **risk management double strict rakho.** Aur asli baat yeh hai — trading mein sabse important part strategy nahi, **risk management** hai. Achhi strategy + poor risk control = fail. Yeh sunkar ajeeb lagta hai, par is file ke end tak clear ho jayega kyun.

Jab tak risk define nahi hota, trading mein success nahi milta. Isliye aaj teen sawaal ke jawab seekhenge: kitna trade karu, kitna risk lu, aur kitna leverage use karu.

## Position Sizing Ke 3 Pillars

Position sizing matlab — har trade mein kitna paisa lagana hai. Iske 3 pillars hote hain:

**Pillar 1 — Kitna trade karu?** Number of trades kitne honi chahiye — din ke hisaab se, mahine ke hisaab se. Zyada trades ≠ zyada profit.

**Pillar 2 — Kitna risk lu?** Apne capital ka kitna % risk lena hai bina mental damage ke. Yeh pillar sabse important hai.

**Pillar 3 — Kitna leverage lu?** Surprise wali baat — yeh decide karne ki zaroorat hi nahi padti. Jab Pillar 2 (risk) define ho jaata hai, leverage automatically nikal aaata hai. Pehle risk, phir leverage — ulta nahi.

Ek concept yahan pakad lo — **mental drawdown.** Account ki drawdown se zyada dangerous emotional drawdown hai. Agar loss ne tujhe mentally tod diya, toh agla trade revenge mode mein hoga — aur wahan se series of losses shuru hoti hai. Isliye risk itna rakh ki mental drawdown na aaye. Aur haan — capital chhota hai toh thoda zyada % risk le sakta hai; professional trader isliye professional hai kyunki wo kam % risk leta hai.

## Trading Risk Ke 6 Types

Risk sirf ek nahi, 6 types ka hota hai. Har ek ko samajh:

**1. Market Risk:** Unfavorable price moves se loss. Example — Nifty 10% circuit lag gaya aur teri overnight position andar fas gayi. Gap-up/gap-down isi category ka darr hai.

**2. Leverage Risk:** Sabse zyada log yahan marte hain. Soch — tune 0.5 lot lagaya aur laga "bas ₹50,000 ka exposure hai." Galat! Nifty future ka contract value ~₹18 lakh hai. 10% ka gap move = ₹1.8 lakh ka loss — teri value ka 80-90% wipeout. Leverage chhoti si position ko nuclear bomb banata hai.

**3. Liquidity Risk:** Illiquid option strikes ya small-cap stocks mein ghus gaya, ab exit nahi mil raha. Panic mein nahi nikal paoge bina impact cost khaye (2.1 yaad kar).

**4. Systematic Risk:** RBI policy announcement, elections, US market crash. Yeh pura system ek saath hit karta hai — **diversify bhi nahi hota.**

**5. Strategy Risk:** Market condition badal gayi ya strategy overfit thi — strategy drawdown phase mein chali jaati hai.

**6. Execution Risk:** Slippage, API delay, order fill nahi hua. Good news — yeh minimize kiya ja sakta hai (smart execution techniques, 2.1 mein dekhi thi).

Yeh sab interconnected hain — market risk (circuit lag gaya) → leverage risk (overnight position wipeout). Aur inka common solution ek hi hai: **diversification.** Instrument diversify kar, din diversify kar, strategy diversify kar.

## Risk Ke Levels — Kahan Kahan Limit Rakho

Ab practical rules. Risk ko 4 levels pe limit karni hoti hai:

### Level 1 — Per Trade

| Trader Type | Risk Per Trade |
|---|---|
| Retail Trader | 0.5% - 1% |
| Professional Trader | 0.25% - 0.75% |

Chhota capital hai toh thoda upar wala side le sakta hai. Winning probability badhani hai toh risk per trade kam karo — simple math hai.

### Level 2 — Per Day

Yahan wo trap hai jo sabko maarti hai. Maan le 5 strategies chala raha hai, har ek 1% risk le rahi hai. Ek din mein sabke stop loss lag gaye → **5% gone ek din mein.** 10 aise din = pura capital!

Isliye **maximum risk per day define karo — 3-5% se upar kabhi nahi.**

Aur ek personal lesson jo free mein le lo: 5 strategies chal rahi thin, sab alag instruments pe (Nifty, BankNifty, FinNifty, Sensex). Laga sab diversified hai. Phir ek phase aaya jahan **sab strategies ka drawdown ek saath aa gaya** — combined 100%+ drawdown hota, bach gaye thode se. Alag instruments bhi correlated ho sakte hain — yeh paise dekar seekha hota toh bahut mehnga padta.

### Level 3 — Per Strategy

Kisi ek strategy mein **25% se zyada capital mat daalo.** Ideal range 5-10% per strategy hai. Zyada concentration = jab woh strategy drawdown mein jaayegi, mental drawdown bhi gehra hoga.

### Level 4 — Per Instrument

Sirf BankNifty pe sab kuch mat jodo. Spread rakho — Nifty + BankNifty + FinNifty. Diversification har problem ka partial solution hai, har level pe lagao.

## Drawdown Math — Jo Dar Lani Chahiye

Yeh table dekh aur dhyan se. Loss hone ke baad recover karne ke liye kitna gain chahiye:

| Loss | Recovery Chahiye |
|---|---|
| 20% | 25% gain |
| 50% | **100% gain** |
| 70% | **233% gain** |

Pattern dikh? Har girne ke baad uthne ki speed **exponentially** mushkil hoti jaati hai. 50% loss ke baad double karna hai apna paisa — bas wapas aane ke liye. Isliye chhota loss lena sasta hai, bada loss lena mehnga.

Aur ek kadwi sachchai — trading mein **certainty naam ki cheez nahi hai.** Tujhe bachata kya hai? Sirf discipline aur diversification.

## Risk System Ko Code Mein Karna

Algo trader ka fayda yahi hai — yeh saare rules code enforce kar sakta hai. Manual trader kabhi nahi kar paya. Tere bot mein yeh elements hone chahiye:

- **Maximum loss limit:** Din/strategy ki max loss boundary — cross hua toh trading band
- **Maximum trade count:** Din mein itni hi trades, usse zyada nahi
- **Maximum position sizing:** Lot size cap
- **Emergency stop loss:** Sab kuch band karne ka ek master switch
- **Volatility filter:** High volatility mein entries rok do
- **Time-based shutoff:** Din ke hisaab se auto-stop
- **Limit order usage:** Slippage kam karne ke liye
- **Slippage control:** Smart slicing — order ko chhote tukdon mein todo, sahi price pe execute karo

Python se yeh sab easily ho jaata hai. Code ko emotions nahi hoti — woh rule todta nahi chahe tu chahe.

## Nutshell

Strategy se bada risk management hai. Position sizing ke 3 pillars: kitna trade, kitna risk, kitna leverage (jo risk se automatically niklega). 6 risk types — market, leverage, liquidity, systematic, strategy, execution. Limits yaad rakh: per trade 0.5-1%, per day max 3-5%, per strategy max 25%, har level pe diversify. Drawdown math dara ke rakhega — 50% loss ke liye 100% gain chahiye. Aur algo trader ka superpower: poora risk system Python se code ho jaata hai.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Risk management = profit kam karne wali cheez nahi.** Ulta hai — yeh tujhe game mein dher tak khelne deti hai. Jo lamba khelta hai, wahi seekhta hai aur jeetta hai.
- ❌ **Leverage = free money nahi.** 18 lakh ka contract 1.2 lakh margin pe milta hai, par loss bhi full contract value ka hota hai. Leverage risk ke pillar ko halka mat lo.
- ❌ **Diversification = systematic risk ka ilaaj nahi.** Elections/crash jaisa systematic risk sab kuch ek saath hit karta hai — diversification baaki 5 risks manage karti hai, yeh nahi.
