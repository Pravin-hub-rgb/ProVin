# 2.3 Crypto Market Structure — with Bitcoin Example

Ab tak hum Indian stock market ki duniya mein the — NSE, SEBI, circuit breakers, 9:15 se 3:30 ka market. Ab ek alag duniya dekhte hain jo algo traders ke liye golden opportunity hai — **crypto**. Bitcoin sabne suna hoga, lekin trader ke nazariye se uska market kaise structure hota hai, stock market se kaise alag hai, aur algo trading ke liye ismein kya khaas hai — yeh is file mein samjhenge.

## Crypto vs Indian Stock Market — Core Differences

Pichli files ke concepts ko yaad karke compare karte hain. Wahi concepts, naye rules:

**1. Market kabhi band nahi hota.** NSE subah 9:15 pe khulta hai, 3:30 pe band. Crypto? **24×7×365.** Saturday raat 2 baje bhi BTC trade ho raha hoga. Iska direct matlab algo trading ke liye — tera bot kabhi "market close" ka wait nahi karta, aur monitoring utna hi important hai kyunki gap-up/gap-down jaisa concept hi nahi hai — market har second move kar sakta hai.

**2. Exchange = Broker (dono ek saath).** Yaad kar 1.4 mein ek exception padhi thi — *"Binance exchange BHI hai aur broker BHI."* Ab woh clear hogi. Stock market mein tu Zerodha use karta hai jo NSE se baat karta hai. Crypto mein **exchange hi tere liye sab kuch hai** — Binance, Coinbase, Delta Exchange (India). Yeh khud matching engine chalate hain, khud tujhe API dete hain. Middle layer (broker) ka concept crypto mein effectively exchange ke andar merge ho gaya hai.

**3. Regulation alag hai.** India mein SEBI sab regulate karti hai — algo classification, margins, circulars (1.5 yaad hai?). Crypto mein koi central regulator nahi hai jaisa SEBI karta hai. Global guidelines follow hote hain, par har exchange apne rules banata hai. Fayda: kam restrictions, jaldi start. Nuksaan: protection bhi kam — isi liye risk management (agli file ka topic) aur zyada critical hai.

**4. Circuit breakers nahi hote.** Nifty pe 10% circuit lagta hai toh trading ruk jaati hai. Crypto mein aisa kuch nahi. Bitcoin ek din mein 10-20% gir sakta hai aur market band nahi hoga. Tera stop loss hi teri asli suraksha hai.

## Bitcoin Example Se Samjho

Bitcoin (BTC) se hi saari cheezein practically dekhte hain.

### Volatility

Nifty din mein average ~1% move karta hai. Bitcoin? **5-10% normal din hai.** Extreme dinon pe 20%+ bhi dekha gaya hai. Example ke liye soch — BTC ₹50 lakh se ₹45 lakh ho gaya ek raat mein. Stock market mein yeh crash kehlata, crypto mein Tuesday tha.

Iska matlab algo trader ke liye: position sizing aur stop loss distance dono volatility-adjust hone chahiye. Stock wale parameters copy mat kar.

### Trading Pairs Aur USDT

Crypto mein shares directly rupees mein nahi khareedte. Har coin ka ek **base pair** hota hai — sabse common **USDT** (ek stablecoin jo $1 ke barabar rehti hai).

- `BTC/USDT` = kitne USDT mein 1 Bitcoin milega
- `ETH/USDT`, `SOL/BTC` — aise pairs

Toh jab log bolte hain "BTC $60k", wo actually BTC/USDT ka price hai. Algo likhte waqt tujhe symbol ka yeh format samajhna zaroori hai.

### Order Book — Wahi Microstructure

2.1 mein jo seekha — bids, asks, best bid/best ask, spread — **sab crypto mein bhi same chalta hai.** Order book structure identical hai. Farak sirf itna:

- Liquidity exchange pe depend karti hai — Binance pe BTC ka spread bahut tight hota hai (bahut saare buyers-sellers), lekin chhote coins pe wide
- 24/7 hone ki wajah se raat ke time liquidity gir jaati hai — spread widen hota hai, bilkul waise jaise Bank Nifty options mein 3 PM ke baad hota hai (2.1 yaad kar)

### Perpetual Futures — Crypto Ka Khaas Derivative

2.2 mein futures padhe the — fixed expiry, M2M settlement. Crypto mein inka apna version hai: **Perpetual Futures (Perps).**

- Futures jaisa hi contract, lekin **expiry date nahi hoti** — jab chahe hold karo
- Leverage milta hai — kabhi-kabhi 100x tak (stock futures mein max ~9x tha!)
- Position ko open rakhne ke liye **funding rate** lagta hai — longs aur shorts ke beech ka balance mechanism

Warning yahan clear rakh — 100x leverage matlab 1% ke against move pe poora account gone. Beginners ke liye perps high-risk territory hai.

## Algo Learning Ke Liye Crypto Kyun Best Hai

Yeh course crypto pe focus isliye bhi karta hai:

- **Data fetch karna sabse easy hai.** Delta Exchange jaise platforms se BTC/ETH ka historical data bina API key ke, bas `requests` library se mil jaata hai. Compare kar — Zerodha Kite Connect setup kitna heavy tha.
- **Koi paperwork nahi.** Account banana minutes leta hai, SEBI-type compliance nahi.
- **24/7 practice.** Bot banaya, weekend pe bhi live test kar sakta hai.
- **Strategies transfer hoti hain.** Crypto pe seekha logic — indicators, backtesting, execution — Indian market mein bhi wahi apply hota hai. Bas volatility parameters adjust karne padte hain.

## Risks Jo Stock Market Mein Nahi Hain

Fair picture ke liye nuksaan bhi samajh lo:

- **Exchange risk:** Exchange hi hack ho sakta hai ya collapse (FTX example — billion-dollar exchange ek raat mein gaya). Tera paisa exchange pe hai, kisi regulated clearing corporation ke paas nahi.
- **Extreme volatility:** Stop loss slippage ki wajah se bhi miss ho sakta hai — price tere SL se neeche seedha gap maar sakta hai, aur market band hone wala koi mechanism nahi hai.
- **Regulatory uncertainty:** India mein crypto pe tax rules aur regulations abhi evolve ho rahe hain.

Isliye golden rule: **crypto mein risk management stock market se double strict rakho.** Aur yehi perfect segue hai — agla topic wahi hai.

## Nutshell

Crypto market structure: 24×7 market (no close, no circuit breakers), exchange hi broker hai (Binance/Delta), no SEBI-style regulation. BTC example: 5-10% daily moves normal, USDT pairing system, order book/microstructure wahi 2.1 wala, perps = expiry-free futures with extreme leverage. Algo learning ke liye best playground — free data, easy APIs, instant practice. Par risk double — exchange risk, volatility risk, regulatory risk. Isliye agla topic: position sizing aur risk management.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Crypto = guaranteed profit machine nahi.** Haan opportunities zyada hain, par losses bhi utne hi fast hote hain. Volatility dono taraf kaam karti hai.
- ❌ **Sab exchanges same nahi hote.** Binance aur koi naya chhota exchange — liquidity, security, API quality sab alag. Established exchange chuno.
- ❌ **"No regulation = no rules" nahi.** Tax obligations phir bhi hain (India mein crypto pe tax lagta hai), aur exchange ki apni KYC/rules hote hain.
