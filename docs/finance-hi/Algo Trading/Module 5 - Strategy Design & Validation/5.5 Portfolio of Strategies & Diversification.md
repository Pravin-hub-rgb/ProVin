# 5.5 Portfolio of Strategies & Diversification

## Bank Wala Analogy

Bank soch: ₹10,000 crore loan dene hain. Kya poora paisa ek hi insaan ko dega? Kabhi nahi — woh default hua toh sab gaya. Bank hazaron logon mein baant deta hai.

Ab teri baari: kya apna poora trading capital **ek hi strategy** ko de rahe ho? Woh strategy hi "ek insaan" hai jo default kar sakti hai.

Aur default kyun hogi? Kyunki market hamesha kisi na kisi **regime** mein hota hai:

```text
Trending / Sideways / High-Volatility / Low-Volatility
```

Aur yahi problem hai:

```text
❌ Trend strategy    → sideways mein fail
❌ Mean reversion    → trending mein fail
```

Different strategies alag-alag regimes mein kaam karti hain. Ek ke bharose baithega toh regime badalte hi tu spectator ban jayega.

## Single Strategy Fail Kyun Hoti Hai

Teen reasons:

**1. Regime change.** Upar wala table hi hai — koi bhi single approach har mausam ke liye nahi.

**2. Drawdown unbearable ho jaata hai.** Yahan ek concept hai jo zyada tar log miss kar dete hain — drawdown ke **do aspects**:

| Aspect | Matlab |
|---|---|
| **Total drawdown** | Paisa kitna gira (e.g., −15%) |
| **Time in drawdown** | Recovery mein kitna time laga (e.g., **8 mahine**) |

15% drawdown ke saath 90% return sunne mein zabardast lagta hai. Par agar woh 15% dip **8 mahine tak** chhapa rahe? Practical sawaal: *"kya main 8 mahine tak apne system ko drawdown mein dekh paunga — bina use question kiye, bina band kiye?"* 10–15 losing streaks aa sakti hain, teen mahine ka loss phase aa sakta hai. Akeli strategy pe yeh psychological load brutal hota hai.

**3. Over-reliance = career risk.** Tumhari poori livelihood ek hi logic pe tiki hai (maan lo "support zone 3 touch → breakout"). Logic ne kaam karna band kar diya? Sab khatam — sirf loss nahi, confidence bhi.

## Portfolio of Strategies

> *"Portfolio of strategies = trading systems ka set jo independently operate karte hain aur return/risk mein alag-alag contribute karte hain."*

Yeh bilkul stocks-portfolio jaisa hai — bas companies diversify karne ki jagah **trading systems** diversify karte hain.

Index/mutual fund analogy perfect fit hai: Nifty 50 = 50 companies. Ek doob gaya, 49 chal rahe. Law of large numbers ki wajah se consistent return milta hai aur total-failure ka chance almost zero. Passive index investing isi philosophy pe khadi hai.

Robust portfolio mein mix hona chahiye:

```text
Trend following + Mean reversion + Volatility strategy
+ Intraday + Positional + Different instruments
```

Kyun? *No single strategy works in all market conditions.* Point full stop.

## Diversification & Correlation

> **Diversification = capital ko different UNCORRELATED strategies mein baantna, taaki ek ka failure capital ko destroy na kare.**

Diversification ke dimensions:

| Dimension | Example |
|---|---|
| Across strategies | Trend + mean-reversion + volatility |
| Across timeframes | Daily positional + intraday |
| Across regimes | Trending-friendly + sideways-friendly |
| Across instruments | Equity, commodity, crypto, fixed income |
| Across logic types | Different hypotheses |

### Correlation — Is Topic Ka Hero

2.5 (Statistical Thinking) mein correlation seekha tha — ab woh trading ka sabse important shabd ban jaata hai. Do strategies/equities ke returns ka relationship:

| Correlation | Behaviour | Diversification Value |
|---|---|---|
| **+1** | Identical — ek upar, doosra bhi upar | ❌ Zero — dono saath haarenge |
| **0** | No relationship | ✅ OK |
| **−1** | Opposite — ek upar toh doosra neeche | 🔥 Best |

Real examples:
- **Gold vs Equity:** traditionally negative-ish — equity girta hai toh gold upar
- **Fixed income vs Equity:** Corona crash mein equity −40%, bonds upar — negative correlation saved balanced portfolios

Goal: **lower/negative correlation wali strategies combine karna.** Negative rare hai par best hai.

⚠️ Trap alert: 5 strategies combine kar li, par agar **sab ek hi time pe loss de rahi hain** — portfolio diversified NAHI hai. Sirf count nahi, correlation check karo.

## Strategy Menu — Mix Ke Liye

| Type | Best Regime |
|---|---|
| Trend following | Trending |
| Mean reversion | Sideways |
| Option selling (straddle/strangle) | Low volatility |
| Breakout / long straddle | High volatility |
| Intraday (ORB/VWAP) | Daily regularity |
| Event driven | Budget day, expiry, Fed |

Dekha pattern? **Opposite behaviours** — trend (trending mein jeete) + reversal (sideways mein jeete) + straddle (low-vol mein jeete). Jab ek haaregi, doosri jeetegi. Yehi best diversification hai.

## Practical Example — Do Strategies Ka Sangam

Do strategies lete hain, equal weights (50/50 — risk-normalized): ₹10L capital → ₹5L each.

### Strategy 1 — Trend Following (Nifty futures, daily, positional)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Capital journey (2 yr) | ₹10L → ₹14.9L |
| Total return / CAGR | 49% / **22.3%** |
| Sharpe | 0.95 |
| Max drawdown | **18.7%** |
| Trades (W/L) | 78 (30W/48L) — win rate **38.5%** |
| Avg win : avg loss | 4.6 : 1.3 (**3.5:1 ratio**) |

Sound familiar? Yehi profile hai jo 5.3 ke SuperTrend case study mein dikha tha — **kam jeet, badi jeet** (3.5:1 payoff). Trend following wins BIG, not often.

### Strategy 2 — Options Selling Mean Reversion (Nifty, intraday, range-bound)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Capital journey (2 yr) | ₹10L → ₹16.18L |
| Total return / CAGR | ~68% / **30.2%** |
| Max drawdown | **27.5%** |
| Trades (W/L) | 460 (312W/148L) — win rate **67%** |
| Expectancy | 0.18 per trade |

Opposite personality: **zyada jeet, chhoti jeet** — high win rate but small wins; ek bada loss poora mahina kha jaata hai.

### Combined Portfolio (50/50)

| Metric | Mean Reversion | Trend Following | **Combined** |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAGR | 30% | 22.3% | **26%** |
| Max Drawdown | 27.5% | 18.7% | **12.3%** ✅ |
| Worst month | −13.8% | −9.5% | **−5.8%** ✅ |
| Return per unit risk | ~1.09 | — | **~2.1** ✅ |

Magic dekho:

- CAGR thoda gira (best single ki 30% → 26%) — chhota cost
- **Drawdown AADHA ho gaya** (27.5% → 12.3%) — bada reward
- Worst month bhi aadha (−13.8% → −5.8%)
- Risk-adjusted return (Sharpe ~1.95) dono singles se behtar

### Equity Curve Ka Pattern

Yeh sabse beautiful insight hai:

```text
Mean reversion:  smooth chalti rahti + kabhi sudden clip khaati hai
Trend following: flat rehti hai + kabhi sudden jump deti hai
Portfolio:       SMOOTH STAIRCASE — dono ke gaps ek doosre ko bhar dete hain
```

Jab mean reversion apna bad month kha rahi thi, trend following jump de rahi thi — aur vice versa. Isliye combined curve almost dip-free hai. Time-in-drawdown wala psychological torture (8-mahine wala sawaal) portfolio mein practically disappear ho jaata hai.

Key lesson: individual strategy ka drawdown usi pe depend karta hai; **portfolio use smooth kar deta hai.**

## Nutshell

Single strategy regime change se marti hai, unbearable time-in-drawdown deti hai, aur career risk ban jaati hai. Portfolio = trading systems ka index — law of large numbers teri taraf. Diversification dimensions: strategies, timeframes, regimes, instruments, logics. Correlation hero hai: +1 useless, 0 ok, −1 best; count nahi, correlation check karo. Proof: Trend (38.5% WR, big wins) + Mean Reversion (67% WR, small wins) → combined CAGR 26%, DD sirf 12.3%, smooth staircase equity. Thoda return sacrifice, massive stability gain — yehi professional trading hai.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Zyada strategies = diversified portfolio nahi.** Paanch trend-following systems = ek hi bet paanch baar (+1 correlation). Diversification uncorrelated LOGIC se aata hai, count se nahi.
- ❌ **Portfolio = guaranteed profit machine nahi.** Correlation regimes ke saath shift hota hai — crisis mein "uncorrelated" assets bhi saath gir sakte hain. Portfolio risk kam karta hai, eliminate nahi karta.
- ❌ **Combined CAGR lower = worse nahi.** 26% vs 30% comparison unfair hai jab drawdown 27.5% vs 12.3% hai. Risk-adjusted lens (return per unit risk ~2.1x) asli picture dikhata hai — aur woh portfolio ke favour mein hai.

1. Drawdown ke do aspects kaunse hain aur time-in-drawdown itna important kyun?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Aspects: total drawdown (paisa kitna gira, e.g., −15%) aur time in drawdown (recover hone mein kitne mahine, e.g., 8). </details>

2. Correlation +1/0/−1 ka matlab aur diversification value?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** +1 = identical movement (dono upar/dono neeche) — diversification zero, dono saath haarenge. 0 = no relationship — decent. −1 = opposite movement — best, ek ka loss doosra cover karta hai. Real examples: gold-equity traditionally negative-ish; corona mein fixed income upar jab equity −40%. Goal: low/negative correlation combine karna. Trap: 5 strategies le liya par sab same regime pe loss de rahi — count ≠ diversification.

</details>

3. Combined example mein CAGR gira (30→26%) par result phir bhi better kyun?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Kyunki drawdown aadha ho gaya (27.5→12.3%) aur worst month bhi (−13.8→−5.8%). Risk-adjusted return dekho: return-per-unit-risk ~1.09 → ~2.1 — DOUBLE. Plus recovery math: 12.3% DD recover karna easy, 27.5% painful (aur uska time-in-drawdown alag hi khel tha). Compounding smooth staircase se long-term mein actually faster hota hai kyunki deep dips compounding tod dete hain. Stability > raw return.

</details>

4. "Smooth staircase" equity curve kaise banta hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Opposite-personality strategies overlap se: mean reversion smooth chalti hai par occasionally clip khaati hai; trend following flat/sideways rehti hai par suddenly jumps deti hai. Combine karo — jab MR apna bad phase mein hai, TF apne jump-phase mein hota hai (different regimes!). Ek ke gaps doosra bhar deta hai. Net curve: lagatar chhote steps upar — staircase. Isliye psychologically live karna bhi easy: har hafte kuch positive chal raha hota hai.

</details>

5. Bank analogy trading pe exactly kaise map hota hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Bank poora capital ek borrower ko nahi deta — default = total loss; hazaron borrowers mein spread karta hai (law of large numbers). Trader: poora capital ek strategy mein = strategy ka regime-failure = account-level disaster. Solution same: capital multiple UNCORRELATED strategies mein baanto (trend + mean-rev + vol-selling + intraday...). Ek default kare, baaki carry karte hain. Difference bas yeh: bank borrowers ko diversify karta hai, tu market REGIMES ko diversify karta hai.

</details>
