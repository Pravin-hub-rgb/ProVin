4.4 ki strategy yaad hai? Humne jaan-boojh ke bina target aur bina stop loss chhodi thi — "advanced features aage aayengi" bola tha. Woh aage aa gaya. Ab strategy ke teen core organs design karenge: **kab enter, kab exit, kitna risk**. Aur pehli hi line mein ek baat jo har naye trader ka worldview badal deti hai:

> *"Entry, exit, aur risk rules hi har profitable algo system ki core foundation hain — profitability INDICATORS se nahi, RULES se decide hoti hai."*

# 5.3 Entry, Exit & Risk Rules

## Rules > Indicators

Common misconception: "achha indicator mil gaya toh paisa banega." Galat. Achhi idea + strong indicators + **galat entry/exit rules** = failed system. Kyunki ultimately decide kya karta hai — *kab enter kiya, kab nikle, kitna khoya, kitna kamaya* — yeh sab rules hain, indicators sirf input hain.

Isliye indicator selection se zyada important hai **rule design**. Chalo dono sides dekhte hain.

## Entry Rules

> **Entry rule = trade mein kaise aur kab ghusna hai.**

Achhe entry rule ki 4 qualities:

| Quality | Matlab |
|---|---|
| **Objective / clear** | Zero guessing — do log same rule padhke same trade len |
| **Repeatable across ALL data** | Sirf pichle 6 mahine pe chale → galat. 2 saal ke data pe bhi chalna chahiye |
| **Codable & backtestable** | Python mein likha ja sake, test ho sake |
| **Logical** | Kisi market idea pe based ho (trend/reversion/volatility) — sirf curve-fit nahi |

### Entry Rules Ki 5 Families

| Type | Examples | Idea Kya Hai |
|---|---|---|
| **Trend Following** | Range breakout, MA breakout, Higher Highs/Lows, RSI strength | Trend chal raha hai, uske saath jao |
| **Mean Reversion** | RSI < 30 buy, Bollinger lower band touch, gap fill, red candle + reversal candle | Zyada door gaya, ab wapas average pe aayega |
| **Volatility-Based** | ATR explosion, volatility contraction → breakout | Sannata tute ga, phir move aayega |
| **Time-Based** | ORB (first 15-min range breakout), VWAP bounce, closing-basis trades | Din ke specific time windows ka behaviour |
| **Indicator/Pattern** | EMA crossover (4.4!), MACD crossover | Classic signal combos |

Ek concrete example samajh lo: **RSI < 25 → BUY.** Yeh **mean reversion** family hai kyunki market neeche ja raha hai aur tum keh rahe ho "yahin se reverse hoga" — market ki counter-direction mein jaana. Wahi rule agar hota "RSI 60+ cross → BUY" toh trend-following hota. Same indicator, opposite philosophy — isliye *rule family* matter karti hai, indicator nahi.

## Exit Rules — The Forgotten 70%

⚠️ Sabse bada psychological trap: **traders entry pe obsess karte hain, exit ignore karte hain.** Reality: **exit determines ~70% of performance.** Entry kitni bhi achhi ho, galat exit profit ko loss mein badal dega.

Exit ka kaam: **profit secure karna, losses ghatana, drawdown control karna.**

### Exit Ke 4 Types

| Type | Kya Karta Hai |
|---|---|
| **Profit-target exit** | Fixed profit level pe book karo |
| **Stop-loss exit** | Fixed loss level pe kat jaao |
| **Indicator-based exit** | EMA cross reverse / MACD reverse → exit (4.4 wali strategy ka exit yehi tha!) |
| **Trailing stop-loss** | SL price ke saath upar slide karta rehta hai — profit lock |

### Trailing Stop-Loss Ek Minute Mein

Buy @ 100, initial SL @ 95. Price 110 pe pahunchi → SL 105 pe shift karo. Price 120 → SL 115. Ab chahe price kahin bhi gira doon, tumhe **kam-se-kam +15 lock hai**. Uptrend chalta rahega toh profit bhi chalta rahega. Simple concept, powerful result.

### Full Ya Partial Exit?

- **Full exit:** poora position ek saath band
- **Partial:** half book karo, baaki trail karo — winner ko daudne do, kuch profit pocket mein bhi aa jaye

Kaunsa better hai? Yeh opinion se nahi — **data analysis se** decide hota hai. Backtest dono versions chalao.

🔑 **Golden design principle:** *Best exit rules SIMPLE hote hain.* Rule jitna complicated, profit banane ki probability utni kam (inversely proportionate). Complexity flexibility nahi, fragility laata hai.

## Case Study — SuperTrend Backtest

Chalo sab kuch practical karte hain. SuperTrend (4.2 mein padha — ATR-based flipping trend line) pe yeh system:

```text
Rule (sirf 2 lines):
Buy jab SuperTrend turns GREEN
Sell/Exit jab SuperTrend turns RED
```

Nifty pe daily chart se shuru kiya — trades bahut kam aati, toh **1-hour** pe switch kiya. Backtest results:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total trades | **183** |
| Winning trades | 47 |
| Losing trades | 136 |
| Win accuracy | **25.68%** 😱 |
| Net P&L | **+5,917 points** ✅ |

Ruk ke dekho. **75% trades HAARI!** Aur phir bhi net result solidly positive. Kaise?

Yeh **typical trend-following profile** hai: bahut saare chhote losses (SL tight), par jab trend pakda toh **bade wins** — momentum poori ride mili. 136 losses × chhota amount < 47 wins × bada amount. 4.6 wala asymmetry lesson ulte direction mein: win rate low ho sakta hai jab payoff ratio high ho.

Aur hypothesis clear hai: alag-alag regions/investors apne-apne levels pe buy/sell karte hain → moves mein **momentum** banti hai. Trend following 100+ saalon se surviving strategy hai — fadé nahi hui.

### Rule Design Se Kya Seekha

- **Repeatable** — same rule, poora data
- **Clear** — *"5 saal ke bachche ko samjha do toh samajh aaye"* itna simple
- **No jargon, full logic** — peeche hypothesis visible hai (momentum)

## Risk Rules Kahan Hain?

Notice kiya? Alag se koi "risk section" nahi banaya. Kyunki risk management yeh sab **already hai**:

```text
Risk control =
    controlled stop-losses        (exit rule)
  + trailing exits                (exit rule)
  + position sizing               (Module 2)
```

Jab exits disciplined hain aur sizing circuit/gap-aware hai (5.2), risk automatically managed hai. Alag se exotic "risk formula" invent karne ki zaroorat nahi — boring fundamentals hi kaam karte hain.

## Nutshell

Rules foundation hain — indicators nahi. Entry rule qualities: objective, repeatable across all data, codable, logical. Paanch families: trend, mean-reversion, volatility, time-based, pattern. Exit ≈ 70% of performance — 4 types: target, SL, indicator-based, trailing (full vs partial = data decides). Simple exits > complex exits. SuperTrend proof-of-concept: 25.68% win rate, phir bhi +5,917 points — trend following = many small losses, few big wins. Risk = SL + trailing + sizing combined. Clear, repeatable, logical — bas yehi chahiye.

## What It Is NOT

- ❌ **Entry rule = strategy nahi.** "RSI < 30 buy" akela adhoora hai — exit, sizing, timeframe sab jodkar strategy banti hai. Log entry share karke "strategy bata di" sochte hain.
- ❌ **Low win rate = bad system nahi.** 25.68% wala system profitable tha. Judge hone wala number expectancy hai (4.6), win rate akela nahi.
- ❌ **Complicated rules ≠ professional rules.** Institutional-grade systems famously boring/simple hote hain. Complexity backtest-fit karne ka symptom hota hai, quality ka nahi (5.4 mein isi ka poora naam aayega: overfitting).
