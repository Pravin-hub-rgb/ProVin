Jab `2.2 Forcing Lines` mein tumne check ya capture ke baad opponent ke short replies dekhe, ab ek important safety net add karna hai. Har forcing move attractive lag sakta hai, lekin board par move click karne se pehle tumhara apna king aur exposed piece bhi protect hona chahiye.

Jaise road cross karte waahin tum sirf green light nahi dekhte, balki apni lane, oncoming traffic, aur apni destination bhi check karte ho, waise chess move mein apni piece ka destination aur opponent ka counterattack dekho. Yeh teen-second habit rapid games mein bahut badi galtiyan rok deti hai.

# 2.3 Move Se Pehle Safety Check

**Safety check** ka matlab hai move chhodne se pehle verify karna ki opponent tumhari nayi position ko turant punish nahi kar sakta. Yahan “punish” ka matlab hai material, king, ya position ka important loss. Safety check calculation ka replacement nahi hai; woh calculation ko order aur discipline deta hai.

## Check 1: Apna king

Sabse pehle apne king ki current safety aur possible destination dekho. King ke aage koi check, pin, ya open file ho to move ko blind nahi chhodna chahiye. **Pin** ka matlab hai ek piece king ke aage khadi ho aur us piece ko move karne se king attacked ho jayega. Isliye pinned piece ko sirf dekhna nahi, us piece ke move karne ke baad king ka kya hoga yeh bhi sochna hai.

Agar tumhara move king ko attacked square le ja raha hai, to opponent ke replies count karo. Sirf yeh sochna ki “king paas dikhta hai” kafi nahi; board ke perspective mein woh square safe bhi hona chahiye ya clear escape/response hona chahiye.

## Check 2: Piece ki nayi destination

Jo piece move ho rahi hai, uski destination ko akhri baar mein dobara dekhni hai. Kya woh square enemy piece ke direct capture range mein hai? Kya move ke baad piece ka original control area khatam ho raha hai? Kya piece blocked ho gayi hai aur ab woh practical contribution nahi de rahi?

Isse “hanging piece” ka concept naturally milta hai. Hanging piece wo piece hai jo opponent ko free capture dene wali ho kyunki uska defender, control, ya retreat square missing hai. Calculate karte waqt apni material ki list nahi, apne pieces ki protection ki list bhi rakho.

## Check 3: Counterattack aur discovered idea

Apna move sochne ke baad ek baar opponent ki taraf se poochho: “Main jo kar raha hoon, iska jawab kya hai?” Agar opponent ka reply check, capture, ya aapke king par attack de, to line ko abhi evaluate karna hai. Yeh generic “what if” sochna nahi, balki position ko immediate opponent perspective se dekhna hai.

Phir **discovered attack** ka basic idea socho, jismein ek piece move karne se doosre piece ki line suddenly khul jaati hai. Abhi hum ise detail mein nahi dekhenge; agle topic ke liye itna kaafi hai ki apni move ke baad nayi lines, pins, aur attacks scan karo.

## A 10-second safety script

Har move par teen chhote sawal repeat karo:

1. King ke paas kya immediate danger hai?
2. Move karne wali piece protected hai aur uska nayi square safe hai?
3. Opponent ka ek forcing counterattack kya hai?

Agar teenon jawab clear hain, to move ko candidate list mein rakho. Agar ek jawab unclear hai, to board par piece ko touch karke ya mentally move karke dobara check karo; itna time lena beginner ke liye smarter hai.

Safety check ko overthink mat karo. Iska kaam har move ko engine-level proof banana nahi, balki ek obvious blunder ko avoid karna hai—jaise apni queen ko free capture dena, king ko open file par le jaana, ya defender ko move karke undefended piece chhod dena.

## What It Is NOT

**Safety check** ka matlab “har move se pehle poora position ka engine analysis” nahi. Yeh ek targeted scan hai: king, moved piece, aur immediate counterattack.

**Check** dekhna sirf apne pieces ko dekhna nahi hai. Opponent ke forcing replies bhi checks hain, captures hain, ya threats ban sakte hain. Inko ignore karna safety check ko adhoora banata hai.

## Checkpoint for a real game

Rapid game ke baad apne notes mein un moves ko mark karo jin par opponent ne turant material capture kiya tha. Har mark ke saamne likho: king unsafe ho gaya tha, koi piece free capture de raha tha, ya counterattack calculate nahi kiya tha? Pattern dikhne se tumhari safety habit measurable ho jayegi.
