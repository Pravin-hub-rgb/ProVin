# 1.5 Simple Checkmates aur Combinations

## Check aur checkmate

**Check** ka matlab hai current move ne opponent ke king ko attack kiya. Har check mate nahi hota, kyunki opponent king ko move kar sakta hai, check ko block kar sakta hai, ya checking piece ko capture kar sakta hai. **Checkmate** tab hota hai jab check ke baad yeh teen response options sab band hon.

Isko board par aise samjho jaise ek darwaza check hone ke baad bhi alternate exit na ho. Tumhe sirf “king ko hit kar raha hai” nahi dekhna; capture, block, aur king escape — teenon count karne hain. Yehi reason hai ki sparse back-rank positions aur simple endgame mates strong players ko bhi foran dikh jaati hain.

## Combination kya hai

**Combination** aisi move sequence hai jismein ek tactic doosre ko enable karta hai aur final aim material win, checkmate, ya major advantage hota hai. Iske moves usually forcing hote hain, lekin kabhi ek quiet setup move bhi pehla step hota hai—jaise kisi piece ko better square par laana—jisse baad ki forcing sequence possible bane. Ek single capture bhi tactic ho sakti hai, lekin combination mein move order aur opponent ke replies important hote hain. Simple early-1000 combinations usually 2–4 moves ki hoti hain, isliye lambi forced line ko pehle se calculate karne ki zaroorat nahi.

Jaise recipe mein pehle onion fry karke flavor banate hain aur phir final dish assemble karte hain, waise combination mein first move board ko setup karta hai aur last move finish karta hai. Is analogy ka fayda yeh hai ki har move ko random nahi, goal ke liye dekhna sikhate hain.

## How to calculate a simple combination

Pehle candidate final move dhoondho: kya checkmate, major capture, ya strong threat mil raha hai? Phir us final move ko achieve karne ke liye required forcing move par wapas jaao. Ab opponent ka strongest legal response calculate karo, aur har response ke baad apna planned continuation verify karo.

Is process mein candidate move ko opponent ke easiest response se nahi, strongest legal response se compare karna hota hai. Jab multiple moves se same goal mile, simplest line often practical hoti hai; is course mein calculation ki clarity ko engine-style deep lines se zyada important rakhte hain.

## Exercise — put the combination together

Ab hum yahi back-rank position dobara dekh rahe hain, is baar isse finish-line exercise ki tarah solve karna hai. Is position mein pehla check defender ko force karta hai, aur uske baad final mate ka verification matter karta hai.

**Time limit/task:** 3 minutes. Find the complete forcing combination, then list the king escape squares after the final check.

**Best move:** `1. Nf7+!`

**Short main line:** `1. Nf7+ Rxf7 2. Qg8#`

**Why it works:** The knight check makes the rook capture on f7, so the rook leaves its back-rank defensive job. The queen then lands on g8 with adjacent check; the g1 rook protects the queen, g7 is covered, h7 is occupied, and the rook cannot block a check from an adjacent queen.

**Common mistake:** Treating the final queen move as the whole combination and missing the forced rook capture. A combination ka confidence final pattern se nahi, opponent ke strongest response ko verify karne se aati hai.

## What It Is NOT

Check aur checkmate ek hi cheez nahi hain. Har check ko mate bol dena common beginner mistake hai; opponent ko ek legal escape, block, ya capture mil sakta hai.

Combination bhi guaranteed win nahi hai. Agar opponent ke multiple replies hain aur tumne unhe calculate nahi kiya, to attractive-looking line practical game mein fail ho sakti hai.
