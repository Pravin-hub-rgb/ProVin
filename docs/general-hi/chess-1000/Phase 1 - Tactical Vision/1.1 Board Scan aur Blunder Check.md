# 1.1 Board Scan aur Blunder Check

## Scan ka simple order

Chess mein har move calculate karna possible nahi, especially 10+0 mein. Isliye starting mein ek fixed order use karo: pehle opponent ka king, phir checks, phir captures, phir opponent ki threats, aur last mein apni normal plan. Is order ko ek traffic officer ki tarah samjho — pehle koi accident ho raha hai ya nahi, phir normal traffic flow dekho.

“Check” ka matlab hai tumhari current move opponent ke king ko attack karegi. “Capture” ka matlab hai koi unprotected valuable piece foran le sakta hai. “Threat” ka matlab hai opponent tumhare baad ek forcing move karega, even if abhi board par move nahi hai; ise future mein dekhna part of the scan hai.

Scan ko 5–10 seconds se zyada lamba karna bhi nahi hona chahiye. Pehle immediate problem note karo, phir candidate moves compare karo, phir move chuno. Yeh thoda mechanical lagega, lekin crowded positions mein emotion ko pause karke simple checks ko chance deta hai.

## Blunder kya hai

**Blunder** ka matlab hai aisi move jo opponent ko clear tactical ya material advantage de deti hai, jaise tumhare piece ko bina protection capture karne ka chance dena. Yeh hamesha “galat move” ka synonym nahi, lekin practical rapid rating ke liye ek aisi mistake jise foran identify karna bahut valuable hai.

Sabse common beginner blunder mein se ek “maine piece move ki, opponent ko force nahi mila” wali feeling hai. Asli test yeh hai: move ke baad opponent ke liye kya immediate reply hai? Agar reply clean capture, check, ya fork hai, to move se pehle wapas scan karo.

Iska matlab piece movements dobara padhne ki zaroorat nahi; focus apne move ke baad ki board par shift karna hai. Ek tactical move ka simple kaam yahi hai: opponent ke king, queen, rook, ya kisi important square ko vulnerable banana. Detailed fork aur skewer patterns abhi nahi padhayenge; agle topic mein unko alag-alag samjhenge.

## Practical scan

Rapid game mein har move par full calculation zaroori nahi, lekin ek repeatable scan zaroori hai. Start mein thoda mechanical lagega; baad mein aap bina engine ke “king, check, capture, threat” sequence ko ek glance mein dekh paoge. Complex positions ki deeper calculation Phase 2 mein seekhenge, isliye abhi hum simple wins ko reliably notice karna seekh rahe hain.

Scan ka tradeoff yeh hai ki kuch quiet, non-tactical positions ko foran classify nahi kar paoge. Isliye har move ko forced move samajhne ki galti na karo; scan ke baad safe development ya pawn move bhi legitimate choice ho sakta hai. Tactical eye ko naye traffic rules pehchane wali practice ki tarah use karo; har position ko threat samajhna zaroori nahi.

## What It Is NOT

Board scan engine replace nahi karta. Yeh sirf tumhe woh immediate possibilities dikhata hai jo calculation start karna worthwhile banati hain; deep lines ke liye abhi aur tools chahiye.

Blunder ko sirf material count karke define nahi kar sakte. Kabhi positional loss bhi important hota hai, aur kabhi material capture ke baad compensation milta hai. Is course mein priority clean, avoidable losses ko pehle rokne par hai.
