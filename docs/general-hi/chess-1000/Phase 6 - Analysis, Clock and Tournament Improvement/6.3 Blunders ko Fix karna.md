# 6.3 Blunders ko Fix karna

**Blunder** woh move hai jisne aapki position ko itna kharab kar diya ki aapko material, check, ya mate ka clear risk face karna pade—jaise koi free piece ho jaye, mate diya jaaye, ya ek simple tactic allow ho. Iska matlab hamesha “engine ka sabse bada red arrow” nahi hai; kabhi practical move bhi blunder hota hai jab us move ka reason aapke paas nahi tha.

Blunder ko blame samajhna healthy nahi hai. “Main slow tha” statement useful tab hai jab usse next game ka testable action nikle, jaise “opponent ke development move ke baad immediate check count karunga.” Bina action ke confession aaj feel achha karta hai, par next game ke behaviour ko nahi badalta.

## Pehle mistake ka naam nahi, cause dekho

Analysis mein hum teen cheezein alag karte hain: position, move aur cause. Position woh facts hain jo board par maujood the; move woh choice jo aapne ki; cause woh missing check ya thinking error thi jisne choice kharab ki.

Aap ek mistake ko in simple families mein rakh sakte ho:

- **Tactic miss** — capture, check, fork, pin, ya discovered attack ko search nahi kiya.
- **Loose piece** — aapka piece defended nahi tha, lekin aapne uska protection assume kar liya.
- **King danger** — check, pawn storm, ya open line ko underestimate kiya.
- **Structure aur plan** — pawn move, exchange, ya piece activity ka reason clear nahi tha.
- **Clock pressure** — position difficult thi, lekin saara time ek hi unclear choice par chala gaya.

Ek game mein ek hi mistake ko do families mein daalna bhi possible hai. Jaise aapne loose knight rakha aur time pressure mein usse defend nahi kiya; is case mein cause ka combined note zyada useful hota hai. Lekin category banana apne aap mein naya distraction nahi banana chahiye. Har mistake ko cause aur next cue ke saath samajhna zaroori hai.

## Lichess par blunder fix karne ka cycle

Aap apni recent rated game kholiye aur ek red position choose kijiye. Aapko “sabse badi mistake” nahi, apne liye sabse actionable mistake chahiye, kyunki large volume se seekh kam aur actionable mistake se correction zyada hota hai.

Is cycle ko aise chalaiye:

1. Position ko pause karke FEN aur apna move likhiye.
2. Batayein ki aapne kya threat expect kiya tha.
3. Apna move restart karke opponent ke forcing reply khud sochne ki koshish kijiye.
4. Ab engine se best continuation aur ek alternative compare kijiye.
5. Blunder ko cause category mein rakhiye.
6. Ek chhota next-week assignment likhiye, jaise “agli teen games mein loose piece ko move karne se pehle defender count karo.”

Engine check karne se pehle move likhna is cycle ka important guardrail hai. Isse aap apni calculation improve kar sakte ho aur sirf answer reveal karke habit nahi banate. Jo line aapne khud dekhi, wahi baad mein pattern ban sakti hai.

## Har category ke liye ek practical cue

Tactic miss ko fix karne ke liye har capturing ya checking candidate ke baad opponent ka simplest reply likhiye. Piece values se pehle forced sequence ko check karna seekhna hota hai, lekin board par aisa likhne se aapki calculation habit visibly badalti hai.

Loose piece ko fix karne ke liye move karne se pehle defenders, blockers aur capture squares count kijiye. Ek defender hona kaafi nahi hota agar woh aapke next capture ke baad move ho jaye; isliye “defended” shabd ko thoda khol kar sochna hai.

King danger ko fix karne ke liye opponent ka pawn advance, open file aur discovered attack ko ek saath dekhiye. King safety ka check karna ek line nahi, position ka lens hai; aapka king sirf tab move karta hai jab uski protection, squares aur counterplay ka practical reason ho.

Structure aur plan ko fix karne ke liye har pawn move ke baad sirf squares count na karein, aage ka purpose likhiye. “Pawn move khol raha hai, lekin kaunsa piece us file par aayega?” Jaisa sawaal future plan ko visible banata hai.

Clock pressure ko fix karne ke liye uncertain calculation ko unlimited detail mein kholne se pehle jaldi switch kijiye. Ek line calculate na ho pa rahi toh simple, forcing, ya check move choose karke position ko reset karna bhi strong decision ho sakta hai.

## Ek blunder ke baad recover karna

Blunder hone ke baad turant resign karna ek hi option nahi hai. Pehle immediate forcing moves check kijiye: kya check, capture, block, ya threat hai jo aapko ek aur minute de sakta hai? Aap apne king ko bachayein, piece ko regain karein, ya worst case position ko simplify karke draw chances create karein.

Recovery ke baad teen sawaal poochhiye: “best move ab kya hai?”, “main kis mistake ko repeat nahi karunga?”, aur “kya result ab bhi improve ho sakta hai?” Isse aap loss ke baad dobara apni soch mein aa jaate ho, sirf scoreboard dekhne ke bajaye.

Yahan Black ko move dena hai aur FEN `r5k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/5PPP/4R1K1 b - - 0 1` hai. Is position mein Black ke liye koi immediate check nahi hai; `Kf8` ek legal practical move hai. Rook a8 diagonal capture nahi kar sakta, aur `Ra1` ke baad bhi e1 par White ka rook first-rank line block karta hai. Aap pehle rook ka path aur check ke blockers verify karke false mate claim ko reject karo, phir engine se legal continuation compare kijiye.

## Agle hafte ka assignment

Aap apne error list mein sirf teen triggers chun lijiye. Har trigger ke liye ek observable count rakhiye, jaise “three games mein loose pieces,” aur ek prevention cue likhiye. Phir un games ko review karo aur dekho ki cue ne mistake rokne mein madad ki ya nahi.

Assignment ko apne daily routine mein chhota rakhiye. Teen minute ka note bhi useful hai agar woh exact position, exact missed question aur next move capture karta hai. Lambi wish-list nahi, repeatable evidence chahiye.

## What It Is NOT

Blunder fix karna **har loss ko catastrophic banana nahi hai**. Har game ka result ek sample hai, aur improvement ka signal board decisions se aata hai. Ek loss ka matlab “main kamzor hoon” nahi, sirf itna hai ki agle review ka ek case mil gaya hai.

Blunder fix karna **sirf engine percentages badalna nahi hai**. Aapka score agar badla, lekin woh same tactical pattern nahi raha, toh number ki chhoti improvement ko readiness na samjhiye. Behaviour aur decision quality ko zyada time dijiye.

Blunder fix karna **har mistake ko engine ke against justify karna nahi hai**. Kabhi aapka move best practical move hota hai, phir bhi opponent ne behtar position bana li. Honest note mein “better move available tha, lekin maine choose nahi kiya” aur “move theek tha, result kharab hua” ka fark rakhiye.

Ab jab aapke blunder patterns clear hon, toh in patterns ko 10+0 ke clock ke saath combine karna zaroori hai. Kyunki wahi tactical attention training session mein poori tarah kaam karta hai aur real game mein aadha second baki ho, wahan routine ki zaroorat padti hai.
