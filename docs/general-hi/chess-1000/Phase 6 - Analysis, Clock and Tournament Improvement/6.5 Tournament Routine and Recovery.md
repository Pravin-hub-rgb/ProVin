Exam ke din ek subject ke baad doosra subject turant start karna har learner ke liye productive nahi hota. Thoda paani, stretch, khana aur chain reset hone ke baad agla attempt better hota hai. Rated chess tournaments bhi aise hi hote hain: ek result ke baad turant next game shuru karna smart routine nahi, agar body aur mind ko recover hone ka thoda time nahi mila.

`6.4 10+0 Clock Routine` mein humne ek game ke andar time stages aur practical selection ko samjha. Ab wahi discipline event level par chahiye, jahan aap ek session ke baad result dekh kar recover karte ho aur next game ko sahi mindset se start karte ho.

# 6.5 Tournament Routine and Recovery

**Tournament** kai rated games ka organized set hai jahan opponents, time control aur rounds ek schedule ke hisaab se hote hain. Aapka goal har round mein jeetna nahi, balki apni preparation ko real conditions mein test karna aur next round ke liye useful information lena hai.

Lichess par event join karne se pehle event page par time control, rated ya unrated status, duration aur pool check karna smart routine hai. Event ke andar aap apni rating ko protect karne ke bajaye fair training sample choose karenge, taaki har round aapko genuine practice mile.

## Suitable rated event kaise choose karein

10+0 rapid events hi aapke current target ke liye practical starting point ho sakte hain, lekin “suitable” ka matlab sirf time control match karna nahi hai. Event ki estimated duration aapke din ke budget mein aana chahiye, aur aapko result ke baad analysis ke liye thoda time rakhna chahiye.

Event pool ko dekh kar realistic expectation set kijiye. Current level ke aas-paas opponents se aap seekh sakte ho, lekin har weaker opponent ko avoid karna bhi training nahi hai. Kuch stronger games aapko uncomfortable ideas dikhayengi, jabki kuch familiar games aapke recurring mistakes ko clearly expose karengi.

Enter karne se pehle ek fallback soch lijiye. Agar event unexpected lamba ho, network issue aaye, ya aapka schedule change ho, toh aap kya karenge? Sirf “aaj mood nahi hai” par withdraw karna healthy nahi, lekin apni health, sleep aur real-life commitments ko blindly ignore karna bhi tournament routine nahi hai.

## Event se pehle ka warm-up

Event se 15–20 minutes pehle Lichess par ek short warm-up karein: ek familiar opening ke moves, kuch simple tactics, aur ek quick clock awareness exercise. Iska maqsad naya giant opening chapter padhna nahi hai; purpose aapke hands, eyes aur decision routine ko activate karna hai.

Har game se pehle ek line likh sakte hain: “Aaj mujhe apna plan clearly batana hai, aggressive hokar random move nahi karni.” Aapka goal har opponent ko beat karna nahi, apni attention quality ko maintain karna hai. Jaise warm-up se body ready hoti hai, waise hi ek fixed pre-game checklist decision ko clean rakhti hai.

## Har round ke baad result check karo

Har game ke turant baad result, opponent rating, time control aur apna rough feeling note kar lijiye. Aapko har move turant engine mein check karne ki zaroorat nahi; pehle result ko context dein aur body ko recover hone ka chance dein.

Agli game se pehle paani piyen, screen break lein, bathroom jayein, aur paanch gehri saansein lein. Ye chhoti reset routine aapke focused attention ko preserve karti hai. Phir ek fixed pre-game checklist use kijiye, naya “last minute” plan nahi.

Position ko apni error list mein source move ke saath save kijiye.

## Loss se seekhna, punish karna nahi

Loss review mein “main kamzor player hoon” shuru mat kijiye. Pehle decisive moment tak jaiye, apna move aur opponent ka forcing reply likhiye, phir alternatives compare kijiye. Har loss se sirf ek ya do actionable lessons nikaalne ka target rakhiye, taaki notes actionable rahen aur emotional baggage na badhe.

Aap result-pool gaming se bachein. Iska matlab hai opponents ko sirf apne rating ya expected result ke hisaab se select karke easy points farm karna, weaker pools ko manipulate karna, ya favourable result na aaye toh event chhod dena. Aap fair rated events choose karein, realistic opponents ke saath khelein, aur opponent ko seekhne ka resource samjhein.

Rating ka ek game hamesha complete story nahi hota. Pool strength, time pressure, form aur luck bhi result mein mix hote hain, isliye ek isolated result se personal worth judge karna healthy nahi hai. Event ke end mein 20-game sample jaisa broader record dekhiye, kisi ek lucky peak par nahi.

## Event ke baad recovery

Event khatam hone ke baad turant ek aur tournament queue join karna recovery ko bypass karta hai. Food, paani, light movement aur sleep aapke decision quality ko support karte hain, kyunki 10+0 bhi mentally demanding hai. Kam se kam ek break lein, phir results dekh kar ek written summary banayein.

Aap score se pehle quality ka review kar sakte hain: kitne games time trouble mein hue, decisive errors kis type ke the, aur kitne games mein aapne apna plan clearly follow kiya. Phir rating ka snapshot dekh kar trend note kijiye. Isse aap next event select kar sakte ho, kyunki aapko pata hoga aapki preparation kis type ke pressure mein better thi.

## 45-minute recovery routine

Aap ek session ko review, rest, aur next-step ke teen hisson mein split kar sakte hain. 15 minutes ek loss ka written review, 10 minutes hydration aur walk, aur 20 minutes next event ke liye preparation. Agar tournament ke bahar ho, toh 20 minutes tactics ya position work bhi useful hoga.

Recovery ka fayda tabhi hai jab aap usse avoid nahi karte. Har event ke baad “kal ka ek kaam” likhiye, jaise “king danger ko count karna” ya “first ten moves ka simple plan use karna.” Ye kaam agle event ka bridge banega.

## What It Is NOT

Tournament routine **har round ko jeetne ka pressure nahi hai**. Agar har result ko final exam samjhenge, toh ek haar ke baad focus khatam ho sakta hai. Har round information aur practice ka sample hai.

Tournament routine **opponent shopping ya result-pool gaming nahi hai**. Easy points ke liye favorable opponents dhoondhna fair training nahi, aur rating ko sirf result se farm karna sustainable progress nahi hai. Realistic events, fair play, aur useful review choose kijiye.

Tournament routine **rest ko khatam karna nahi hai**. Kuch logon ko recovery ki value samajhne se focus kam ho jaata hai, lekin 10+0 mein bhi mental energy zaroori hai. Apne schedule ko realistic rakhna long-term discipline hai.

Ab aapke paas Phase 6 ke tools ek saath hain: analysis, engine feedback, blunder fixes, clock routine, aur tournament recovery. Agla exercise pack inko apni decisions par lagayega; uske baad `6.6 Quiz - Analysis, Clock and Tournament Improvement` se aap apna understanding check karenge, aur phir `7.1 Personal Improvement Plan` mein un tools ko apne 10-week schedule mein convert karenge.
