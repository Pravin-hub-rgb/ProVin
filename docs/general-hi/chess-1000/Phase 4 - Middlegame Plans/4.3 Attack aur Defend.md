# 4.3 Attack aur Defend

**Attack** ka matlab hai opponent ki position mein coordinated pressure banana, jisme ek ya zyada pieces ek target—king, weak pawn, weak square, trapped piece—par kaam karein. Sirf piece aage laana attack nahi hota; jab us piece ka kaam baaki pieces aur squares ke saath fit hota hai, tab pressure plan banata hai. Attack ko samajhne ke liye first target, entry aur support dekhna useful hai.

King centre mein ho toh attack zyada dangerous ho sakta hai, kyunki uske aage kam defenders hote hain. Open file, exposed king aur back rank—yeh attack ke common conditions hain; fork ka matlab hai ek piece ek saath do targets par attack kare. In mein se koi ek condition khaali dekhkar turant attack nahi hona chahiye. Board par aapko poora chain check karna hai: kya target vulnerable hai, kya entry milegi, aur kya defender ko react karne ka time rahega?

## Attack ki chain

Pehla element **target** hai. Target ko “king” bol ke hi chhod dena beginner ke liye useful nahi; baad mein woh decide karna padta hai ki king ke aas-paas ka weak square, back rank, loose pawn ya trapped piece aasan target hai. Doosra element **entry** hai, yaani attacker ko kis square se andar aana hai. Teesra element **support** hai, kyunki support piece ka capture aksar unsafe bana deta hai, na ki impossible; defender overloaded ya pinned ho sakta hai, ya woh turant recapture nahi kar sakta.

Example: open c-file par white rook ka pressure, target ko knight aur bishop ka support, aur weak `c7` square—yeh sab milkar ek attack banate hain. Har move ka test yeh hai ki kya agla attack step banta hai ya kya ek piece ne target dekh kar aage badhkar apna hi cover chhod diya. Agar pieces alag-alag pressure karein, toh attack ko “sab pieces attack kar rahe hain” kehna galat hoga.

## Defence ka kaam sirf king bachana nahi

**Defence** ka matlab hai opponent ki attacking resources ko mehnga banana aur apni position ki integrity sambhalna. Isme weak square cover karna, attacking piece exchange karna, critical file block karna, counterattack dhoondna, ya apne pieces ko safer squares le jaana aa sakta hai. Defence ka strongest move woh hai jo opponent ka plan slow kare aur saath mein apna practical plan bhi banaye.

Aap har baar “king ko move kar do” bolne se pehle poochho: kya move ke baad king sirf ek temporary jagah gaya, ya uske peeche permanent cover ban gaya? Kyunki king ko ek square aage push karne ki koshish aksar usi file mein wapas attack laati hai. Better move woh hai jo entry band kare, exchange kare, ya opponent ko uski hi weakness par force kare.

## Attack aur defence ek hi game ke do raaste hain

Ek position mein aap attack bhi kar sakte ho aur defensive resource bhi rakh sakte ho. Agar aapka rook weak square par pressure daal raha hai par aapki knight aur king aise exposed hain ki opponent ka pawn break aapko attack kar de, toh attack ke saath safety ka insurance zaroori hai. Isi tarah, agar aap defensive pieces ko sirf cover ke li rakhte ho, aapka opponent free activity paa sakta hai.

Counterattack ko samajhna bhi zaroori hai, par ise “always counterattack” formula mat banaiye. Counterattack tab meaningful hota hai jab woh aapke weak piece ko target kare, aapki extra pawns ko kaam de, ya opponent ke king ko uski hi position mein pressure de. Aapka defence, attack ko completely cancel nahi karta; woh board par control ki ladaai ko agle stage tak le jaata hai.

## Practical attack-defence checklist

Board par attack samajhne ke liye yeh sequence yaad rakho: king safety, target vulnerability, entry square, supporting piece aur defender ke escape squares. Phir defence mein yeh dekho: critical square ka owner kaun hai, kaunsa piece attacker ko rok sakta hai, aur kya exchange karna apni position ko better karta hai. Yeh checklist line-by-line theory nahi hai; yeh aapke attention ko 20-30 seconds mein concrete board par le aata hai.

| Sawaal | Attack mein | Defence mein |
|---|---|---|
| Target kya hai? | King, weak pawn ya trapped piece | Entry square, defender ya back rank |
| Kya line khuli hai? | Open file/diagonal ko support | File block ya cover move |
| Kya backup hai? | Second piece target ko hold karta hai | Counterattacker ya exchange |
| Kya badla? | Force weak square | Force opponent replan kare |

## What It Is NOT

Attack **har move check karna nahi** hai. Check ek tool hai, target aur attacking plan ko execute karne ka; sirf baar-baar check karne se pieces exposed ho sakte hain aur defender ko counterplay mil sakta hai. Phir bhi check tabhi zaroori ho sakta hai jab woh king ka covering piece nikal de, entry force kare ya threat ko immediately banaye.

Defence **passive khadi rehna nahi** hai. Sirf ek square move karna agar woh do moves baad phir vulnerable ho jaata hai, toh woh delay nahi, free tempo opponent ko de raha hai. Better defence apne pieces ko support karke, lines block karke, exchange karke ya counterattack karke position ko permanently badal deti hai.

Attack aur defence **rating guarantee ya winner ka formula nahi** hain. Material, king location, pawns aur piece placement ek saath decide karte hain; isliye har position ko poori tarah check karo. 4.3 ka idea yeh hai ki aap “mere king ko attack kar raha hai” bolne ke bajaye aap dekh sako ki woh attack kis target, kis entry aur kis support se aaya hai.

Ab attack aur defence ke baad `4.4 Simplify ya Pieces Badhaayein` mein dekhenge ki kab material badhana sahi hai aur kab extra pieces ko exchange karke position ko aasaan karna behtar hai.
