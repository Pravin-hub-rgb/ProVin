Jab aap kisi recipe mein ingredient change karte ho, toh wahi instructions har baar kaam nahi karte; aap taste aur texture dekh kar next step adjust karte ho. Chess planning bhi aise hi hai—opening ka setup ek recipe hai, lekin opponent ke har jawab ke baad position ka taste badal sakta hai. Isliye goal ko ek line pakad kar nahi chahiye, balki use board par test karna hota hai.

4.4 Simplify ya Pieces Badhaayein mein humne seekha ki extra material, active placement aur safe trade ko ek doosre se compare kaise karte hain. 4.5 `Plan se Position tak` mein ab planning ke final step par kaam karenge: apne candidate goal ko actual position mein kaise convert karein aur har opponent reply ke baad plan ko kaise update karein.

# 4.5 Plan se Position tak

Middlegame planning ka last useful part **position** ko samajhna hai—yaani current board par kaunsi cheezein already achieve ho chuki hain aur kaunsi abhi sirf imagine ki hui possibility hain. Aapka plan ek future sentence hai, jabki position current evidence hai. Dono ko compare karte rehna hi plan ko calculation aur real decision banata hai.

Ek candidate plan ko board par teen layers mein check kar sakte hain. Pehla layer immediate move hai: kya ek legal, purposeful move hai jo goal ki taraf ja raha hai? Doosra layer opponent response hai: opponent ke best resistance ke baad aapka target, file ya king square bhi available rahega? Teesra layer end position hai: kya aapki pieces wahan actual pressure, material aur activity ke saath pahunchengi?

## Apne plan ko sentence mein likho

“Main queenside expand karunga” bahut broad hai. Usse refine karke likh sakte hain, “Main c-pawn se queenside space banaunga, opposing d-pawn ko challenge karunga, phir rook ke liye file ya weak square dhoondhunga.” Is sentence mein goal, pawn break aur final piece ka role connected hai. Aapko har move par naya essay nahi likhna; bas apne goal ke teen moves ya teen intermediate steps ko note karna kaafi hai.

Phir check karo ki woh route aapki current position mein available hai. Agar rook ke liye file tabhi khulegi jab aap king ko aage le jayen, toh plan ka cost calculate karo. Agar pawn break opponent ke strong piece ko target bana deta hai, toh attack aur defence ke saath compare karo. Yahi stage par opening theory khatam aur actual planning shuru hota hai.

## Opponent ke move ke baad dobara scan

Opponent ka move aapka “correct” plan turant reject nahi karta, lekin aapko naya evidence deta hai. Agar woh defending square par rook ya pawn le gaya, toh weak square ka pressure kam ho gaya; aap alternate target ya exchange consider kar sakte ho. Agar woh counterattack shuru kare, toh aap apna plan modify karne ke liye defensive resource choose kar sakte ho, jabki original opening line par nahin tik sakte.

Har move ke baad teen quick questions poochho: king safe hai? Plan ka next step abhi bhi legal aur useful hai? Kya opponent ne meri position ka best target badal diya? Agar teenon ke jawab “haan” hain, toh aapka confidence badhega; agar ek bhi jawab “nahi” hai, toh dobara evaluate karo. Yeh process engine ki zaroorat nahi karta—yeh 1000-level games mein decision filter hai, magic formula nahi.

## Position ki quality ko compare karna

Ek final position ko dekh kar sirf material count mat karo. Rook active hai ya blocked? King ke paas cover hai ya open squares? Weak pawn ka defender aas-paas hai? Kya aapke pieces mutually defend kar sakte hain, ya har piece ko alag bachana padega? In practical questions ka comparison aapko batata hai ki move ne aapki position ko genuinely better banaya ya sirf move number badhaya.

Aap isse 10-minute study routine mein use kar sakte ho. Lichess par apni ek game choose karo, apna first middlegame goal likho, aur 10-15 moves ke liye opponent ke responses ke saath alternative routes note karo. Engine ka use analysis ke liye optional verification hai; pehla kaam apne reasoning ko honest likhna hai. Isse aap apni recurring mistakes ko patterns mein dekh paoge—jaise har baar open file ko dekh kar king ignore karna.

## Ek example: plan, interruption, replan

Suppose aapka plan hai queenside pawns se weak squares banana. White ke liye `c5` ke baad c-file ka pressure imagine karo, lekin opponent `...dxc5` se central structure badal deta hai aur aapke queenside space kam ho jaata. Ab plan cancel karke har move blindly follow karna galat hota hai; aap attack continue kar sakte ho, simplify kar sakte ho, ya kingside counter-plan bana sakte ho.

Replanning ka matlab naya opening dhoondna nahi hai. Matlab hai goal ke peeche woh reason dekhna jo board par abhi valid hai, phir us reason ke liye best route choose karna. Yeh adaptability 1000 rating ke target ke liye valuable hai, lekin ek hi game mein baar-baar direction badalna bhi aimless hota hai; har switch ka ek clear reason hona chahiye.

Is process ko ek compact decision loop mein dekh sakte hain:

| Decision stage | Apna sawaal | Agar jawab clear hai toh |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Board par kya protected aur weak hai? | Position ka base read |
| Direction | Main goal kya hai? | Plan ka focus |
| Test | Best reply ke baad bhi route bacha hai? | Practical candidate |
| Decision | Move se king, material, activity kya hota hai? | One move |
| Review | Naya board evidence kya hai? | Replan ya continue |

## What It Is NOT

Plan se position tak ka kaam **apne aap engine ka replacement nahi hai**. Aap shallow calculation aur basic tactics ke saath candidate moves compare kar sakte ho, lekin complex position mein calculation ki depth abhi important rahegi. Strategy ko calculation se replace karna nahi, dono ko ek saath use karna hai.

Replanning **random mood swings nahi** hai. Har direction change ka reason weak square, counterattack, piece placement ya material change se aana chahiye. Sirf ek “interesting” move dekh kar plan chhod dena flexible nahi, focus khona hai.

“Winning plan” ka label bhi guarantee nahi hai. Lichess games mein tactics, time aur opponent ki choices change kar sakte hain; isliye plan ko probability aur safety ke saath evaluate karo. 45-minute daily schedule mein aapka goal har game mein ek measurable improvement hai, jaise pehle se zyada purposeful moves ya kam king mistakes—not a fixed rating promise.

4.5 ke baad `4.6 Exercise Pack` mein hum in strategies ko six legal positions par test karenge. Har position mein pehle board dekhoge, phir limited attempt ke baad solution reveal karoge, aur uske baad reason dobara padhoge.
