# 0.2 Baseline Test

## Baseline ka matlab

Baseline matlab jahan tum abhi khade ho — na ideal position, na koi test ka result jo tumhe prove karna chahta hai. Yeh chhava hai jo aaj ki practice se compare karne ke liye banegi. Isliye har position pehle tumhare attempt ko note karo, phir answer kholo.

Yeh test kisi ko chess mein kamzor ya strong prove nahi karta. Iska kaam yeh identify karna hai ki checking, capturing, threat creation, aur simple checkmate mein se kaunsa area tumhe abhi extra attention maang raha hai. Jaise doctor symptoms dekhkar final diagnosis nahi lagata, waise baseline tumhari future rating ka final result nahi hai.

Har board ke saath FEN diya gaya hai. **FEN** ka matlab hai Forsyth–Edwards Notation, ek compact chess-position notation jismein pieces, side to move, aur kuch game-state information likhi hoti hai. Tumhe ise parse karne ki zaroorat nahi; is course mein FEN ko board ke exact text record ki tarah use karo.

## Test kaise lein

Har position par 2–3 minutes do, move likho, aur answer se pehle ek reason likho. “Main kyun chuna?” ka ek line bahut zaroori hai, kyunki sahi move ka pattern seekhna galat move ke pattern se zyada transferable hai. Engine ya solution tabhi kholo jab attempt complete ho. Sequence simple development se start hokar checks, captures, aur back-rank/checkmate decisions ki taraf badhta hai.

Neeche ke boards White-perspective mein hain: White neeche ki side se dekhta hai, a1 board ke bottom-left mein hai, aur side-to-move diagram aur FEN dono mein clearly likha hai. Har exercise ke answer us position ke neeche hidden accordion mein hai, isliye pehle khud solve karna zaroori hai.

## Position 1 — ek practical development move

Yeh opening-style position hai, jisme tactical calculation nahi, ek purposeful move identify karna hai. Tumhara goal sirf ek legal move chhodna nahi; central control aur king safety ke liye move ko explain karna hai.

![Phase 0 baseline position 01](/diagrams/chess/phase-0/phase-0-baseline-01.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `rnbqkbnr/pppp1ppp/8/4p3/4P3/8/PPPP1PPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 0 2`  
**Time limit/task:** 3 minutes. Choose one strong developing move that helps prepare the center and kingside castle, then write its purpose.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Nf3`

**Short main line:** `1. Nf3` is enough for this diagnostic; the important point is the purpose, not a long opening theory line.

**Why it works:** The knight leaves the back rank, develops toward the center, and makes kingside castling easier to prepare. It is a practical choice even though several legal developing moves could appear attractive.

**Common mistake:** Choosing a pawn move first and then moving the same knight immediately, which spends two tempi. “Tempi” means a useful move that gains a turn without giving the opponent a free reply.

</details>

## Position 2 — check and mate awareness

Ab board sparse hai, isliye queen aur king ke around ki lines saaf dikh rahi hain. Tumhe forcing move pehle dhoondhna hai, phir opponent ke escape squares check karne hain.

![Phase 0 baseline position 02](/diagrams/chess/phase-0/phase-0-baseline-02.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `6kr/5ppp/8/3Q4/8/8/8/4K2R w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 3 minutes. Find White’s strongest checking move, and state whether Black has a legal answer.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Qd8#`

**Short main line:** `1. Qd8#` ends the game immediately.

**Why it works:** The queen reaches the eighth rank and checks the king on g8 along the row. Black’s own rook on h8 and pawns on f7, g7, and h7 block the king’s escape squares, while the check cannot be blocked safely.

**Common mistake:** Playing a safe-looking queen move and forgetting to calculate the opponent’s own pieces as walls. Back-rank ideas often work because the king has fewer safe squares than it first appears.

</details>

## Position 3 — capture before comfort

Yahan queen visibly exposed lag rahi hai, aur White ke paas ek knight jump ka option hai. Tumhara task comfortable development nahi, balki immediate material aur king-safety gain ko compare karna hai.

![Phase 0 baseline position 03](/diagrams/chess/phase-0/phase-0-baseline-03.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `6kr/5q2/8/4N3/8/8/8/4KR2 w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 3 minutes. Find the knight move that wins material, and check whether the destination is protected.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Nxf7`

**Short main line:** `1. Nxf7` wins the queen; the rook on f1 protects the knight on f7.

**Why it works:** The knight attacks f7 from e5 and captures the black queen. Because the white rook controls f7, Black cannot answer by simply capturing the knight with the king, so the gain is decisive.

**Common mistake:** Seeing the capture but not checking the recapture square. A capture is useful only if the opponent cannot immediately win the capturing piece back.

</details>

## Position 4 — back-rank review

Yahan Black ka king apne pawns se surrounded hai, aur White ke rook ke paas clear eighth-rank line hai. Pehle rook ka route dekhna, phir king ke escape squares count karna hai.

![Phase 0 baseline position 04](/diagrams/chess/phase-0/phase-0-baseline-04.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/8/R5K1 w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 3 minutes. Find a rook move that uses Black’s own pawns as a wall.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Ra8#`

**Short main line:** `1. Ra8#` checks along the eighth rank and has no legal response.

**Why it works:** The rook travels up the open a-file to a8 and attacks the king on g8. The pawns on f7, g7, and h7 stop the king from stepping off the back rank, so the attack is mate.

**Common mistake:** Looking only at empty squares around the king. In real games a king can be trapped by its own pieces, which is exactly what makes a back-rank attack possible.

</details>

## Position 5 — answer a check

Yahan side to move Black hai aur White queen check de rahi hai. Is exercise mein best move ka matlab hai check ko immediately resolve karke apni best legal hope ko foran identify karna.

![Phase 0 baseline position 05](/diagrams/chess/phase-0/phase-0-baseline-05.svg)

**Side to move:** Black  
**FEN:** `7k/r5Q1/5K2/8/8/8/8/8 b - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 3 minutes. Find the forced response, and verify which pieces can legally reach the checking queen.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `...Rxg7`

**Short main line:** `...Rxg7` captures the checking queen along the seventh rank.

**Why it works:** The rook travels across the open rank and removes the check. The king cannot capture on g7 because the white king on f6 protects that square.

**Common mistake:** Trying `...Kxg7` and ignoring the protecting white king. A checking move is not automatically safe; after every capture, the opponent’s protection must be checked.

</details>

## Position 6 — finish the simple mate

Yeh position deliberately sparse hai taaki tum mate ke teen parts clearly dekh sako: check de raha hai, escape squares block kar raha hai, aur capture ke baad queen ko protect kar raha hai. Move likhne ke baad har king square mentally check karo.

![Phase 0 baseline position 06](/diagrams/chess/phase-0/phase-0-baseline-06.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `k7/8/1QK5/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 3 minutes. Find a queen move that gives checkmate in one.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Qb7#`

**Short main line:** `1. Qb7#` ends the game.

**Why it works:** The queen on b7 checks the king on a8 and covers the escape squares a7 and b8. Black cannot capture on b7 because the white king on c6 protects the queen.

**Common mistake:** Calling any queen check a mate without checking the king’s other squares. Mate requires that every king escape, block, and capture has been answered.

</details>

## Phase readiness checkpoint — baseline quick read

Ab jab sab positions attempt ho gaye hon, har attempt par 0, 1, ya 2 points rakho: 0 matlab move maine nahi dekha, 1 matlab move galat tha lekin baad mein reason samajh aaya, aur 2 matlab move aur reason dono the. Yeh score judgment nahi hai; yeh sirf batata hai ki aage kis family ke drills ko time dena hai.

| Score | Reading | Next action |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–4 | Safety, capture, ya check recognition mein gap | Board-scan warm-up roz karein aur weak pattern ke 3 positions revisit karein |
| 5–8 | Beginner ke liye expected mixed baseline | 0.3 routine maintain karein aur har game ka ek repeated mistake note karein |
| 9–12 | Tactical awareness ki achhi shuruaat | Phase 1 mein patterns ko jaldi identify karke practical games mein speed badhayein |

Agar kisi position mein legal movement ya king safety ki basic uncertainty hai, to piece-movement basics ko yahin dobara revisit kar sakte ho; is course un beginner lessons ko repeat nahi karega. Uske baad bhi in baseline positions ke attempts aur Phase 0 quiz ke concepts ko notes aur practice se reinforce karna hai, kyunki baseline tabhi useful hota hai jab tum decisions ko measurable habits se compare karte ho.

## What It Is NOT

Baseline ek pass-fail exam nahi hai jo tumhe chess se “achha” ya “bura” prove karta hai. Yeh sirf starting snapshot hai, isliye score ko future rating ka prediction nahi samajhna chahiye.

Engine ka answer dekh kar pehla attempt banana bhi baseline nahi hai. Agar answer pehle se pata ho, to pattern recognition nahi, memory test hoga; exactly isliye hum answer ko har position ke end mein hide karte hain.
