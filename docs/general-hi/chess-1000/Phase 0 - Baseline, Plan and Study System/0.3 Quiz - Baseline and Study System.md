# 0.3 Quiz - Baseline and Study System
## MCQ

Q: Lichess par `10+0` rapid game mein starting time aur increment ka matlab kya hai?
A. Har player ko 10 minutes milte hain aur har move ke baad 1 second increment
B. Har player ko 10 minutes milte hain aur koi extra increment nahi hota
C. 10 moves ke baad game 0 seconds par khatam hota hai
D. Dono players ko 10 minutes milte hain aur har move 0 seconds mein hota hai
> B. `10+0` mein har player ko shuru mein 10 minutes milte hain aur har move ke baad koi extra second nahi judta. Isliye time management aur move quality dono important hain.

---

Q: Phase 0 baseline test ka primary purpose kya hai?
A. Tumhe permanently strong ya weak player prove karna
B. Ek fixed rating predict karna jo har future game par apply ho
C. Starting strengths aur repeated gaps observe karna
D. Saare opening variations ratna
> C. Baseline ek starting snapshot hai jo dikhata hai checks, captures, safety, aur patterns mein abhi kya strong hai aur kahan practice chahiye. Yeh rating forecast nahi hai.

---

Q: Game log ka sabse useful entry kaunsa hai?
A. Sirf final result
B. Sirf opponent ka naam
C. Ek critical moment, uska reason, aur next action
D. Har move ka 500-word paragraph
> C. Ek critical moment, uska reason, aur next action se log future practice ke liye actionable feedback banta hai. Sirf result par focus tumhe repeated mistake identify karne nahi deta.

---

Q: 45-minute daily cadence ka balanced target kya hai?
A. Sirf 45 minutes games, koi study nahi
B. Board scan, focused tactics, practical games, aur short review/log
C. Sirf opening book padhna
D. 45 minutes engine analysis aur koi game nahi
> B. 45-minute routine mein small scan, focused tactics, practical games, aur review ka sequence hota hai. Isse theory real decisions se connect hoti hai aur daily feedback banta hai.

---

## TrueFalse

Q: 1000 rating ka target follow karna har learner ke liye guaranteed result hai.
- True
- False
> False. 1000 ek direction hai, guarantee nahi. Rating opponents, consistency, time, aur review quality par depend karti hai, isliye roadmap measurable habits aur honest rating expectations use karta hai.

---

Q: FEN ek compact notation hai jo chess position aur side-to-move ko represent kar sakti hai.
- True
- False
> True. FEN, ya Forsyth–Edwards Notation, board ke pieces aur side to move ko text form mein record karta hai. Is course mein ise exercise position ke exact text record ki tarah use kiya gaya hai.

## ShortAnswer

Q: Baseline ko future rating improvement ke liye useful kaise banate ho?

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Sample Answer:** Baseline ko ek fixed judgement nahi, balki starting snapshot ki tarah dekhta hoon. Main har baseline position ka attempt aur reason note karta hoon, phir 0.3 routine ke game logs se compare karta hoon. Jahan repeated safety, capture, ya check gaps dikhein, unhe next week ke focused drills aur review actions mein badal deta hoon.

</details>

Q: Ek losing game ke baad useful game-log entry kaise likhoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Sample Answer:** Main result ke saath ek critical position choose karunga, jaise “12th move par maine queen ko free piece diya.” Phir likhunga ki opponent ka recapture kahan available tha aur maine kyun miss kiya. Next action hoga “har capture se pehle opponent ke replies count karunga,” taaki entry blame nahi balki measurable habit target banaye.

</details>
