Driving test ko aap sirf ek lucky parking maneuver se pass nahi maante. # 7.3 Ten-Game Capstone

**Capstone** ka matlab hai course ke tools ko ek final, integrated assignment mein use karna. Is project mein aap serial 10 comparable rated Lichess rapid 10+0 games khelenge, har game ki quality aur metrics record karenge, aur last mein written review se decide karenge ki aap around 1000 ke liye ready ho ya ek aur block chahiye.

Capstone ko “last exam” ki tarah stressful banayenge bina practical banaye, isliye iska format khud clear rahega. Games realistic honge, selection fair hogi, aur aap losses ko review input ki tarah handle karenge. Koi bhi result ko delete karke sample cherry-pick nahi karna; poora record readiness evidence hoga.

## Capstone ka pre-commitment

Shuru hone se pehle 10 game numbers aur realistic dates note kijiye. Har game ke liye same fields rakhiye: date, rating snapshot, opponent rating, result, time-trouble flag, primary error, written review, aur next cue. Aap ek column mein “what I practised” bhi rakh sakte hain, taaki improvement ka source trace ho.

Agar tournament mein khelna hai toh `6.5 Tournament Routine and Recovery` mein check kiye suitable rated event ko select kijiye. Agar online queue khelni hai toh practical time control aur result context choose kijiye. Opponent/result-pool gaming se bachein; capstone ko fair training sample banana hai, rating farming nahi.

## Har game ke liye five-step protocol

Game shuru karne se pehle ek primary focus aur ek clock cue likhiye. Focus tactical clarity, king safety, piece activity, conversion, ya time management ho sakta hai. Phir game ke dauran realistic decisions lo aur har move ka live commentary mat likhne ki koshish karo; board ki attention aur clock routine test hone dene do.

Game ke baad apna move aur decisive moment likho, tab engine kholo. Aap `7.1` aur `7.2` ke workflow ko repeat karoge: write your move before checking engine, compare alternatives, find the decisive moment, classify the lesson, aur turn it into a small next-week assignment. Ek capstone game ka output sirf result nahi, yeh complete review evidence hai.

Har game ke baad recovery bhi record kijiye. Ek paani break, short walk, aur next-game checklist ka evidence aapke sustained routine ko samjhaane mein madad karta hai. Har loss ke baad ek actionable lesson aur har win ke baad ek realistic next focus rakhiye.

## Capstone ke written review

Har game ke baad aap ek paragraph likhein: situation, apna move, missed alternative, primary error category, aur next cue. Har paanch game ke baad midpoint summary mein count kijiye ki time trouble kitni games mein hua, reviewed decisions kitne hue, aur kaunsa trigger sabse zyada repeat hua. Aap block ko secretly reset nahi karenge; missing review ko bhi note karenge.

Block khatam hone par 20-game combined sample ko dekhiye. Training block ke 10 aur capstone ke 10 comparable rated games ko saath dekhna zaroori nahi hai, lekin aapki honest readiness evidence ko kam se kam 20 rated games par based hona chahiye. Agar kuch games unrated rahe, toh readiness claim ko abhi “enough evidence” nahi kehna chahiye.

## 1000 readiness ka definition

Yahan success ko ek lucky rating peak nahi maana jaata. Aapka goal **kam se kam 20 comparable rated 10+0 games mein 1000 ke aas-paas stable performance** dikhana hai, saath mein practical tactics, time management aur review quality. “Stable” ka matlab har game identical hona nahi, balki range aur decision quality ek realistic band mein rehna hai.

1000 target honestly useful hai, lekin **rating cannot be guaranteed** kisi plan, engine, tournament, ya course se. Opponent strength, time controls, pool composition, form aur random factors result ko affect karte hain. Capstone ka fayda yeh hai ki aap apni preparation ka evidence banate ho, outcome ko control karne ka false promise nahi.

## Readiness decision ke teen options

Block ke baad aap in teen honest labels mein se ek choose kar sakte ho:

- **Continue around 1000:** 20-game sample stable hai, common errors manageable hain, aur review routine continue karna beneficial hai.
- **Repeat the 10-game block:** Stability abhi nahi hai, time-trouble rate high hai, ya written reviews regular nahi hain; phir targeted block dobara kijiye.
- **Slow down and rebuild:** Tactics, king safety, ya endgames mein foundational gaps hain; 45-minute plan ko smaller fundamentals aur review goals ke saath adjust kijiye.

Decision ko rating ke ek number se nahi, apni written review aur future assignment se justify kijiye. Agar data mixed hai, toh “repeat” honest aur useful answer hai. Iska matlab failure nahi, balki next experiment ko clearer banana hai.

## What It Is NOT

Capstone **guaranteed rating event nahi hai**. Iska fayda fair, comparable sample aur decision trace hai; koi bhi result aapko 1000 ka promise nahi kar sakta.

Capstone **sirf best games dikhane ki presentation nahi hai**. Losses, time-trouble games, aur incomplete reviews bhi dataset mein honest rahein. Cherry-picking se self-assessment behtar dikhta hai, lekin decision improvement nahi hoti.

Capstone **rating ko personality measure nahi karta**. Aap apni 20-game sample se planning skill seekh sakte ho, lekin worth ya ability ko ek number se define karna healthy nahi. Data ko next action ke liye use kijiye.

Ab capstone ke data ke saath `7.4 Final Review aur Next Plan` mein final review aur clear next-step plan banayenge. Yahan course content khatam hoga, lekin measurable chess practice nahi hogi.
