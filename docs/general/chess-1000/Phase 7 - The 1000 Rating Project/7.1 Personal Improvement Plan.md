Fitness plan banane par trainer aapko sirf “roz gym jao” nahi kehta. Woh weekly target, recovery aur measurement decide karta hai, taaki aapko result dikhe aur next week plan adjust ho sake. Chess improvement bhi isi tarah kaam karta hai: apne 45 minutes ke schedule ko measurable actions aur honest review se connect kijiye, sirf motivation se nahi.

`6.6 Exercise Pack` mein aapne six decisions par apna move likhna, alternatives compare karna aur error list ko next-week assignment mein badalna practice kiya. Ab hum un tools ko 10 weeks ke personal improvement plan mein arrange karenge, jismein 10-game training block aur uske baad 10-game capstone bhi aa rahe hain.

# 7.1 Personal Improvement Plan

**Personal improvement plan** ka matlab hai aapke liye likha hua schedule, metrics aur feedback loop. Ye sirf “main 1000 banunga” ka wish nahi hai; ismein yeh bhi hai ki aap 45 minutes mein kya karoge, kis game ko review karoge, aur kaunsa result aapke liye genuinely improvement ka evidence hai.

Target ko seedha “Lichess rapid 10+0 ke aas-paas 1000” rakhiye, lekin is target ko guarantee ki tarah mat samjhiye. Rating opponents, pool, form, time pressure aur luck se badalti hai. Isliye honest success ka matlab hai **kam se kam 20 comparable rated games mein 1000 ke aas-paas stable performance**, ek lucky peak nahi.

## Pehle baseline set karo

Aap apne current level ko ek baseline ki tarah dekhiye. Pichhle 10 rated 10+0 games ke notes dekhein, ya agar itne nahi hain toh agle 10 games baseline block ki tarah record karein. Har game mein rating snapshot, result, time-trouble status, apna dominant error aur review completion note kijiye.

Baseline ka purpose aapko koi final number batana nahi hai. Iska kaam yeh dekhna hai ki aapka time kis phase mein khatam hota hai, kaunsa error repeat hota hai, aur aap kitni der tak ek complete written review karte ho. Jaise doctor weight nahi, baseline habits bhi comparison ka starting point hain.

**Stable performance** ka matlab ek hi game mein temporary rating jump nahi hai. Aap multiple comparable rated games mein aas-paas 1000 ke snapshots, sensible results, aur similar mistakes ke saath competent decisions dikha rahe ho. Ye evidence rating ka guarantee nahi, lekin preparation ki honest readiness hai.

## 10-week structure

Plan ko teen hisson mein soch kar rakhiye: foundation, live integration, aur final consistency check. Har week ka output ek observable product hoga, jaise reviewed games, filled log, ya tactical checkpoint, taaki sirf pages read karke plan complete na lage.

| Week | Main focus | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Baseline, piece activity, checks and captures | 10-game log plus recurring-error list |
| 3–4 | Common tactics and simple opening plans | Written before/after move comparisons |
| 5–6 | King safety, pawn breaks, and piece coordination | Five fully reviewed decision points |
| 7–8 | Endgames and 10+0 clock routines | Two clock-pattern reviews and conversion practice |
| 9 | Ten-game training block | Complete ten-game block with review evidence |
| 10 | Ten-game capstone and readiness decision | Twenty-game sample, final review, next plan |

Table ko ek timetable ki tarah use kijiye, ek punishment ki tarah nahi. Agar kisi week koi extra loss aa jaaye, toh agle week ke checkpoint par focus kijiye aur plan ko quietly realistic bana lijiye. Aapki progress ko sirf weekly rating change se na naapiye.

## Din ka 45-minute template

Aapke 45-minute session ko flexible blocks mein rakh sakte ho. Ek practical starting point 10 minutes recall, 20 minutes practice, 10 minutes review, aur 5 minutes logging hai; agar tournament ho toh isme se kuch time actual game ko mile.

Recall phase mein purane error triggers aur ek tactical theme dobara dekhiye. Practice phase mein puzzles, opening exploration, endgames ya Lichess games use kijiye, lekin har activity ka ek purpose note kijiye. Review phase mein apna move engine se pehle likhiye, aur last five minutes mein next assignment choose kijiye.

Aap daily rating target nahi set karenge. Daily target evidence hai: ek question solve karna, ek game review karna, ya ek trigger ko count karna. In small completions ka network hi 10 weeks mein repeatable improvement banata hai.

## Metrics ko fair tarike se track karo

Har game ke liye paanch fields rakhiye: rating snapshot, result, time-trouble flag, primary lesson, aur review done ya nahi. Inke saath weekly ek `accuracy of review` note kijiye, jisme aap likhte ho ki decisive moment ko kitni der baad dhoondha aur alternative board par khud check kiya ya nahi.

Rating ko alag column mein rakh kar overall progress ko fair rakhiye. Ek block ka score, opponent pool, time control aur rating change ko ek saath nahi dekhna chahiye. Metrics ka maqsad aapko har din compare karna nahi, over several weeks a realistic pattern dekhna hai.

Har week ek line mein likhiye: “is week meri sabse common mistake ___ thi, next week main ___ trigger check karunga.” Jab 10 weeks ke notes ek saath padhein, toh aapko dikhega kaunsa pattern sach mein repeat ho raha hai aur kaunsa sirf ek game ki kahani tha.

## Plan ko apne schedule se match karo

Agar aapke paas fixed 45 minutes nahi hain, toh plan ko honest realistic version mein adjust kijiye. Chhoti consistent session bhi long interrupted “intensive” session se better ho sakti hai, kyunki review ka loop complete hoga. Aap har week missing time note karke next assignment adjust kar sakte ho.

Rating ko daily identity mat banaiye. Aapka plan board decisions, time routines aur review completion ko measure karta hai; rating uska downstream outcome hai. Is distinction se aap har loss ke baad plan todne se bachenge aur har win ko overcelebrate karne se bhi.

## Agle hafte ka kaam

Agle saat din mein aap apna 10-game baseline log start kijiye. Har game ke baad 10–15 minute mein result, time-trouble status, primary error aur next cue likhiye; engine se deep review teesre din ya phir usi routine ke andar kijiye. Chhahon se zyada fields add karne se bhi practical na ho, simplicity rakhiye.

Week ke end par 45 minutes ka review kijiye: kaunsi field mein pattern dikha, kaunsi line kab trigger hui, aur agle week ka observable task kya hai. Yehi `7.2 Ten-Game Training Block` ka starting material banega.

## What It Is NOT

Personal plan **rating guarantee nahi hai**. 1000 ek useful target hai, lekin results ko aap completely control nahi karte; plan aapki decisions ko improve karta hai, outcome ko guarantee nahi.

Personal plan **har din 10+0 rated game khelne ka bahana nahi hai**. Kuch din review aur practice ke liye honge, aur yeh progress ke liye zaroori bhi hain. Har game se pehle purpose likhna consistency ko random queueing se bachata hai.

Personal plan **sirf ek number ko chase karna nahi hai**. Ek lucky peak rating ko stability samajhna galat hoga; multiple comparable games, decision traces, aur recurring-error change zyada reliable evidence hain.

Ab schedule clear hai. Agli lesson mein aap is plan ke andar mandatory ten-game training block chalayenge, aur har game ko review evidence ke saath track karenge.
