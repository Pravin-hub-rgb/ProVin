# Quiz - Rating Readiness

## MCQ

Q: Is course ke rating target ko honest kaise define karna chahiye?
A. Ek game mein 1000 dekh liya to guaranteed progress
B. Kam se kam 20 comparable rated games mein 1000 ke aas-paas stable performance, rating guarantee nahi
C. Sirf tournament jeetne par target complete
D. Sirf engine evaluation average
> B. Stable performance multiple comparable games aur decision evidence ka pattern hai. Koi bhi plan rating guarantee nahi kar sakta.

---

Q: Ten-game training block aur ten-game capstone mein capstone ka extra focus kya hai?
A. Sirf total score badhana
B. Integrated protocol, ten-game tracked sample, written review aur final readiness decision
C. Engine ka number copy karna
D. Sabse weak opponents choose karna
> B. Capstone tools ko real, comparable games mein integrate karke documented evidence collect karta hai. Fair selection aur honest review uska hissa hain.

---

Q: Har reviewed game ka minimum useful written record kya hai?
A. Sirf result
B. Sirf opponent rating
C. FEN, apna move aur reason, forcing reply, alternative aur next cue
D. Sirf engine percentage
> C. Minimum record problem, thought, comparison aur next action ko connect karta hai. Isse review future practice mein use hota hai.

---

Q: Final readiness decision mein repeat block kab choose karna chahiye?
A. Jab ek lucky rating peak mile
B. Jab stability, time routine ya review evidence abhi weak ho aur targeted block improvement de sakta ho
C. Jab koi game improve na ho
D. Jab engine ka number perfect na ho
> B. Repeat tab meaningful hai jab data ek targeted improvement gap dikhaye aur plan ko us gap par adjust kiya ja sake. Ek unlucky result automatic reason nahi hai.

---

## TrueFalse

Q: 10-game training block ko complete karna sirf 10 games khelne ke barabar hai, review ke bina bhi poori tarah useful hai.
- True
- False
> False. Block ka learning output har game ke focused review aur written assignment se aata hai. Bina review ke volume badh sakta hai, par decision quality ke reliable evidence kam hote hain.

---

Q: Rating readiness ka honest basis ek lucky peak se zyada 20 comparable rated games mein stable performance aur written review hai.
- True
- False
> True. Multiple comparable games aur documented decisions broader evidence dete hain. Ye bhi rating ko guarantee nahi karta, lekin readiness ka fairer assessment hai.

## ShortAnswer

Q: Ten-game capstone aur final review mein aap kaunse metrics track karoge, aur readiness label kaise choose karoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Sample Answer:** Main har game ka rating snapshot, opponent rating, result, time-trouble flag, primary error, written review status, aur next cue track karunga. Final review mein 20 comparable rated games ke snapshots, time-trouble pattern, recurring error categories, aur review quality dekh kar `continue around 1000`, `repeat the 10-game block`, ya `slow down and rebuild` choose karunga. Choice ko observed evidence se justify karunga, aur rating cannot be guaranteed yaad rakhunga.

</details>

---

Q: Course ke baad agle 4 hafte ka realistic chess plan explain kijiye.

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Sample Answer:** Main 10 aur comparable rated 10+0 games khelunga, har game ke baad FEN, apna move, alternative, primary lesson aur next cue likhunga. Har paanch game ke baad 45-minute midpoint review karunga, common trigger ko target karunga, suitable rated events choose karunga, aur fair opponents ke saath khelunga. Daily 45-minute routine mein warm-up, focused practice, game ya position work, review aur logging fit karunga; goal stable performance ka evidence banana hai, rating ka false promise nahi.

</details>
