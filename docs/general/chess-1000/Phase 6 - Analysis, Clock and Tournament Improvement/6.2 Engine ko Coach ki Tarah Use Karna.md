Jab aap gym video dekhte ho toh trainer sirf “haath thoda seedha rakho” bolkar aapko hundreds of reps nahi karwata. Woh aapki current movement ka ek important signal batata hai, aur aap us signal ko apne next set mein apply karte ho. Chess engine bhi aise hi kaam karta hai, lekin sirf tab jab aap usse apni soch ka coach banate ho, apni choice ki jagah nahi.

`6.1 Game Analysis Without Engine` mein humne seekha tha ki apna move engine se pehle likhna, alternatives compare karna aur decisive moment dhoondhna zaroori hai. Ab bharose ko add karna hai, lekin bharosa tabhi useful hai jab usse apni soch ke saath compare karo. Isi liye is lesson mein hum Lichess par coach jaisa review karenge.

# 6.2 Engine ko Coach ki Tarah Use Karna

**Chess engine** ek computer program hai jo bohot saari legal moves dekh kar position ko evaluate karta hai. **Evaluation** engine ka qualitative ya numerical judge hota hai; is chapter mein hum exact number ko truth nahi maanenge, sirf is baat ko samjhenge ki side ke liye position achhi hai ya kharab.

Engine ko Google jaisa samajhna bhi helpful hai. Aap ek sawaal poochte ho, kai answers milte hain, lekin aap apne experience se decide karte ho ki kaunsa answer situation mein fit hai. Chess mein engine line dikhata hai, par aapki tactical aim, practical understanding aur board par maujood position decide karti hai ki us line ko follow karna sahi hai.

## Engine ko call karne se pehle

Lichess mein aap apna game analysis ya Study view mein khol sakte hain aur computer analysis add kar sakte hain. Exact button ka naam badh sakta hai, isliye main interface ka screenshot nahi, kaam ka order sikhayega.

1. Recent game kholiye aur ek important position par pause kijiye.
2. FEN aur apna move board par likh kar position ko restart se dobara check kijiye.
3. Apne liye do candidate lines likhiye, sirf engine ki best line nahi.
4. Ab engine analysis chalu kijiye.
5. Engine ki recommended line, aapke candidate lines aur opponent ke forcing replies ko compare kijiye.
6. Ek line select karne se pehle reason likhiye: “main yeh isliye chahunga kyunki…”

Aap Lichess ka analysis speed, **depth** control, ya multiple lines feature use kar sakte ho. **Depth** ka matlab hai engine ne kitne aage ke moves tak calculation dekhi; zyada depth usually zyada calculation hai, lekin har position mein depth badhana zaroori nahi. Aapka goal top line copy karna nahi, apni thought process ko readable banana hai.

## PV ko line ki tarah padho

Engine report mein **PV**, ya **principal variation**, dikhata hai. Iska matlab hai engine ki preferred sequence, jaise ek pehla move aur uske baad ke replies. PV ko ek aisi recipe samajh sakte ho jo ek particular position mein achhi lagi thi; agle board par wahi recipe guaranteed nahi hoti.

Jab aap PV padhein toh teen sawaal poochhiye. Pehla: kaunsi move thi jab position ki quality actually badni shuru hui? Doosra: engine ne kaunsi forcing line ignore karne ki ijaazat di, aur kyun? Teesra: aapka weaker player ya practical kya tha jo maine miss kiya?

Engine evaluation badhne par aapko foran “main galat tha” mat kehna hai. Evaluation change hona useful signal hai, par context bhi matter karta hai. Agar aapne low-clock mein best theoretical move chuna aur game haar gaya, toh lesson “better decision nahi, time aur practicality par focus” ho sakta hai.

## Alternative ko samajhna, blindly copy karna nahi

Jab aap apna candidate move engine ke best move se alag rakhte ho, toh pehle compare karo ki dono moves ka immediate consequence kya hai. Kya aapka move material bachata hai? Kya opponent ko forcing check deta hai? Kya aapne piece activity improve ki ya sirf ek empty square ko target bana liya?

Lichess review mein aap apne move par `+/-` jaise arrows ya circles nahi nikal sakte, lekin notes mein arrow jaisa shorthand likh sakte ho: “e5→d7, recapture force.” Iska fayda yeh hai ki aap ka baad mein apni choice ko hi explain kar paate ho. Ek line ko blindly copy karna training nahi, sirf transcription hai.

## Coach note kaise banaiye

Har important review ko aise four lines mein compress kijiye:

- **Situation:** Position ka type, jaise hanging piece, open king ya bad pawn structure.
- **My thought:** Aapne kyun woh move choose kiya, bina engine ke.
- **Coach point:** Alternative ne kaunsa threat ya resource miss karwaya.
- **Next cue:** Agle games mein kaunsa observable reminder use karoge.

Maan lijiye aapne aap ka rook b7 mein rakh diya aur opponent ne use dushri taraf se attack kiya. Coach note ka useful version sirf “rook b7 galat tha” nahi hai; useful version hai “open file ko dekh kar rook ko pehle safety ya trade ke liye move karunga, ya immediate tactic calculate karunga.” Is tarah note agle decision ko badal sakta hai.

## 45-minute practical routine

Aap 10 minute apna independent review kar sakte ho, 15 minute do candidate branches calculate kar sakte ho, aur 15 minute engine ke comparison se coach note bana sakte ho. Baki 5 minute ko next assignment decide karne ke liye rakhiye, isliye session board par close hone ke baad bhi actionable ho.

Ek hi game ko teen baar analyse karna helpful ho sakta hai, lekin ek din mein das games ke superficial percent bars dekhna nahi. Aapko apna thought, alternative aur trigger likhna hai; engine ka green number dekhna secondary hai.

## What It Is NOT

Engine **apni soch ka replacement nahi hai**. Woh position ka strong signal deta hai, lekin aapko explain karna hota hai ki signal kahan se aaya aur real game mein kya practical meaning rakhta hai.

Engine **har position ka human samajh nahi hai**. Opening lines, time trouble, practical piece activity aur opponent ki psychology ko woh poori tarah capture nahi karta. Isliye “engine ne best kaha” hona ek reason hai, final sentence nahi.

Engine **har disagreement ko blunder proof nahi karta**. Evaluation change ka magnitude context ke bina judge mat kijiye; ek queen sac aur ek quiet pawn move ke red arrows ka matlab alag ho sakta hai.

Ab engine aapko mistake ka naam de sakta hai, lekin improvement ka kaam woh hai ki aap woh mistake ki **category** aur pehle se warning sign samjho. Agli lesson mein hum recurring blunders ko fix karne ke liye ek practical routine banayenge.
