# Quiz - Analysis, Clock and Tournament Improvement

## MCQ

Q: Apni game ke important position par engine se pehle kya karna chahiye?
A. Engine ki best line ko turant copy karna
B. Apna candidate move aur uski reason FEN ke saath likhna
C. Sirf final result dekh kar agli game start karna
D. Har possible line solve karte rehna
> B. Apna candidate move aur reason likhne se aapki calculation test hoti hai. Engine aapki thought ka feedback deta hai, uske aage ki shortcut nahi.

---

Q: Game analysis mein decisive moment kya hota hai?
A. Woh move jo hamesha sabse lambi calculation maangta hai
B. Woh position jahan game ka direction clearly badalne laga ho
C. Lichess ka opening mein pehla move
D. Har move jisme engine evaluation badle
> B. Decisive moment woh point hai jahan position ka result, plan ya practical direction materially badalne lagta hai. Har evaluation change equal nahi hota.

---

Q: Lichess rapid 10+0 mein “10+0” ka matlab kya hai?
A. Har move par 10 minute increment
B. Har player ko 10 minute total, fixed increment ke bina
C. Har game mein 10 bonus second
D. 10 minute mein 10 games
> B. Har player ke paas starting clock mein 10 minutes hote hain aur fixed increment nahi hota. Total decision time game ke saare moves mein distribute hota hai.

---

Q: Engine ko coach ki tarah use karne ka best approach kya hai?
A. Har disagreement ko engine ki galti maanna
B. Sirf engine ka exact number yaad rakhna
C. Apna reasoning, PV aur practical alternatives compare karna
D. Har position par engine ka move bina soche follow karna
> C. Apna reasoning aur alternatives likh kar engine ke PV se compare karna feedback ko learning mein badalta hai. Engine top move ko blind copy karna thought development nahi hai.

---

## TrueFalse

Q: Lichess mein engine ki best line dekhne ke baad aapko apna pehle ka move analyze karne ki zaroorat nahi.
- True
- False
> False. Pehle apna move aur reason likhna zaroori hai, warna aap calculation develop nahi karenge. Engine ka kaam compare karke signal dena hai, thought ko replace karna nahi.

---

Q: Suitable rated event ko time control, duration, pool, rated status aur realistic learning value ke saath check karna ek practical choice hai.
- True
- False
> True. Time control, expected duration, pool, rated status aur analysis ke liye available time ek saath dekhna chahiye. Fair, realistic learning value event ko easy points se zyada important banati hai.

## ShortAnswer

Q: Apni game ka decisive moment dhoondhne aur next-week assignment banane ka apna workflow samjhaaiye.

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Sample Answer:** Main Lichess par recent game dheere replay karke pause karunga, important position ka FEN likhunga, aur engine kholne se pehle apna candidate move, likely forcing reply aur alternative note karunga. Phir engine se compare karke quality difference samjhunga, mistake ko tactical, strategic, structure, king-safety ya time category mein rakhunga. Us category se ek chhota observable assignment banaunga, jaise agle teen games mein loose piece ke defenders count karna.

Q: 10+0 clock aur tournament recovery ke do practical habits explain kijiye.

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Sample Answer:** 10+0 mein main fixed seconds-per-move rule nahi maanunga; opening, middlegame aur endgame ke liye alag stage goals rakhunga, immediate threats ko priority dunga, aur simple plan ke saath time manage karunga. Tournament ke baad result ko context dunga, hydration aur short break lekar next round ke liye attention reset karunga, phir loss se ek ya do actionable lessons likhunga. Har round ke baad results check karke realistic rated events chununga aur opponent/result-pool gaming se bhaagunga.

</details>
