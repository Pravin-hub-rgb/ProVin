Kabhi practice cricket mein aapko sirf ek shot practice nahi milti; aap multiple shots ke beech rotate karte ho, taaki ek moment ki success poori game ki readiness na samjha jaaye. Yahan bhi hum chess ke ek decision ko baar-baar solve karne ke bajaye six alag decision points de rahe hain. Har position ko apni game ke jaisa treat kijiye: pehle apna move soch kar likhiye, phir chhupa hua answer dekhiye.

`6.5 Tournament Routine and Recovery` mein aapne seekha tha ki real games ke baad pause, result context aur honest loss review zaroori hain. Ye exercise pack us routine ko ek safe training space deta hai, jahan galti karna part of practice hai aur har answer ke saath ek next-week lesson milta hai.

# 6.6 Exercise Pack

**Exercise pack** ka matlab hai chhote, focused positions par deliberate practice, random moves karke score badhane ke bajaye. Yahan ki positions constructed training positions hain, lekin har prompt aapse apni game ke decision point ko replay karne ko kahta hai. Aapko har answer perfect nahi chahiye; apni reasoning ko compare karna hai.

Har position ke liye is order ko follow kijiye: FEN padhiye, side to move confirm kijiye, apna move board par khel kar likhiye, phir opponent ki best response soch kar ek alternative note kijiye, aur sabse aakhir mein details kholiye. **Write your move before checking engine** yahan central rule hai, kyunke pehla purpose answer copy karna nahi, apna decision process dekhna hai.

## 1. Safe squares aur back-rank mate

![White-to-move back-rank decision point](/diagrams/chess/phase-6/phase-6-analysis-01.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/8/R5K1 w - - 0 1`

Is position ko aise imagine kijiye jaise aapke opponent ke pawns king ke aage aa gaye hon aur aapka rook ek file free kar raha ho. **Attempt task:** apna move likhiye, phir batayiye ki escape squares kaise close hote hain aur kaunsi line se rook deliver hota hai.

<details>
<summary>Show best move and lesson</summary>

**Best move/continuation:** `Ra8#`. Rook a-file par a8 tak jaakar back rank par check deta hai; f8 aur h8 rook se attacked hain, jabki f7, g7 aur h7 par Black ke apne pawns escape block kar rahe hain. Black ke paas is check ko interpose karne ka legal move nahi hai.

**Engine-style evaluation reasoning:** Qualitative engine reading is “forced mate for White.” Exact number ki zaroorat nahi; decisive signal rook ka rank delivery aur Black ke blocked escape squares hain. Aapko apne move se pehle opponent ka back-rank idea calculate karna tha.

**Lesson to add to the error list:** “Open file se rook move karne se pehle opponent ke king ke escape squares check karo.”

</details>

## 2. Rook geometry aur false mate claim

![Black-to-move rook-geometry decision point](/diagrams/chess/phase-6/phase-6-analysis-02.svg)

**Side to move:** Black  
**FEN:** `r5k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/5PPP/4R1K1 b - - 0 1`

Black ke rook ne a1 jaane ka tempting idea de sakta hai, lekin White ka e1 rook check line ko block karta hai. **Attempt task:** ek legal move choose kijiye aur aage ke capture ko verify kijiye—rook ka path clear hai, destination par capture hai, aur blocker check line ko rok raha hai ya nahi?

<details>
<summary>Show practical move and lesson</summary>

**Practical legal move:** `Kf8`. Black king g8 se f8 par safely ja sakta hai; ye check ya checkmate nahi hai. Rook a8 diagonal move nahi kar sakta, aur `Ra1` ke baad bhi e1 par White ka rook first-rank line block karta hai, isliye is position mein koi immediate Black mate nahi hai.

**Engine-style evaluation reasoning:** Qualitative reading is “equal practical move; no immediate Black check.” Aapko exact evaluation number par nahi, rook geometry, blocker aur SAN notation verify karna tha. Capturing ya mating move ka label lagane se pehle destination aur check line dono dekhne the.

**Lesson to add to the error list:** “Capture ya mate ka label lagane se pehle rook ka legal path, destination par piece aur check ke blockers verify karo.”

</details>

## 3. Queen ke liye open line banana

![White-to-move queen back-rank decision point](/diagrams/chess/phase-6/phase-6-analysis-03.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/4QPPP/6K1 w - - 0 1`

Yahan aapke paas queen hai, lekin queen ka best move board ke left side par nahi, seedhe rank par shift karke ho sakta hai. **Attempt task:** queen ke possible checks ko line ke saath likhiye aur identify kijiye ki Black ke king ke paas kaunsa legal escape block ho gaya hai.

<details>
<summary>Show best move and lesson</summary>

**Best move/continuation:** `Qe8#`. Queen e2 se e8 tak vertical line par jaati hai aur g8 ke king ko rank par check karti hai. f8 aur h8 queen ki rank line se cover hain, aur f7, g7, h7 par Black ke pawns apne king ko escape nahi de rahe.

**Engine-style evaluation reasoning:** Qualitative engine-style reading is “direct mate once the open e-file is found.” Aapko calculation ki lambi list nahi, bas queen ke destination, rank control aur escape squares compare karne the. Is move ne bina piece capture kiye direct result banaya.

**Lesson to add to the error list:** “Open file par piece move karne se pehle queen aur rook ki rank/file delivery check karo.”

</details>

## 4. Undefended knight ko free capture

![White-to-move knight capture decision point](/diagrams/chess/phase-6/phase-6-analysis-04.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `r3k2r/ppp2ppp/8/3n4/8/2N5/PPP2PPP/R3K2R w KQkq - 0 1`

Yahan board par ek central knight khada hai, lekin aapko usse capture karne se pehle surrounding pieces ko bhi dekhna hai. **Attempt task:** `d5` knight ke saare capture sources likhiye, phir choose kijiye ki material grab karne ke baad piece ko kahan active rakhna hai.

<details>
<summary>Show best move and lesson</summary>

**Best move/continuation:** `Nxd5`, aur ek possible continuation `...Kf8` ke baad knight ko central aur protected activity par le jaana. Knight c3 se d5 aakar Black ke knight ko capture karta hai; d5 par target occupied hai aur move legal dikh raha hai. `Kf8` forced nahi hai, lekin yeh is position mein ek legal practical reply example hai.

**Engine-style evaluation reasoning:** Qualitative reading is “White wins material while keeping a central piece,” not a promise of immediate mate. Engine ko exact number par nahi, material gain aur piece activity ke trade-off par dekhna tha. Agar aap pawn structure ya king safety ignore karte, toh aapka comparison incomplete rahega.

**Lesson to add to the error list:** “Free material ko count karne se pehle capture ke baad piece activity aur counterattack note karo.”

</details>

## 5. King aur queen ka final conversion

![White-to-move king-and-queen conversion](/diagrams/chess/phase-6/phase-6-analysis-05.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `7k/5K2/6Q1/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1`

Ab board par kam pieces hain, isliye har square ka role badal jaata hai. **Attempt task:** queen ko Black ke king ke paas move karne ke options likhiye, aur apna preferred move se pehle opponent ke legal escape squares count kijiye.

<details>
<summary>Show best move and lesson</summary>

**Best move/continuation:** `Qg7#`. Queen g6 se g7 par jaakar h8 ke king ko diagonal check deti hai. h7 queen se covered hai, g8 par White king f7 control rakhta hai, aur Black ke paas check ko block karne ke liye koi piece nahi hai.

**Engine-style evaluation reasoning:** Qualitative engine-style reading is “queen and king produce a forced mate.” Exact evaluation number ki jagah king aur queen ke coordinated control ka pattern note kijiye. Position mein kam pieces hone ke bawajood coordination hi decisive thi.

**Lesson to add to the error list:** “Endgame mein piece count kam hone par king aur sliding piece ke coordination ko dobara calculate karo.”

</details>

## 6. Queen capture ko mate tak continue karna

![Black-to-move mating capture decision point](/diagrams/chess/phase-6/phase-6-analysis-06.svg)

**Side to move:** Black  
**FEN:** `q6k/8/8/8/8/8/5PPP/R5K1 b - - 0 1`

Aapke saamne ek attack mein aaya rook hai, lekin sirf capture karna kaafi nahi; capture ke baad king ka exit map bhi dekhiye. **Attempt task:** Black ke rook capture options likhiye, phir unke baad White king ke legal squares par ek line note kijiye.

<details>
<summary>Show best move and lesson</summary>

**Best move/continuation:** `Qxa1#`. Queen a8 se a1 tak jaakar White ke rook ko capture karti hai aur first rank par king g1 ko check deti hai. White ke paas koi interposing piece nahi hai, king ke f1 aur h1 squares queen ke line mein hain, aur f2, g2, h2 apne pawns se block hain.

**Engine-style evaluation reasoning:** Qualitative engine reading is “capture and checkmate is the clearest continuation.” Move ke material gain se pehle aapko rank control aur apne escape squares bhi count karne the. Yeh isliye zaroori hai kyunki ek simple capture sirf free-point distraction nahi, poora winning move bhi ho sakta hai.

**Lesson to add to the error list:** “Attacked piece capture karne se pehle rank/file control aur opponent ke king ke escape squares verify karo.”

</details>

## Readiness checkpoint

Ab details band karke chhahon positions dobara attempt kijiye, bina engine ke. Har position par aapko teen cheezein milni chahiye: apna pehla move, opponent ka forcing reply, aur ek chhota lesson jo aap apni error list mein add karenge.

Checkpoint pass hone ke practical signals yeh ho sakte hain:

- Chhahon mein se kam se kam chaar (at least four) positions par aap decisive moment ko capture ke bina samajh paaye.
- Har answer mein aapne board par apna move aur kam se kam ek alternative likha.
- Aapne exact evaluation number ke bajad qualitative reason bataya, jaise forced mate, material gain, ya safer king position.
- Aapne har position se ek specific next-game cue nikala, “better sochna” jaisa vague message nahi.
- Aap ek 10+0 routine mein yeh exercises finish karne ke baad ek rated game bhi khelne ko ready ho.

Teen se zyada positions par aap sirf engine ke answer ke bina koi direction nahi bana paye, toh itna mat samjhiye ki course fail ho gaya hai. Iska matlab hai tactical vocabulary aur pattern recognition ko thoda aur review chahiye; wahi information aapke next block ka input hai.

## What It Is NOT

Exercise pack **rating forecast nahi hai**. Chhahon positions mein best answers aapke tactical recall aur explanation ko measure karte hain, lekin aapke next opponents, time pressure, aur result ko predict nahi karte. Iska maqsad score ya six out of six par pressure dena nahi, balki ek repeatable decision loop practice karna hai.

Exercise pack **engine ke replacements nahi hain**. Aapka move likhna, forcing reply count karna, aur alternative compare karna zaroori steps hain; engine in steps ke baad feedback deta hai. Agar aap har position ka answer copy karke aage badhte hain, toh exercise ka learning result kam ho jaata hai.

Exercise pack **har real game ki poori history reconstruct karne ka kaam nahi karti**. Position ko apni game ke jaisa treat karna useful lens hai, lekin practice position ka ek deliberate example hai. Asli value source move ko error list mein convert karne mein hai, sirf position ke score mein nahi.

## Ek 45-minute pack session

Session ke pehle 15 minutes mein do positions attempt kijiye aur sirf apne moves note kijiye. Beech ke 15 minutes mein baaki chaar positions kijiye, aur last 15 minutes mein engine ke comparison se apni reason aur error list update kijiye. Engine ko attempt ke baad khologe, isliye exercise feedback banega, shortcut nahi.

Har position ka “loss ka full review” banana zaroori nahi hai. Tactical question ko concise rakh kar source position, missed question aur prevention cue likhna kaafi hai. Aapka goal six perfect answers nahi, ek repeatable decision loop banana hai.

Ab aapke paas Phase 6 ka drill set ready hai. Iske baad `6.6 Quiz - Analysis, Clock and Tournament Improvement` se apna understanding check kijiye. Uske baad `7.1 Personal Improvement Plan` mein aap is exercise routine ko 45-minute daily practice ke 10-week schedule mein convert karenge, phir 10-game training block aur 10-game capstone se apni readiness check karenge.
