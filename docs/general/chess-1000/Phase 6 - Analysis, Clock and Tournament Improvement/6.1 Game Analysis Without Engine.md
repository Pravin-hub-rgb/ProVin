Aapne cricket ka replay dekha hoga: scoreboard se yeh seedha pata nahi chalta ki kya hua tha, lekin slow-motion mein woh ek delivery dikhati hai jisne match ka rukhne wala moment bana diya. Chess mein bhi aisa hota hai. Aap moves dekh kar soch sakte hain “main galat kyun gaya?”, par asli kaam hota hai uss ek moment ko pehchanna jab board ka direction badal gaya.

`5.6 Quiz - Essential Endgames` mein aapne seekha tha ki king aur pawn ke basic ideas kaise decisive ho sakte hain. Par abhi ek important gap hai: woh ideas sirf quiz position mein pehchan lene se kuch nahi hota, agar aap unhe apni actual game mein dekh hi nahi paate. Isliye ab hum theory ko apne decisions se jodenge.

# 6.1 Game Analysis Without Engine

**Game analysis** ka matlab hai kisi finished game ko dobara dekhna aur samajhna ki position kaun banayi, kaunsi choice ne uska direction badla, aur agli baar aap kya alag kar sakte hain. Yeh live calculation sirf dohraane jaisa nahi hai. Aap slow speed mein kisi important move se pehle ruk kar sochte hain, kyunki tabhi aap apni reasoning ko dekhte hain.

Isi tarah ek GPS apni current location nahi batata; route ka record karke dikhata hai ki aapne junction miss kyun kiya. Aapka Lichess game record bhi aisa record hai. Is chapter ka aim hai ki aap har loss ko ek emotional sentence “main haar gaya” se badal kar ek chhota, repeatable decision task mein badal sakein.

## Pehle apna move likho

Analysis ka pehla rule hai: engine ko chhodo aur problem ko pehle apne shabdon mein solve karo. Har important position ke liye FEN, **Forsyth–Edwards Notation**, likho. FEN board ki arrangement, side to move, castling rights aur kuch aur zaroori information ko ek compact line mein sametata hai; aapko har baar manually sab squares bharni nahi padti.

Ab aap apna candidate move board par khelne se pehle likh dete hain. Sirf “maine `Nxd5` khela” likhna kaafi nahi; saath mein yeh bhi likho ki aapne kya calculate kiya, kya attack expect kiya, aur opponent ke best reply ke baad aapka plan kya tha. Is tarah engine aapke answer ko judge karta hai, aapke dimaag ko replace nahi karta.

Yeh order important hai. Agar engine hamesha aage khula rahe, toh aap uski best move ko copy karne lagoge aur apni soch kabhi develop nahi hogi. Jaise gym mein weight pehle se pata ho toh form badal jaata hai, waise hi aapka apna pehle ka move aapki training ka base banega.

## Lichess par apni game kaise padhein

Lichess ke game page par aap apna game khol sakte hain aur usse analysis ya study view mein le ja sakte hain. UI ka exact label badl sakta hai, par kaam wahi hai: apna game slowly replay karke jab `You` ka important decision aane wala ho, pause kijiye.

Iske baad ek chhota workflow follow kijiye:

1. Position ko FEN aur important pieces ke saath likhiye.
2. Apne aage wale 3–5 candidate moves soch kar unhe ek line mein note kijiye.
3. Board par apna preferred move khelein, lekin abhi calculation complete mat kijiye.
4. Position ko restart karke sirf apni calculation dobara dekhiye.
5. Opposition ke forcing reply, piece values, king safety aur pawn structure ke baare mein ek-ek line note kijiye.
6. Ab engine feedback dekhne ke liye pause kijiye.

Yeh sequence aapko “mistake ki gallery” banane se bachata hai. Aap har move ka commentary nahi likhenge, sirf woh positions samjhenge jahan decision aage badh sakta tha. Yahan **ply** ka matlab hai ek individual move, yaani ek player ka ek chess move. Aapka kaam har ply ko solve karna nahi hai; aapka kaam game ka **decisive moment**, yaani direction badalne wala moment, dhoondhna aur samajhna hai.

## Ek position ko teen notebook lines mein padhna

Maan lijiye aapne resign kiya, lekin aapko lagta hai shayad aapka knight late aa gaya. Board par sirf itna likhna kaafi nahi hai: “Main galti se galt knight rakha.” Sath mein yeh likhiye ki “king ko cover karne ke liye knight ki zaroorat thi,” “opponent ka center counterattack plan tha,” aur “agli game mein queenside ko jaldi kholne se pehle king ka safety check karunga.”

Ab us position ko restart karke alternatives compare kijiye. Shayad knight turant bhi achha hota, par koi retreat move aapka long-term plan zyada practical banata. Aapka kaam engine ki ek best line copy karna nahi hai; aapka kaam samajhna hai ki **aapki choice aur doosri choice** ki costs kya thi.

Aap ek simple label de sakte hain:

- **Tactical miss** — ek capture, check, fork, pin ya discovered attack miss hua.
- **Strategic unclear** — move legal thi, lekin pawn structure, piece activity ya king plan ka purpose samajh nahi aaya.
- **Time problem** — position badhi thi, clock ne sochne ka kaam aur ghata diya.
- **Endgame conversion** — material advantage thi, par technique ya plan se result pakka nahi hua.

In labels ko baad mein engine se compare karna helpful hai. Agar aapka label galat nikla, toh woh information bhi valuable hai: iska matlab aapne position ka ek important feature miss kiya, sirf move ka result nahi dekha.

## Apna personal error list banaiye

Har analyzed game ke baad ek **error list** banaiye. Ismein poori game ki list nahi, sirf do ya teen recurring mistakes honi chahiye. Har mistake ke saath ek short trigger likhiye, jaise “king ko open karte hue queen ko free de diya” ya “low clock mein unclear piece ko free chase kiya.”

Har entry ko agle hafte ke small assignment mein badliye. Agar aapka trigger “captures calculate nahi kiye” hai, toh next teen games mein har capture ke baad forced reply likhne ka task rakhiye. Assignment chhota aur observable hona chahiye; “better khelo” jaisa nahi.

Iska lesson “king ke aage pawnon ke peeche safe rehna” hai, isliye is move ko apni error list ke “safe squares nahi dekhe” trigger ke saath note kijiye.

## 45-minute analysis session

Analysis ka matlab ek poori din engine ke saath nikalna nahi hai. Ek practical session mein 10 minutes ek recent game ke saamne baith kar decisive moment dhoondhna, 20 minutes doos alternative branches khud calculate karna, aur 15 minutes apna error list update karna kaafi hai.

Agar 45 minutes mein aap nahi kar paate, toh kam karke bhi complete review kijiye. Ek well-written note, teen ghante ki scattered engine browsing se zyada useful hota hai. Aapko result ka theater nahi, decision ka evidence chahiye.

## What It Is NOT

Game analysis **har move ko engine ke against jeetne ki competition nahi hai**. Aapko har position mein perfect move nahi chahiye; aapko apni repeated costly mistakes aur unke pehle observable signs samajhne hain.

Game analysis **rating ka past tense report nahi hai**. Rating ek noisy signal hai, aur ek loss ka matlab hamesha aapki understanding kharab nahi hui. Board par kya hua, woh zyada reliable evidence hai.

Game analysis **sirf result dekhna bhi nahi hai**. Jeetne wali game mein bhi aap ek aise moment se seekh sakte ho jahan aapne chance waste kiya, aur haarne wali game mein bhi ek achha defensive move mil sakta hai.

Ab jab aap apni game ke baad engine ke saath safely compare kar sakte ho, toh next lesson mein engine ko ek coach ki tarah use karna seekhenge. Coach ka kaam aapki choice ko replace karna nahi, balki aapko dikhana hai ki aapka plan kahan weak tha.
