Train station par announcement hoti hai “train ten minute mein leave karegi”, aur aap us announcement ko sun kar apni packing, platform aur next move ko adjust kar lete ho. Aapko har second ka fixed schedule yaad rakhne ki zaroorat nahi hoti; aapko bas itna pata hona hota hai ki kis phase mein kaunsa kaam pehle karna hai. Chess clock bhi isi tarah ka external signal hai, lekin iska matlab har move par same number seconds rakhna nahi.

`6.3 Blunders ko Fix karna` mein humne dekha ki tactical, king-safety, structure aur time-pressure errors alag-alag hote hain. 10+0 mein in errors ko alag karna zaroori hai, kyunki aapke paas opening mein, middlegame mein aur endgame mein alag kinds ka time hota hai.

# 6.4 10+0 Clock Routine

Lichess par **10+0** ka matlab hai har player ko game shuru mein 10 minutes milte hain aur har move par fixed increment nahi add hota. Yeh “har move mein 10 minute” nahi hai. Clock aapke saare 40-plus moves ke total decision time ko manage karta hai, isliye sawaal `seconds per move` nahi, **kab kaunsa kaam finish karna hai** hai.

10+0 fast chess hai, par chess aur sprint mein antar karta hai. Sprint mein aap maximum effort chahiye; 10+0 mein aapko apne saare moves ka pura thought time nahi milega, isliye important position ko extra care dena padta hai. Ghadi dekhna aapki calculation ko improve karta hai, haan, lekin har move ko panic mein khelna bhi improvement nahi hai.

## Clock ko teen phases mein dekho

Game ke shuru mein aapko opening ke do complete moves sochne hain, lekin har possible line ka poori book chapter nahi padhna hai. Apne development, castling aur central pawn structure ke baare mein ek simple route note kar lijiye, phir opponent ki development dekh kar route adjust kijiye. Isse aap opening ka poora chapter khatam karne se bach kar middlegame ke liye bhi kuch bacha sakte ho.

Middlegame mein aap ek aisi position choose karo jahan ek clear question ho: kya center strong hai, kaunsa piece attack karega, ya king ka weak point kya hai? Har position ka poora evaluation karke 10+0 ke liye zyada time waste karna practical nahi. Apna goal board par likho aur jaise hi forcing move, capture ya threat milta hai, calculation ko wahin push kijiye.

Endgame mein rook, queen, knight ya bishop ka plan clear hona chahiye. Pawns promote karne, pieces exchange karne, ya rook ko active square le jaane ka ek simple purpose note kijiye. Jab plan clear ho, aap calculation chhoti aur disconnected branches mein tod sakte ho, kyunki aap har possible variation ko solve karne ki koshish nahi kar rahe.

## Koi universal seconds-per-move rule nahi

Online advice aksar kisi “har move par itne seconds” ki recipe deti hai. Woh number aapke opponent, position, internet speed, aur training level par depend karta hai, isliye koi universal rule nahi hai jo sab players par fit ho. Better choice hai apna clock pattern notice karna aur board ke urgent moments ko samajhna.

Aap ek study session mein teen 10+0 games khel kar dekh sakte hain ki time aap kab use karte ho aur kab lose karte ho. Sirf number note kijiye, prescription nahi: “opening ke baad 6 minutes bache the” ya “last ten moves mein har move expensive tha.” Ye observations aapke liye checkpoints ban sakte hain.

Lichess mein jab aap apna move click karte ho, clock turant ghadi start karti hai. Isliye aap move sochne ke baad confidently click kijiye, lekin position ko dhal-dhalane se pehle candidate moves shortlist kijiye. Board par galat move ko accidentally click karna bhi time cost hai, isliye aapka analysis habit aur interface discipline dono matter karte hain.

## 10+0 mein time trouble kaise pehchano

**Time trouble** woh stage hai jab aapke paas calculation ke liye bahut kam realistic time bacha ho. Iska matlab sirf clock ka red number nahi, balki aapki calculation ki simplicity, board ki clarity aur selection ka pressure bhi badh jaata hai.

Jab position complicated lage, toh pehle immediate threats ko order dein. Check, capture aur block ko saath mein dekhiye, phir unhe ek-ek karke resolve kijiye. Aap har possible rook-endgame branch solve karne ki koshish kiye baghair ek simple, practical choice identify kar sakte ho.

Aap “pass” ya “zugzwang” jaise advanced terms seekh rahe ho toh unhe abhi simple working definition ke saath rakhiye: pass ka matlab hai ek move na karna jab move karna position kharab karta hai; zugzwang ka matlab hai player ko move karna pada, lekin har legal move uski position ko thoda worse karta hai. In terms ka detailed study aage ke advanced topics mein hoga, par 10+0 mein principle hai: har move karna zaroori nahi.

## Lichess practice routine

Aap apne 45-minute session ka ek practical version aise bana sakte hain: 15 minute simple tactics ya position work, 15 minute do teen 10+0 games, aur 15 minute clock-aware review. Har game ke baad likhiye ki time kis phase mein gaya, kis move par anxiety aayi, aur ek forcing move ko identify karne mein kya rule yaad raha.

Review mein engine ko turant chala kar “correct move” mat nikaliye. Apna move aur pehle choice likhiye, phir engine se compare kijiye. Isse aap sirf time pressure mein survive karna nahi seekhte, balki woh decisions choose karna seekhte jo aapki actual position mein practical ho.

## What It Is NOT

10+0 routine **fixed seconds-per-move rule nahi hai**. Koi bhi universal number aapke position aur opponent ko fit nahi karta; personal observation aur stage awareness zyada kaam aati hai.

10+0 routine **har move ko jaldi karne ka excuse nahi hai**. Fast khelna useful ho sakta hai, lekin rushed capture, king danger aur pawn move samajh ke bina speed nahi hoti.

10+0 routine **time ko ignore karke calculation ki freedom nahi deta hai**. Clock aapko limited research time deta hai, isliye har naye question ko equal priority dena zaroori nahi. Simple goal choose karke baaki branches consciously defer karna seekhna hai.

Ab aap single game ke andar clock ko manage karna jaante ho. Agli lesson mein dekhenge ki kaise ek tournament ke multiple games aur real result ke baad recovery ko plan karein, kyunki sirf board focus karna real rated improvement ke liye kaafi nahi.
