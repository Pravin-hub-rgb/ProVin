# 5.6 Exercise Pack — Essential Endgames

Har position par pehle king aur piece locations ko bole, phir 2 minutes mein best move aur ek likely reply likho. Solution reveal se pehle apna reason sentence mein bolo—“maine yeh is liye kila kyunki…”—kyunki endgame ka goal sirf move nahi, decision ka pattern hai.

## Position 1 — Basic Mate Support

Black ka king g8 par hai aur f7, g7, h7 pawns ne back rank ke escape squares ko cover kiya hua lag raha hai. White ke paas queen, bishop aur ek knight hai; ab checkmate ka support chain dhoondho.

![Position 1 — basic mate support, White to move](/diagrams/chess/phase-5/phase-5-endgame-01.svg)

**Side to move:** White (W)  
**FEN:** `6k1/3N1ppp/8/7Q/8/8/2B5/7K w - - 0 12`  
**Attempt limit/task:** 90 seconds; find the mating move and name two pieces or squares that make the king’s escape impossible.

<details>
<summary>Reveal solution</summary>

**Best move:** `Qh7#`.

**Short continuation:** `Qh7#`—there is no legal Black move after the check.

**Why:** The queen on `h7` is protected by the bishop on `c2`, the knight on `d7` controls `f8`, and the queen controls `h8`. Black’s own pawns occupy the remaining escape squares, so every check component has a concrete board reason.

**Common error:** `Qf7+` is a different check, but the queen and knight relationship is easier to see when the mating queen goes to `h7`. Do not call a move mate without checking capture and escape.

</details>

## Position 2 — King and Pawn Cooperation

White ka king e5 par hai, pawn e4 par aur black king door e8 par. Ab pawn ko aage push karne se pehle king ko useful support square par le jaane ka best move dhoondho.

![Position 2 — king and pawn cooperation, White to move](/diagrams/chess/phase-5/phase-5-endgame-02.svg)

**Side to move:** White (W)  
**FEN:** `8/4k3/8/4K3/4P3/8/8/8 w - - 0 20`  
**Attempt limit/task:** 2 minutes; bring the king toward the pawn and write one plausible black king reply.

<details>
<summary>Reveal solution</summary>

**Best move:** `Kd5`.

**Short continuation:** `Kd5 Kd7 e5`; after the king supports the pawn, the advance can be considered with a clear relationship between the two pieces.

**Why:** `Kd5` improves the king’s access to the pawn and keeps the pawn from standing alone. The king can then choose a useful next square while the defending king’s approach is visible.

**Common error:** Moving the king too far without connecting it to the pawn wastes the support opportunity. The pawn cannot move to `e5` in the starting position because the king occupies that square, so always check occupancy before discussing a push.

</details>

## Position 3 — Use the Opposition

White king e5, white pawn d5 aur black king e7 hain. Black king ke saamne white ka direct opposition nahi hai—ab white pawn ke check move se opposition aur route kaise badal sakti hai?

![Position 3 — opposition with a checking pawn push, White to move](/diagrams/chess/phase-5/phase-5-endgame-03.svg)

**Side to move:** White (W)  
**FEN:** `8/4k3/8/3PK3/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 30`  
**Attempt limit/task:** 2 minutes; find the pawn move that uses the kings’ alignment and give Black’s forced king response.

<details>
<summary>Reveal solution</summary>

**Best move:** `d6+`.

**Short continuation:** `d6+ Kd7 Kf6`; White uses the check to obtain a new king route while keeping the pawn advanced.

**Why:** The pawn move changes the king’s direct opposition and creates a forcing reply. After Black moves, White can reposition toward the key-square zone instead of continuing to push the pawn mechanically.

**Common error:** A quiet king move may leave the pawn’s central route unchanged and allow Black to hold the opposition. The pawn push is valuable because it changes both the board and the move order, not merely because it is forward.

</details>

## Position 4 — Reach a Key Square

White king e5 aur pawn e4 hain, jabki black king a8 door hai. Ab identify karo kaunsa key-square zone entry White ke liye available hai aur king ko wahan le jaao.

![Position 4 — key-square entry, White to move](/diagrams/chess/phase-5/phase-5-endgame-04.svg)

**Side to move:** White (W)  
**FEN:** `k7/8/8/4K3/4P3/8/8/8 w - - 0 35`  
**Attempt limit/task:** 2 minutes; choose the first key-square route and name the next safe king step after a possible king reply.

<details>
<summary>Reveal solution</summary>

**Best move:** `Kd6`.

**Short continuation:** `Kd6 Kb7 Ke6`; both White king moves enter the useful zone around the pawn’s advance.

**Why:** For a non-rook pawn, the squares around `d6`, `e6` and `f6` form the practical key-square picture here. Reaching one of them gives the king a better relationship to promotion and reduces the defender’s freedom to create a blockade.

**Common error:** The pawn cannot be pushed to `e5` while White’s king is still on `e5`; first the king must create space and support. Also, any distant black king does not mean every king move works—check the route for the defender’s approach.

</details>

## Position 5 — Rook Cut-Off

White ke paas rook a6, pawn c6 aur king a1 hai, jabki black king h8 par isolated hai. Ab rook ko active rank par le jaakar defending king ke escape direction ko control karo.

![Position 5 — rook cut-off, White to move](/diagrams/chess/phase-5/phase-5-endgame-05.svg)

**Side to move:** White (W)  
**FEN:** `7k/8/R1P5/8/8/8/8/K7 w - - 0 40`  
**Attempt limit/task:** 2 minutes; choose a checking move that begins a cut-off plan and give one illustrative king reply.

<details>
<summary>Reveal solution</summary>

**Best move:** `Ra8+`.

**Short continuation:** `Ra8+ Kh7 Ra7+`; the rook controls the rank and then narrows the king’s remaining side route.

**Why:** The rook uses its active rank to cut the king off from a useful central area. The pawn remains available for support, and the rook’s next move continues the pressure instead of simply wandering with checks.

**Common error:** A pawn push such as `c7` may be legal, but it does not use the rook to restrict the king and gives up the active tempo. Rook-pawn technique needs both pieces’ purposes, not only a pawn move.

</details>

## Position 6 — Capture the Trapped Knight

Black knight f8 par hai; white rook f7 par use seedha capture kar sakta hai, jabki white king f6 aur c6 pawn exits control karte hain. Ab mobility scan karke trapped piece ko immediately capture karo.

![Position 6 — trapped knight, White to move](/diagrams/chess/phase-5/phase-5-endgame-06.svg)

**Side to move:** White (W)  
**FEN:** `5n1k/5R2/2P2K2/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 50`  
**Attempt limit/task:** 90 seconds; identify all knight exits, then find the best capture and one legal king reply.

<details>
<summary>Reveal solution</summary>

**Best move:** `Rxf8+`.

**Short continuation:** `Rxf8+ Kh7`; after the knight is removed, the rook continues to control the king’s route.

**Why:** The rook occupies `f7`, the king controls `e6` and `g6`, the c6 pawn controls `d7`, and the rook controls `h7`; the knight has no safe exit. Capturing immediately is cleaner than waiting while the defender gets a resource.

**Common error:** A distant rook check misses the already trapped piece and can give the king a useful escape. Count exits first, then bring the shortest piece to the target.

</details>

## Readiness Checkpoint

Pack ke baad solutions hide karke dobara positions kholo. Agar aap kam se kam paanch positions par king route, pawn support, key square, rook cut-off ya trapped mobility ke bina-diye ek sentence reason bana sakte ho, toh aap basic endgames ke liye ready hai. Agar har move ka jawab sirf engine ya solution se aata hai, toh 5-minute Lichess endgames karke mistakes annotate karna useful hoga.

Har position ka FEN, image aur side to move match karna routine banao; ek galat square exercise ka difficulty aur answer dono bigad sakta hai. Agle quiz mein Phase 5 ke terms aur reasoning test honge, phir Phase 6 mein game analysis ka process seekhenge.

## What It Is NOT

Exercise pack **endgame ka complete theorem course** nahi hai. Yahan ke moves basic patterns ke recognition hain, isliye inhe ratne ke bajay king route, pawn timing aur piece mobility ko board par explain karna seekho. Advanced theory aur rating ka guarantee is pack se promise nahi hoti.

Ek answer **har possible best move ka claim** nahi hota. Learner ke perspective se ek clear practical move, uska short continuation aur reason already valuable feedback hai; phir calculation ya stronger move ke liye improvement ka next assignment banta hai.

**Jawab:** Position 2 mein goal king ko pawn ke saath cooperate karne wali square par laana hai. Position 3 mein pawn ka checking push opposition aur move-order ko change karta hai, jisse king ko naya key-square route milta hai.

2. Opposition aur key squares ko ek sentence mein kaise connect karoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Opposition king ke approach aur move order ko control karti hai, jabki key square king ko pawn ke useful support zone mein le jaata hai. Saath mein dono ka matlab hai: sahi timing par pawn ko sahi spatial support dena.

</details>

3. Rook-pawn position mein rook ko cut-off ke liye kyun move karna padta hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Cut-off rook ko defending king ke aage le jaakar promotion route aur escape direction control karne deta hai. Isse pawn aage badh sakta hai aur king activity bhi practical ho jaati hai.

</details>

4. Trapped knight position mein mobility count ne best capture kaise dhoondha?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Knight ke legal exits ek-ek karke check kiye gaye: ek rook se blocked, do king se controlled aur ek rook-line par attacked. Jab har safe exit band ho, capture ka move clear ho jaata hai.

</details>
