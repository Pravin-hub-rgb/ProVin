# 5.5 Trapped Pieces aur Common Endgame Traps

**Trapped piece** woh piece hai jiske abhi koi safe square kam ho raha hai, yahan tak ki uski mobility limited ho jaaye. Trapped hone ka matlab “abhi capture ho gaya” zaroori nahi; piece temporarily blocked ho sakta hai, defender usse exchange kar sakta hai, ya rook/king ke support se bahar nikal sakta hai. Isliye pehle mobility count karo, phir capture karke force karne ke baad resulting position dekho.

Beginner scan mein har piece ke liye yeh poochho: piece abhi kahan hai, uske kitne legal safe moves hain, aur kya opponent us square ko control kar raha hai? Agar sirf ek exit square bacha ho aur woh square attacked ho, toh piece practically trapped ho sakta hai. Phir decide karo ki use turant capture karna hai, uska defender exchange karna hai, ya board par ek aur way se pressure badhana hai.

## Stalemate ka trap

**Stalemate** woh draw hota hai jab side to move ke king check mein nahi hota, lekin uska koi legal move nahi hota. Iske ulat, check ke baad koi legal reply na ho toh woh checkmate hota hai. Endgame mein aggressive side king ko corner karke rook ya pawn pushes se accidentally stalemate bana sakta hai. Isse bachne ke liye har forcing move ke baad opponent ke king ke paas legal move, check ya capture resource hai ya nahi—yeh count karna seekho.

Stalemate draw hai, win nahi. Agar aapka plan “king ko completely freeze karna” hai, toh last move se pehle ek extra move ya alternative target check karo. King ko corner karna, promotion path aur opponent ke baaki pieces ko ek saath dekhna practical protection hai.

## Back-rank trap

Back-rank trap tab samajh aata hai jab king ke aage apne pawns hain aur opposing rook/queen peeche se rank par check de sakte hain. King ko “safer” lagne ke liye pawns aage push karna tempting hota hai, lekin un pawns hi escape squares block kar sakte hain. Endgame scan mein king ke saamne pawns aur peeche rook/queen ki line ko saath dekho.

## King greed aur exchange ke baad ka board

King ko central square lene ka sahi reason hota hai, lekin king ko greedy capture ke liye blindly aage le jaana nahi. Queen-pawn, rook-pawn ya bishop-pawn promotion ke baad counterattack mil sakta hai, aur king ka active position suddenly defensive ho ja sakta hai. King move ke saath poochho: kya main ek piece capture kar raha hoon, ya opponent ko ek free piece/promotion de raha hoon?

Exchange ke baad dobara board scan karna bhi common trap rokta hai. Aapne defensive knight ko exchange kar diya, lekin ab king ke aage open file ya back-rank pattern ban gaya. Simplification tabhi helpful hai jab remaining pieces ke saath position clean ho, sirf is liye nahi ki ek piece kam ho gaya.

## Trapped piece ko force karna

Agar opponent piece trapped hai, usko turant capture karna zaroori nahi; pehle uske escape square par control badhao. Rook ko rank badal do, king ko paas laao, ya pawn push se square block karo. Jab har escape block ho, capture ya exchange ka option assess karo. Common error trapped piece ko dekh kar queen ko unnecessary long route par bhaagna hai—closest supporting piece aur fastest route calculate karo.

Is scan ko endgame ke har move ke baad use kar sakte hain. Pieces kam ho gaye hain, isliye ek galti ka material effect bada hota hai aur defending king ki location badalne se threat badh sakta hai. Plan ke saath safety checklist ko alag mat chhodna; yahi practical junior improvement hai.

## What It Is NOT

Trapped piece **already captured** nahi hota. Mobility temporarily limited ho sakti hai, aur piece defender ke saath bahar nikal sakta hai. Isliye “trapped” label ke saath legal escape, capture aur exchange lines verify karo.

Stalemate **automatically aapki jeet** nahi. Agar aapka opponent legal move nahi kar sakta, game draw ho jaata hai, chahe aapki pieces kitni bhi active lagein. Back-rank trap bhi ek pattern hai, har endgame ka final result nahi; board par king, pawns, rook/queen aur escapes dekhna padta hai.

King activity **greed ka license** nahi. King ko useful square par le jaana useful hai, lekin promotion threat ko ignore karke central capture lena position kharab kar sakta hai. Endgame mein “king move” likhne se pehle opponent ka counter-plan bhi sochna chahiye.
