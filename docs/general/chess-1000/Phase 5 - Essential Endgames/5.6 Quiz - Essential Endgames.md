# 5.6 Quiz - Essential Endgames

Aapne ek house ka floor plan dekh kar decide kiya tha ki furniture kaunsa kone mein rakhega, kyunki sahi jagah ka kaam kam aur clean arrangement se zyada hota hai. 5.6 Exercise Pack mein bhi aapne endgames ko king route, pawn support, rook activity aur piece mobility ke through solve kiya. Ab quiz mein wahi reasoning bina board ke yaad rakhte hue check karna hai.

Yeh quiz Essential Endgames phase ka last step hai. Iske baad 6.1 Game Analysis Without Engine mein apni games ko step-by-step dekhna seekhenge, taaki har phase ka ek mistake ya pattern next practice ka plan ban sake.

## What It Is NOT

Yeh quiz **middlegame ke attack aur defence ko endgame ke saath replace** nahi karta. Saare sawaal king activity, basic mates, king-pawn technique, opposition, key squares, rook-pawns aur common traps ke andar hain; inhein board facts se connect karna hai.

Endgame ka sahi answer **rating ya final result guarantee** nahi hai. Ek pattern samajhna aapki understanding ka signal hai, lekin conversion mein calculation aur opponent ki resistance bhi hoti hai.

**In Your Own Words**

1. King activity ko pawn support aur mating route se kaise compare karoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Main king ke destination ka practical role poochhunga: woh pawn ko support kar raha hai, weak square control kar raha hai, ya escape block kar raha hai. Sirf king ka centre mein hona activity ka proof nahi.

</details>

2. Opposition aur key squares ko ek beginner plan mein kaise combine karoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Opposition se kings ka approach aur move order control hoga, aur key-square zone king ko pawn ke useful support destination dega. Pawn push ka timing tab evaluate hoga jab king, defending king aur resulting squares ek saath dekhe jayein.

</details>

3. Rook-pawn position mein cut-off aur stalemate trap se kaise bachoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Jawab</summary>

**Jawab:** Rook ko active rank par defending king ke aage le jaakar promotion route control karunga, har check ke baad legal moves count karunga, aur last move se pehle stalemate ka risk check karunga. Rook ka active pressure aur safe conversion dono goal hain.

</details>

## MCQ

Q: Endgame mein king activity ka sabse sahi beginner-level meaning kya hai?
A. King ko har possible move centre mein le jaana
B. King ko pawn, weak square ya mating route ke saath useful support par le jaana
C. King ko hamesha corner mein rakhna, kyunki woh safe hai
D. King ko queen ko de dijiye
> B. King activity ka matlab useful square par support aur participation hai, chahe woh pawn ke saath, weak square par, ya mate mein ho. Sirf visual activity nahi, board ka practical purpose zaroori hai.

---

Q: Opposition ko beginner level par kaise samjha ja sakta hai?
A. Kings ke beech spatial constraint jisse move order aur approach control hoti hai
B. Sirf pawn ka check hona
C. Queen ko centre mein laana
D. King ka hamesha rook se exchange hona
> A. Opposition kings ke spacing aur approach ko restrict karti hai. Iska matlab side-to-move ko additional decision lena padta hai; opposition pawn move se badal sakti hai.

---

Q: Key square ka practical purpose kya hai?
A. King ko pawn ke useful support zone aur promotion direction ke paas le jaana
B. Pawn ko hamesha black king ke neeche rakhna
C. Rook ko hamesha seventh rank par park karna
D. Stalemate se bachne ke liye hamesha check karna
> A. Key square woh destination hai jahan king pawn ko support aur future promotion direction deta hai. Zone ko pawn se aage ke useful squares par visualize karna padta hai, defending king ko bhi consider karna hota hai.

---

Q: Rook-pawn ending mein cut-off ka matlab kya hai?
A. Rook ko defending king ke aage le jaakar promotion route block karna
B. Pawn ko board se turant hata dena
C. Queen ko rook se exchange karna
D. King ko opponent ke opposite corner bhej dena
> A. Cut-off rook ya king ke through defending king ko pawn se door rakhta hai aur escape choices kam karta hai. Rook ka active rank aur king ka distance board par saath dekhne hote hain.

---

## TrueFalse

Q: Stalemate ek automatic win hai, kyunki side to move ke paas koi legal move nahi hota.
- True
- False
> False. Stalemate draw hota hai, win nahi. Side to move ka king check mein nahi hona chahiye, lekin uske paas koi legal move bhi nahi hona chahiye.

---

Q: Kaunsa piece “trapped” hai, yeh judge karne ke liye mobility, exits aur supporting defenders dekhne hote hain.
- True
- False
> True. Trapped ka matlab capture already confirm nahi hota; legal safe exits count karo aur capture, exchange ya escape possibilities verify karo. Phir immediate board result ke hisaab se move choose karo.

---

## ShortAnswer

Q: King-pawn ending mein opposition aur key squares ko ek practical king plan mein kaise combine karoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Answer:** Opposition se move order aur kings ka approach control karunga, phir key-square zone ko destination banaunga. King ko pawn ke useful squares par le jaane ke liye timing aur defending king ke responses check karunga, taaki pawn push support ke saath ho.

</details>

---

Q: Rook-pawn ending mein wrong-side check, trapped piece aur back-rank trap ko kaise avoid karoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Answer:** Har check par rook ka rank, pawn ka route aur defending king ke escape/capture options dekhunga. Mobility scan karke trapped piece ko support se capture karunga, aur king ke aage pawn wall ke saath opposing rook/queen line par back-rank mate check karunga.

</details>
