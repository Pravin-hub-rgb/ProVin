# 1.6 Exercise Pack

## Pack kaise use karein

Yeh Phase 1 ka exercise pack hai, aur isme fork, pin, skewer, discovered attack, double attack, back-rank idea, aur combination ke liye chhah alag legal positions hain. Har position pehle tum solve karoge; answer kholne se pehle move aur ek-sentence reason likho. Iska maqsad six out of six score karna nahi, balki apni first-try accuracy aur explanation ko compare karna hai.

Positions dheere-dhehere mushkil hain, isliye pehle do ko 90 seconds do, agle do ko 2 minutes, aur last do ko 3 minutes do. Agar time khatam ho jaye to guess ko “not sure” mark karke aage badho; engine answer turant dekhne se pehle apna independent attempt zaroori hai.

## Set 1 — forcing move and fork

Pehla position Phase 1 ke simplest scan-and-fork pattern se shuru hota hai. Board par sparse pieces hain, isliye pehle king aur queen ko ek saath target karne wale knight jump ko dhoondho.

![Phase 1 tactic position 01](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-01.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `8/8/2k1q3/8/8/5N2/8/6K1 w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 90 seconds. Find the forcing knight move and write the opponent’s forced response.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Nd4+`

**Short main line:** `1. Nd4+ Kd5 2. Nxe6`

**Why it works:** The knight attacks the king on c6 and queen on e6 simultaneously. Black cannot save both, so the king moves and the queen becomes available.

**Common mistake:** Capturing the queen without checking the king’s forcing reply. Tactical priority ko material desire se pehle rakhna tha.

</details>

## Set 2 — pin and skewer

Doosra pattern line par blocker ko remove karke king expose karta hai. Is position mein tumhe capture ke baad check ka direct consequence verify karna hai, isliye escape calculation ko ek move aage nahi chhodna.

![Phase 1 tactic position 02](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-02.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `Rrk5/8/2N5/8/8/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 90 seconds. Identify the blocker, capture it, and name the check that follows.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Rxb8+`

**Short main line:** `1. Rxb8+ Kd7`

**Why it works:** The black rook on b8 is pinned between White’s rook and the king on c8. Removing it exposes the king directly, and the white knight on c6 protects b8, so the black king cannot recapture.

**Common mistake:** Trying to capture a different piece while ignoring the adjacent pinned blocker. The shortest legal capture is often the tactical point, but the recapture square must be protected.

</details>

## Set 3 — skewer along a file

Teesra position rook, queen, aur king ko ek file par rakhta hai. Is baar valuable piece ko line se hatane ke baad king par attack karna hai, isliye capture ke target aur blocker ka role alag se note karo.

![Phase 1 tactic position 03](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-03.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `3k4/8/3q4/8/8/8/8/3R3K w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 2 minutes. Remove the queen from the king’s line and calculate one legal king reply.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Rxd6+`

**Short main line:** `1. Rxd6+ Kc7`

**Why it works:** The queen on d6 blocks the rook’s file. After the rook captures it, the rook checks the king on d8, which is the skewer pattern.

**Common mistake:** Treating the queen as the only target and forgetting that removing it is what makes the king attack real. Capture order matters here.

</details>

## Set 4 — discovered check

Chautha position hidden line ko exercise karta hai. Knight abhi rook ke saamne blocker hai, isliye direct rook move ke bajay blocker ko hataane wale knight move ko search karo.

![Phase 1 tactic position 04](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-04.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `7k/8/8/8/8/8/7N/4K2R w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 2 minutes. Find a move that opens the rook’s h-file and gives check.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Nf3+`

**Short main line:** `1. Nf3+ Kg8`

**Why it works:** The knight leaves h2 and uncovers the rook on h1 against the king on h8. The rook has not moved, so this is a discovered check.

**Common mistake:** Checking the rook’s line but not recognizing the knight as the blocker. Isliye scan mein “who is hiding behind this piece?” question poochho.

</details>

## Set 5 — protected double attack

Paanchvi position mein queen capture ke baad two targets matter hain, aur bishop capturing piece ko protect karta hai. Is position ko dekh kar tumhe capturing piece ki safety bhi verify karni hai.

![Phase 1 tactic position 05](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-05.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `5rk1/5ppp/8/3Q4/2B5/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 2 minutes. Find the capture, name the two attacked targets, and check the recapture line.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Qxf7+`

**Short main line:** `1. Qxf7+ Rxf7 2. Bxf7+`

**Why it works:** The queen attacks the king on g8 and rook on f8. If the rook takes the queen, the bishop on c4 recaptures on f7 along the cleared diagonal.

**Common mistake:** Playing the capture and assuming the queen is safe because it gives check. Protection by the bishop is what makes the combination work.

</details>

## Set 6 — deflection and mate

Chhatha position phase ka hardest finish hai. Yahan forcing move se defender ko apni defensive line se nikalna hai, aur final check ke baad king ke escape squares count karne hain.

![Phase 1 tactic position 06](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-06.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `5r1k/7p/6Q1/4N3/8/8/8/4K1R1 w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 3 minutes. Find the forcing combination, verify Black’s reply, and list the king’s escape squares after the finish.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `1. Nf7+!`

**Short main line:** `1. Nf7+ Rxf7 2. Qg8#`

**Why it works:** The knight check forces the black rook from f8 to f7, pulling the defender off the back rank. The queen then delivers an adjacent check on g8, protected by the g1 rook; g7 is covered and h7 is occupied, so the king has no escape or block.

**Common mistake:** Jumping to the queen mate before the knight check, or forgetting to verify the rook’s forced capture. Move order creates the mate.

</details>

## Phase readiness checkpoint

Pack complete hone ke baad apna score aur reason quality note karo. 4 ya zyada correct first attempts ek useful readiness signal hai, lekin guarantee nahi; kam score ka matlab hai weak pattern ko exact lesson se dobara drill karna, course chhodna nahi.

Saan positions ke baad tum yeh bhi check karo: kya tum at least do answers mein opponent ke strongest response ke baad continuation samjha sakte ho? Agar haan, Phase 1 ki tactical vocabulary abhi solid ho rahi hai. Agar nahi, apne missed pattern ko identify karke us file ka focused revision aur next mixed set karo.

## What It Is NOT

Exercise pack ka score rating forecast nahi hai aur six out of six ka pressure tumhe tactics enjoy karna band kar sakta hai. Iska kaam pattern recall aur decision quality ko measure karna hai, identity ko judge karna nahi.

Answer dekhne ke baad “main yeh kar sakta tha” enough nahi hai. Reason likho aur ek similar position dhundho, kyunki transfer wahi hota hai jab pattern ko naye board par recognize karna aaye.
