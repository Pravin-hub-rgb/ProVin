# 1.6 Quiz - Tactical Vision
## MCQ

Q: Board scan mein forcing ideas ko pehle dhoondhne ka practical order kya hai?
A. Pawn structure, opening theory, king, captures
B. King, checks, captures, threats, normal plan
C. Material count, engine score, king, pawns
D. Only pieces on the back rank
> B. King aur immediate forcing moves pehle dekhne se crowded board par important problem miss hone ka chance kam hota hai. Uske baad normal plan aur broader position evaluate kiya jaata hai.

---

Q: Absolute pin aur relative pin mein kya fark hai?
A. Absolute pin mein piece king ko expose karne wala move nahi kar sakta; relative pin mein aisa move legal ho sakta hai, lekin protected piece capture ka material risk hota hai
B. Dono mein pinned piece ka har move illegal hota hai
C. Dono mein pinned piece ko aap hamesha free capture kar sakte hain
D. Relative pin king ko immediately check mein daal deta hai
> A. Absolute-pinned piece king ko check mein chhodne wala move nahi kar sakta. Relative pin mein aisa move legal ho sakta hai, lekin attacker pinned piece ko capture karke material jeet sakta hai, isliye material loss hona common hai.

---

Q: Skewer ka basic pattern kya hai?
A. Valuable piece ko hata kar king ko line par expose karna
B. King ko apne pawn se capture karna
C. Bishop se every queen ko exchange karna
D. Rook ko h8 par park karna
> A. Skewer mein line par khadi valuable piece, usually queen, hata kar king ko direct attack milta hai. Capture ke baad king ka response count karna zaroori hai.

---

Q: Deflection ka practical meaning kya hai?
A. Har queen ko foran exchange karna
B. Defending piece ko us original role se door force karna
C. Board ke center ko permanently khali karna
D. Opening ko completely avoid karna
> B. Deflection defender ko lure ya force karke us kaam se hatati hai jaise line block karna ya square guard karna. Us gap se dusra attack create hota hai.

## TrueFalse

Q: Discovered attack mein blocker hile bina attacker piece khud move kiye bahut door se line activate ho sakti hai.
- True
- False
> True. Isme ek blocking piece move hota hai aur rook, bishop, ya queen ki pehle se maujood line target tak pahunch jaati hai. Attacking piece khud move nahi hota.

---

Q: Check de diya aur king ko ek escape square mil gaya, to wo checkmate hota hai.
- True
- False
> False. Checkmate mein king ka escape, check block, ya checking piece capture — koi legal option nahi hona chahiye. Agar escape square bacha, to move check hai, mate nahi.

## ShortAnswer

Q: Fork aur skewer ko apne words mein difference ke saath explain karo.

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Sample Answer:** Fork ek move mein king aur queen jaise do alag valuable targets ko attack karta hai, isliye opponent ek target bacha sakta hai. Skewer mein pehle valuable blocker, jaise queen, line se capture hota hai aur uske peeche king ko direct attack milta hai. Fork targets create karta hai, skewer blocker ko hata kar king expose karta hai.

</details>

Q: `Nf7+ Rxf7 2. Qg8#` combination mein forcing idea aur mate ka verification explain karo.

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> **Sample Answer:** `Nf7+` knight check Black ke king ko respond karne par force karta hai, aur black rook ko f8 se f7 capture par lena padta hai. Isse defender back rank se hat jaata hai. `Qg8#` adjacent check deta hai; g1 rook queen ko protect karta hai, g7 covered hai, h7 occupied hai, aur rook ke paas block ka move nahi hai. Isliye king capture, escape, ya block kisi bhi tarah nahi kar sakta.

</details>
