# 1.2 Fork, Pin aur Skewer

## Fork

**Fork** ka matlab hai tumhari ek move do alag valuable targets ko attack kar leti hai, jaise king aur queen. Forcing move mein opponent usually ek target bacha sakta hai, isliye doosra target material ya position ka free gift ban jaata hai. Knight aur queen ke common attack patterns mein fork dikhta hai; rook bhi line attack se king aur valuable piece par fork de sakta hai.

Iska analogy traffic ka crossroad hai: jaise ek vehicle ek hi moment mein do alag lanes ko block kar sakti hai, waise tactical move bhi do alag values ko ek line par target kar sakti hai. Fork board par dhoondhne ke liye pehle opponent ke valuable pieces note karo, phir dekho kya tumhara piece unke beech ek common attack square mein aa sakta hai.

Fork ka fayda material aur initiative dono de sakta hai. Tradeoff yeh hai ki opponent usually ek target par respond karta hai, isliye tumhe capture se pehle protection aur escape count karna padta hai.

## Pin

**Pin** tab hota hai jab opponent ka ek piece line mein khada ho aur use move karne se king par attack expose ho. Absolute pin mein piece ka king-exposing move illegal ho jaata hai, kyunki king check mein aa jayega. Relative pin mein piece aisa move kar sakta hai, lekin attacker us protected piece ko capture kar material jeet sakta hai. Isliye pin ko sirf “move karna illegal” ya “hamesha safe” ki shortcut samajhna galat hai; asli rule king safety hai.

Jaise ek building ke aage khadi car ko hilane se road ka exit block ho sakta hai, waise pinned piece ko move karna ek attack ko uncover kar sakta hai. Pin board par capture, check, ya attacking piece ko line se remove karke verify karo; agar king target hai, to opponent ke liye move ka risk badh gaya hai.

Pin se material jeetne ke liye tumhe pinned piece ko attack karna padta hai, lekin agar wo piece defended ho to capture karne ke baad defender ko bhi count karna padta hai. Isliye pin dhoondna useful hai, lekin har pin ko automatic material gift samajhna beginner trap hai.

## Skewer

**Skewer** pin ke almost mirror concept hai: ek valuable piece, usually queen, king se aage line par khadi ho, aur capturing piece ko remove karne se woh line king tak khul jaati hai. Isliye skewer mein pehle valuable piece ko force karke hataate hain, phir king ko attack karte hain.

Jaise ek important signboard hataane ke baad peeche khidki khul jaati hai, waise skewer mein queen ke hatne ke baad king ko direct attack milta hai. Is pattern ko queen, rook, ya bishop ke line par dhoondhna hai; aapko source piece ke saath target piece ke beech ka blocker bhi check karna hai.

Skewer ki strength capturing piece aur uske protection par depend karti hai. Agar capturing piece protected hai aur king capture kar sakta hai, to skewer se guaranteed material gain nahi hota; isliye short main line aur response verify karna zaroori hai.

## Three patterns compare

Teenon patterns mein ek line aur do important pieces hain, lekin sequence alag hai. Comparison table ko teeno definitions padhne ke baad hi use karna, taaki labels ko ratna na pade.

| Pattern | Kya hota hai | Typical result |
|---|---|---|
| Fork | Ek move do targets ko attack karti hai | King, queen, rook, ya material free mil sakta hai |
| Pin | Piece move karke king ko expose karti hai | Absolute pin mein king-exposing move illegal; relative pin mein woh legal ho sakta hai, lekin aksar material loss laata hai |
| Skewer | Valuable piece ko line se hata kar king expose hota hai | Queen ya piece ke baad king ko direct attack milta hai |

## Exercise — pin and removal

Yahan white rook ke saamne black rook aur king ek hi rank par hain. Pehle identify karo kaunsa piece line ko block kar raha hai, phir capture ke baad check ka result dekho.

![Phase 1 tactic position 02](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-02.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `Rrk5/8/2N5/8/8/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 2 minutes. Find the rook capture that exploits the black rook’s role between the white rook and king.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Rxb8+`

**Short main line:** `1. Rxb8+ Kd7`

**Why it works:** The black rook on b8 is pinned between the white rook and king on c8. White removes it, so the attack reaches the king and the pinned rook is lost; the white knight on c6 protects b8, so Black cannot recapture with the king.

**Common mistake:** Trying to calculate a long capture sequence while missing the immediate adjacent capture. A pin is easiest to understand when you first remove the blocker and then check whether the captured square is protected.

</details>

## Exercise — skewer and exposure

Is position mein rook, queen, aur king ek file par hain. Tumhe queen ko capture karke uske peeche king ko attack karna hai, isliye pehle blocker ko identify karo.

![Phase 1 tactic position 03](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-03.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `3k4/8/3q4/8/8/8/8/3R3K w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 2 minutes. Find the rook capture that removes the queen from the king’s line.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Rxd6+`

**Short main line:** `1. Rxd6+ Kc7`

**Why it works:** The queen on d6 blocks the rook’s file attack on the king. After `Rxd6+`, the queen is gone and the rook checks the king directly, which is the skewer pattern.

**Common mistake:** Looking for a knight fork first and ignoring the rook’s line. A skewer can be created by any line piece when the right blocker is removed.

</details>

## What It Is NOT

Fork, pin, aur skewer teen alag visual tags nahi hain jo har position par lagane hain. Inka use tab meaningful hota hai jab move ke baad actual king safety, material, ya initiative ka consequence verify ho.

Pin ko hamesha “pinned piece ka koi bhi move illegal hota hai” mat samajho. Absolute pin mein piece king ko check mein chhodne wala move nahi kar sakta. Relative pin mein aisa move legal ho sakta hai, lekin protected piece capture hone par material ya position ka nuksaan hota hai.
