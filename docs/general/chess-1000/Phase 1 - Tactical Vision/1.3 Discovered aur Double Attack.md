# 1.3 Discovered aur Double Attack

## Discovered attack

**Discovered attack** tab hota hai jab tum ek blocking piece ko move karke pehle se maujood line ko target piece tak attack ke liye kholte ho. “Discovered” ka matlab hai attack naya piece ke move se nahi, uske peeche pehle se khuli potential line se aata hai. Discovered check bhi ho sakta hai, jab line king tak pahunchti hai.

Iska simple analogy traffic ka turning point hai: ek car pehle ek lane block kar rahi thi, use hataate hi doosri lane ka flow active ho jaata hai. Board par blocker ko hataane se pehle check karo ki uske peeche rook, bishop, ya queen ke liye clear line hai kya. Agar line sirf theoretical hai aur path blocked hai, to discovered attack nahi hai.

Discovered attack ka fayda opponent ko prepared choice kam deta hai, kyunki attack ka source move se kuch door ho sakta hai. Tradeoff yeh hai ki tumhe blocking piece aur target line dono track karni padti hai; ek ko dekhna doosre ko miss kar sakta hai.

## Double attack

**Double attack** ka matlab hai ek move ke baad do alag targets par practical threat ho, jaise queen aur king ya queen aur rook. Iska broad umbrella hai aur fork bhi iske andar aa sakta hai, lekin abhi focus geometry par hai: ek move, do immediate consequences, aur opponent ke paas dono ko ek saath defend karne ki limitation.

Double attack ko samajhne ke liye board par opponent ke sabse valuable pieces list karo, phir dekho tumhari move unki common target square ya common line ko kaise activate karti hai. Agar ek target forced response se bach jayega, to doosra target actual material ya mate opportunity ban sakta hai.

Queen aur bishop ka combination ek capture aur protection ke saath double attack ko material jeet sakta hai. Iska fayda simple hai — opponent ko ek move mein sab solve nahi karna padta. Iska drawback yahi hai ki agar tumhari capturing piece unprotected hai, opponent seedha wapas capture kar sakta hai.

## Dono ka working difference

Discovered attack mein tumhari move pehle se khulti hui line ko activate karti hai. Double attack mein tumhari move se ek ya zyada nayi attacks create hoti hain, aur targets aapas mein alag ho sakte hain. Kabhi ek move dono ka combined example ban sakti hai, lekin pehle se identify karo attack ka order.

Is distinction ko ratne se pehle board par visualize karo: pehle blocker ko hatao, phir nayi line dekho; ya pehle nayi target line banao, phir opponent ke escape count karo. “Line open” aur “two targets” ek hi inspection question nahi hain.

## Exercise — open the line

Yahan white knight h-file par rook ke aage blocker hai. Tumhe direct rook move se pehle dekhna hai ki knight ko kahan bhej kar h-file ko open kiya ja sakta hai.

![Phase 1 tactic position 04](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-04.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `7k/8/8/8/8/8/7N/4K2R w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 2 minutes. Find a knight move that leaves the rook’s h-file open and gives check.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Nf3+`

**Short main line:** `1. Nf3+ Kg8`

**Why it works:** The knight leaves h2, opening the rook’s h-file from h1 to the black king on h8. The move is a discovered check because the attacking rook did not move.

**Common mistake:** Treating the position as a direct rook move and forgetting the knight that was blocking the line. Yahan direct rook push nahi, blocker ko hata kar hidden attack activate karna hai.

</details>

## Exercise — queen and bishop combination

Is position mein queen capture ke baad king aur rook ko ek hi queen move se attack milega, aur bishop capturing piece ko protect kar sakta hai. Pehle target relationship dhoondho, phir recapture check karo.

![Phase 1 tactic position 05](/diagrams/chess/phase-1/phase-1-tactic-05.svg)

**Side to move:** White  
**FEN:** `5rk1/5ppp/8/3Q4/2B5/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1`  
**Time limit/task:** 2 minutes. Find the queen capture, name both attacked targets, and check the bishop’s protection.

<details>
<summary>Show answer and review</summary>

**Best move:** `Qxf7+`

**Short main line:** `1. Qxf7+ Rxf7 2. Bxf7+`

**Why it works:** The queen on f7 attacks the king on g8 and the rook on f8. If the rook captures the queen, the bishop on c4 recaptures on f7 because the queen’s departure clears the diagonal.

**Common mistake:** Seeing the double attack without checking the bishop’s protection. A double attack is strongest when the first target is backed by a second piece.

</details>

## What It Is NOT

Discovered attack ko sirf “any check by a rook” kehna galat hai; blocker ka hona aur line ka activation important hai. Double attack ko poori guarantee nahi samajhna chahiye, kyunki opponent ek threat ko block karke doosre ko bhi deflect kar sakta hai.

In patterns ko bina verify kiye lagana bhi sahi nahi hai. Agar target defended nahi hai, ya move se apna king expose hota hai, to calculated advantage nahi bachta.
