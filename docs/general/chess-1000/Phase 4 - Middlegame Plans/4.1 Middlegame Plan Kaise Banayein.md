Aapne phone ke Maps mein apni location ke baad ek route choose kiya hota hai. Abhi traffic nahi dikhta, toh aap seedha road choose kar lete hain; jab congestion dikhta hai, toh goal bhi badal jaata hai—target tak pahunchne ke liye dusra raasta le lena practical hota hai. Chess mein bhi hum board par apne pieces ka ek “route” choose karte hain, lekin har move opponent ke jawab ke saath recalculate karna padta hai.

3.5 Quiz - Opening Fundamentals and Repertoire mein tumne opening ko repeatable starting setup ki tarah dekha tha. Ek opening jo theory ke paar nikal jaye, apne aap mein middlegame plan nahi hoti; ab us setup ko clear goal aur practical moves mein convert karna hai.

# 4.1 Middlegame Plan Kaise Banayein

Opening ke kuch moves ke baad, jab ab development, pawn structure aur king safety ka faisla zyada matter karta hai, woh part middlegame hota hai. Iska koi fixed move number nahi hota—kabhi opening ke 10th move ke baad shuru ho sakta hai aur kabhi 25th move tak简单 theory-chase jaise dikhta rehta hai. Asli pehchaan yeh hai ki aapki setup ek practical kaam kar rahi hai ya nahi.

**Middlegame plan** ka matlab hai “mujhe is position mein yeh result chahiye, isliye main yeh steps sool raha hoon.” Plan ek fixed line nahi hoti; wo ek reason aur direction hoti hai. Jaise aap ghar ka raasta plan karte hain, aap board par apne rook ko open file par, knight ko weak square par, ya king ko safety ke paas le jaane ka reason choose kar sakte hain. Phir opponent ke move ke baad wahi reason thoda adjust ho sakta hai.

## Pehle safety, phir ambition

Middlegame mein sabse pehle king safety check karna useful hai, kyunki ek open line khuli ho toh woh turant attack ban sakti hai. King ke aage pawn aur pieces ke hain ya nahi, opponent ke rooks aur queen ka direct line kahan hai, aur kya koi fork jaisa pattern ek piece ko do hisson se attack kar raha hai—yeh teen sawal pehle dekho. King ko baar-baar check karne ke baad koi “creative” move karna aksar jeetne se pehle hi position khol deta hai.

Iske baad material aur pawns dekho. Material ka matlab hai board par aapke paas kitne pieces hain, jabki pawn structure batata hai ki future mein kaunsa piece strong rahega aur kaunsa weak. Isolated pawns, doubled pawns, connected pawns aur passed pawns—yeh terms tabhi kaam karte hain jab aap unka simple reason samajhte ho; agle topic mein hum unhe detail mein compare karenge. Abhi itna kaafi hai ki aap poori move list ki jagah apne strongest concrete objective choose karein.

## Activity ka matlab hai kaam karna

**Activity** ka matlab hai aapka piece actively participate kare, sirf safe parking mein na baith kar. Active rook file aur ranks badal kar position mein naye targets dhoondhe, knight center ya outpost jaise strong square par land kare, aur bishop diagonals par pressure banaye. Outpost ko abhi simple tarike se samjho: ek square jo opponent ke pawn ki coverage se door ho aur jahan aapka piece der se nahi nikal sakta. Pieces jab active hote hain, unke moves ek dusre plan ke liye raasta banate hain.

Ek practical scan aise chal sakta hai: pehle king safety, phir weak squares, phir open files aur passed-pawn chances, aur last mein aapke paas material advantage hai ya nahi. Iske baad aap do teen candidate goals soch sakte hain aur unhe opponent ke likely reply ke saath compare kar sakte hain. Abhi koi “winning move” dhoondhne ki zaroorat nahi hai; bas position ka direction samajhna hai.

## Plan ko teen parts mein dekho

### 1. Goal

Goal ko ek short sentence mein likho, jaise “opponent ke kingside ko weak banana hai” ya “rook ko open file par laana hai.” Goal ko grand aur vague mat rakho—achhi goal measurable hoti hai, aur board par kisi square ya piece se connected hoti hai. “Main jeetunga” goal nahi hai kyunki usmein kis raste se jeetna, wo nahi bataya.

### 2. Route

Route woh intermediate steps hain jo goal tak le jaate hain. Agar goal open file par rook lana hai, toh route mein opposing pawn ka exchange, file khaali karna, aur rook ke liye entry square banana ho sakta hai. Route par har move ko “kya isse next move ka option milta hai?” ke saath check karo; random activity ko plan mat samajho.

### 3. Recheck

Opponent ka move aapka plan turant cancel nahi karta, lekin uska direction badal sakta hai. Agar opponent ne aapke weak square ko cover kar diya ya king ke paas defensive pawn le aaya, toh aap naya goal choose kar sakte ho. Yahi flexibility grand-master abstraction nahi, balki practical common sense hai—position ka evidence update hua, toh decision bhi update hona chahiye.

## Ek hi position ke through dekhna

Ab in teen steps ko ek sample position par saath mein dekho. Agar aapka rook inactive hai, pawns aapke king ko cover kar rahe hain, aur opponent ka queenside pawn structure mein gap hai, toh “queenside expansion” goal ban sakta hai. Us route mein pehle appropriate pawn move, phir open file aur rook placement, aur phir weak squares par pressure aata hai. Agar opponent counter-play de raha hai, toh aap apna king active karne ya simplify karne wala alternate plan chuno.

Is type ka checklist hi har game ke 20-second scan ko shape deta hai. Aap har baar sab kuch solve karne ki koshish nahi karte; aap apni position ke liye ek dominant, concrete reason choose karte ho. 1000 rating tak ke target ke liye yeh practical clarity important hai, lekin koi plan bhi rating guarantee nahi karta—practice, calculation aur mistakes ka review bhi result par asar dalte hain.

Ab in concepts ko ek hi nazar se compare karna helpful hai, isliye har column mein sirf us sawaal ka jawab hai jo aap position scan mein poochte hain:

| Scan ka hissa | Aapka sawaal | Practical fayda |
|---|---|---|
| King safety | Kaunsa direct attack abhi hai? | Nuksaan se pehle counterattack ya safety |
| Pawns | Kaunsa weak ya passed pawn hai? | Long-term pressure ka clear reason |
| Activity | Kaunsa piece abhi khel sakta hai? | Open files, outposts aur targets |
| Goal aur route | Next do moves kis point ki taraf le jaate hain? | Focus aur faster decisions |

## What It Is NOT

Middlegame plan **“theory ki ek line yaad karna” nahi hai**. Agar aapko sirf ek opening book ke moves yaad hain, lekin board par unke baad ka reason nahi dikhta, toh aapki preparation abhi tak nayi position mein nahi pohochi. Plan ko har move ke baad board par verify karna hota hai.

Plan **force karna** bhi nahi hota. Agar opponent aapka route rok raha hai aur better defensive choice available hai, toh plan ko adapt karna maturity ka kaam hai. Material ko blindly grab karna, king ko danger mein le jaana, ya sirf “main toh attack kar raha hoon” kehna strategy nahi hai.

Plan **rating ka guarantee** nahi hai. Yeh 45-minute daily practice ke liye ek clear thinking framework hai; aap Lichess games mein ise apply karke decisions improve kar sakte ho, lekin har opponent aur har position alag hoti hai. Is course ka target progress ko structured karna hai, outcome ko promise karna nahi.

Ab 4.1 ka framework aapko direction de raha hai; 4.2 `Pawn Breaks, Open Files aur Space` mein hum dekhenge ki pawns ko move karke lines aur activity kaise badli jaati hai.
