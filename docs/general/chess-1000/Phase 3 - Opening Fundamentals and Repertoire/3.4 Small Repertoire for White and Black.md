Jab `3.3 Pawn Structures aur Traps` ne tumhe pawn shape aur early tactical pattern dekhna sikhaya, ab in observations ko apne actual games ke liye organize karna hai. Iska matlab hai bahut saari openings ratna nahi; ek chhoti, samajhne layak selection rakhna hai jise tum 10+0 mein confidently khol sako.

Jaise roz ke bag mein sab kuch nahi rakhna—phone, wallet, keys, aur ek snacks lekar chalte ho—wahi chess mein har possible opening ke saath nahi rehna. Ek apni taraf ka first move, opponent ke common reply ke liye ek simple plan, aur ek fallback kaafi hain; baaki ideas principles se handle karo.

# 3.4 Small Repertoire for White and Black

**Repertoire** tumhare selected opening choices ka set hai. Chhoti repertoire ka fayda memory aur decision speed dono mein hota hai: jab opponent familiar reply karta hai, tum theory nahi, plan ke hisaab se move karte ho. Yeh 10+0 ke liye practical hai, lekin iska matlab nahi ki tum har opponent ko guarantee se jeetoge.

## Chhoti repertoire ke selection rules

Opening choose karne se pehle chaar practical questions poochho: kya main iska basic plan ek saath samajh sakta hoon, kya common replies board par recognizable hain, kya pieces meri safety ke saath develop hote hain, aur kya main ise 10–15 minutes ke study session mein revisit kar sakta hoon? Engine result, GM name, ya fashionable trend akele mein reason nahi hain.

Ek reliable repertoire kam moves rakhti hai lekin un moves ko flexibility ke saath jaanti hai. Iska matlab fixed engine line copy karna nahi; opponent ke unexpected move par development, centre, king safety, aur structure ke principles par lautna hai. `3.2` ke teen filters yahi safety net banate hain.

## White ke liye chhota starter choice

Meri default recommendation **White: `1. e4` aur Italian family** hai. Opening ke pehlich moves ko aise samajho: `1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4`, aur phir `4. d3` ya `4. O-O` jaise purpose-driven moves. Goal board par note karo: pieces active hoengi, white centre influence karega, aur king early pressure se door consolidate hoga.

Agar tumhe `1. d4` zyada comfortable lage, to `1. d4 d5 2. c4` ko apna White primary bana sakte ho. Uska plan bhi wahi hai: centre aur development ko saath le chalana, queen’s gambit ki deeper theory abhi ratne ki zaroorat nahi. Bas is week ek hi White primary choose karo; do options ko parallel mein nahi kholna.

## Black ke liye chhota starter choice

Meri default recommendation **Black: `1. e4` ke against `1...e5`** hai. Basic replies ko `2...Nf6`, `3...Bc5`, aur jab White `3. Bb5` kare to `3...a6` tak rakho. Har move ke baad ek reason note karo: knight develop ho raha hai, bishop active square par aa rahi hai, aur king ko castle karne ka future space mil raha hai.

Agar tumhe White ke `1. d4` ke against `1...d5` zyada familiar lage, to Black primary ko wahi bana sakte ho. Uske liye `1...d5`, knights aur bishops ki development, aur early king safety ko plan banao; har variant ki book line memorize karne ki zaroorat nahi. Yeh choice tumhari preference aur practice comfort ke hisaab se valid hai.

## Choose exactly one per colour

Final selection rule simple hai: ek White primary aur ek Black primary, phir unke common replies ke saath ek notebook page. Main White ke liye `1. e4` aur Black ke liye `1...e5` suggest karta hoon kyunki 10+0 ke liye simple goals visible hain; tum alternative choose kar sakte ho, bas choice ko explain karna chahiye.

Har card par yeh chaar lines likho: “Main yeh first move kyun karta/karti hoon?”, “Opponent ka common reply kya hai?”, “Next do moves ka plan kya hai?”, “Unexpected move par kaun sa principle use hoga?” Isse repertoire memorize nahi, rehearse hoti hai. Har week 10–15 minutes card revisit karo, phir ek Lichess game mein usi plan ko consciously follow karo.

## Kya memorize karna hai, kya nahi

Memorize karne wali cheezein hain starting move, ek ya do common replies, aur unke baad ka plan. Deep side lines, engine variations, aur har possible trick abhi optional hain. Pehle har move ka board-level reason aur safety consequence samajho; baad mein repertoire ko expand karna ho to ek additional line add karo, poori library nahi.

## What It Is NOT

**Small repertoire** ka matlang ek hi opening ko blindly repeat karna nahi. Iska matlab hai limited, well-understood choices rakhna aur unke baad principles par switch karna.

**Opening choice** ka matlang ek guarantee nahi. Koi line tumhe har game nahi jeetegi; 10+0 mein familiarity se decision time aur confidence improve hota hai, result phir bhi opponent aur execution par depend karega.

## Checkpoint: write your two cards

Aaj do cards banao: White primary aur Black primary. Har card par move sequence ke sirf first few move pairs, ek plan sentence, aur ek fallback principle likho. Phir 45-minute session mein ek Lichess rapid game khel ke note karo ki tumne plan follow kiya ya nahi. `3.5 Exercise Pack` mein hum real opening positions par isi choice ko test karenge.
