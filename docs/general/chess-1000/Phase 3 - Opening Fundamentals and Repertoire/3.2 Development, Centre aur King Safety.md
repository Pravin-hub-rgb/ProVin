Jab `3.1 Openings Are Plans, Not Moves` mein tumne opening ko fixed move list se alag karke plan ki language di, ab us plan ke teen core pieces ko board par dekhna hai. Yehi teen pieces tumhe bataenge ki ek move kyun useful hai, sirf isliye nahi ki woh “opening mein aa gaya.”

Jaise ghar banana ho to pehle foundation, load-bearing walls, aur safe entry point ka dhyan rakhna padta hai; chess mein development, centre, aur king safety wahi structural kaam karte hain. Inko samajhne ke baad opening ki moves ek checklist jaisi lagne lagti hain.

# 3.2 Development, Centre aur King Safety

**Development** ka matlab hai apne knights, bishops, aur rooks ko un active squares par laana jahan woh jaldi kaam kar sakein. Sirf pawn aage badalna development nahi hai; aapke minor pieces, jo knights aur bishops hote hain, unka active placement count hota hai. **Centre** chess mein board ke central area—mainly d aur e files aur char-cross ke kareeb ranks—ka influence hai. **King safety** ka matlab hai early attack se bachne ke liye king ka secure position banana.

## Development: pieces ko engaged karna

Development ko aise samjho jaise team ke players ko bench se court par laana. Knight centre mein aakar pawn aur king ke targets create kar sakta hai; bishop open diagonal par aakar important squares control kar sakta hai. Iska fark sirf piece ki presence nahi, balki woh kaam hai jo piece aage kar sakta hai.

Har move se poochho, “Kya koi undeveloped piece ab bhi bench par hai?” Agar haan, to agla move development ko prioritize karna practical hai. `1. e4` ke baad `2. Nf3` isliye natural lagta hai kyunki knight active hota hai aur future castling ke liye raasta khaali rehta hai. Har opening exact same move nahi maangti, lekin development ka principle flexible rahega.

## Centre: pawns se zyada, influence

Centre ko sirf do central pawns se occupied karke measure mat karo. Centre control ka matlab central squares par attack, influence, aur future entry possibilities hain. White ke liye d4 ya e4 pawns, aur unke knights aur bishops ke lines, central space ko naturally support kar sakte hain.

Aksar ek central pawn ko jaldi advance karna development aur king safety se pehle aa jaata hai. 10+0 mein `e4` ya `d4` jaisa centre move accha starting idea ho sakta hai, lekin uske baad development complete karna zaroori hai. Agar pawn aage gaya aur king open reh gaya, to centre ne tumhe space diya hai lekin safety ka bill bhi bheja hai.

## King safety: castle jaldi, imagine clearly

King ko beech mein lambe time nahi rakhna chahiye, jab tak opponent ke pieces centre aur open files par attack kar rahe hain. **Castling** king aur rook ko ek move mein safely reposition karta hai, provided path clear ho aur king check mein na ho. Iska immediate purpose sirf king ko corner se nikalna nahi, future rook coordination aur safety bhi hai.

Castling ko “magic shield” samajhne se bachein. Agar king ke aage open file, enemy bishop ka diagonal, ya attacked square ho, to castling ke baad bhi trouble aa sakta hai. Safe castling ke baad pawn structure aur piece coordination par focus karna padta hai; safety ek moment ka move nahi, continuing habit hai.

## Ek practical opening loop

Har position par teen sawal repeat karo: kaun sa piece develop karega, centre ka kaun sa square influence karega, aur king ke liye next safe square kya hai? In teenon ka jawab ek move mein perfect fit na ho to bhi move ka primary purpose identify karo. Isse tum move ko “acha lagta hai” se “ismein development, centre, ya safety ka kaam hai” par shift karte ho.

Board par annotations se kaam karo: ek arrow development ke liye, ek cross unsafe king line par, aur ek note “centre” ke liye. Har opening book ke pehle do moves annotate karo, phir opponent ke ek simple reply ke baad dobara check karo. Yeh exercise `3.5 Exercise Pack` mein positions par aayega.

## What It Is NOT

**Centre** ka matlang sirf centre ke pawns ko aage badalna nahi. Influence, open lines, aur future piece entry bhi centre control mein aate hain; kabhi pawn galat timing se advance karke centre control kharab kar sakta hai.

**King safety** ka matlang king ko hamesha back rank par rakhna nahi. Safety ka practical test hai: kya king ke around aur uski retreat squares par attack ka pressure manageable hai? Development aur safety ko balance karna part of the plan hai.

## Checkpoint: teen goals, ek sentence

Apne last Lichess opening ko revisit karo aur uske 10 moves ke saath teen columns likho: development, centre, king safety. Har column mein sirf ek ya do board examples rakho, poori theory nahi. Jahan koi move purpose serve nahi karta, us move ko “explain ya replace” ke liye mark karo.
