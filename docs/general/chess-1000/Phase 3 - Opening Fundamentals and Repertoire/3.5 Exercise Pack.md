# 3.5 Exercise Pack

Yeh chhah positions opening ke different stages cover karti hain: initial development, castling, a standard response, a pawn-structure decision, a defensive development move, aur ek early trap. Har answer mein move, short line, reason, aur common mistake hai—taaki pattern sirf theory sentence na ban, practical decision ban sake.

## Position 1 — First development move

Yeh starting position hai, isliye ismein tactical calculation nahi chahiye. White ko apni pehli opening principle apply karni hai: ek piece ko active square par laao aur future king safety ke liye raasta kholo. Move se pehle poochho kaun sa knight abhi bhi development se bacha hua hai.

![Chess position 1: first development move](/diagrams/chess/phase-3/phase-3-opening-01.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 0 1`

**Time limit / task:** 45 seconds. `1. e4` aur `1. Nf3` jaise simple options soch ke ek primary White move choose karo, uska development aur centre reason likho.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `1. e4!`

**Short line:** `1. e4 e5 2. Nf3`

**Reasoning:** White centre influence ke liye `e4` karta hai aur Black ke common reply ke baad `Nf3` knight ko active square par le aata hai. `1. Nf3` bhi reasonable starter ho sakta hai, lekin recommended `1. e4` plan mein centre aur development ko saath build karna hai. Is position ka goal pawn ko sirf aage badalna nahi, future plan banana hai.

**Common mistake:** `e4` ko “best opening move” samajh kar baad ke development aur king safety ko ignore karna. First move ka reason board ke agle do moves se connect karna zaroori hai.

</details>

## Position 2 — Castle before danger

Yeh position `1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4` ke baad hai. White ke paas development ke options hain, lekin king abhi centre mein hai aur Black ke future attack ka time bhi badh sakta hai. Castling ko sirf notation mein nahi, king ko safe future square aur rook coordination ke roop mein socho.

![Chess position 2: castle before danger](/diagrams/chess/phase-3/phase-3-opening-02.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `r1bqkbnr/pppp1ppp/2n5/4p3/2B5/5N2/PPPP1PPP/RNBQK2R w KQkq - 4 4`

**Time limit / task:** 90 seconds. `O-O`, `d3`, aur `Nc3` jaise candidates compare karo; king safety aur future activity ke basis par ek move choose karo.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `4. O-O!`

**Short line:** `4. O-O Nf6`

**Reasoning:** Bishop c4 aur knight f3 develop ho chuke hain, f1/g1 ka rook path clear hai, aur king ko early board se consolidate karna practical hai. Castling ke baad rook f1 centre activity aur king g1 safety ke saath cooperate kar sakta hai. `d3` bhi structure ka move hai, lekin current king-safety goal ke liye castling clearer hai.

**Common mistake:** Har move par centre pawn ko aage badal dena aur king ko `O-O` ke liye bahut deri se rakhna. King safety ko ek baar ki checkbox nahi, opening loop ka ongoing part maano.

</details>

## Position 3 — Practical response to 3. Bb5

Yeh position Ruy Lopez ke pehle move ke baad hai: `1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bb5`. Black ko abhi grand theory book kholne ki zaroorat nahi; ek simple pawn response se bishop ko neutralise karke baad `Ba4` par active bishop develop kar sakta hai. Move ka reason capture se zyada future activity aur safe development hai.

![Chess position 3: Ruy Lopez pawn response](/diagrams/chess/phase-3/phase-3-opening-03.svg)

**Side to move:** Black

**FEN:** `r1bqkbnr/pppp1ppp/2n5/1B2p3/4P3/5N2/PPPP1PPP/RNBQK2R b KQkq - 4 4`

**Time limit / task:** 90 seconds. `...a6`, `...Nf6`, aur `...d6` ke immediate board purposes compare karo; ek practical response aur ek reason choose karo.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `3... a6`

**Short line:** `3... a6 4. Ba4 Nf6`

**Reasoning:** `...a6` bishop ko `Ba4` par retreat karne ka practical option deta hai, jahan woh active diagonal par rahegi. Black ka pawn move threat bhi create karta hai aur development ka immediate rush nahi karta. Line ke baad Black `...Nf6` se knight bhi develop kar sakta hai; full Ruy Lopez theory ki zaroorat nahi.

**Common mistake:** Sirf `...a6` ko “Ruy Lopez response” label karke copy karna aur bishop retreat ya future knight development ka reason na sochna. Move tab samajh aata hai jab board par uska next purpose dikhta hai.

</details>

## Position 4 — Develop with the structure in mind

Yeh position `1. e4 c5 2. Nf3 d6 3. d4 cxd4 4. Nxd4 Nf6 5. Nc3 a6` ke baad hai. White ke paas open-ish central choices aur developed knights hain; task rapid opening principle choose karna hai, koi deep engine line nahi. Candidate ko dekh ke poochho woh piece activity improve karta hai aur Black ke next centre challenge future mein kya karta hai.

![Chess position 4: develop after the central exchange](/diagrams/chess/phase-3/phase-3-opening-04.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `rnbqkb1r/pp2pppp/3p1n2/8/3N4/2N5/PP3PPP/R1BQKB1R w KQkq - 0 6`

**Time limit / task:** 2 minutes. `Bc4`, `Be3`, aur koi simple knight move compare karo; ek development plan aur structure ka ek common consequence note karo.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `6. Bc4!`

**Short line:** `6. Bc4 e5`

**Reasoning:** Bishop f1 c4 active diagonal par aata hai aur f7 target ke kareeb future activity paata hai. `...e5` Black ka central challenge ho sakta hai, lekin White ne pehle undeveloped bishop ko purposeful square par bheja; isse pieces ka kaam board par dikhta hai. `Be3` bhi solid choice ho sakta hai, lekin `Bc4` central aiming aur development ka simple plan deta hai.

**Common mistake:** Har move par `d5` ya koi central break choose karna, jabki bishop abhi bhi bench par hai. Structure ko samajhne ke liye ek undeveloped piece ko active karna bhi ek strong structural plan ho sakta hai.

</details>

## Position 5 — Develop a defender, not a panic move

Yeh position `1. d4 d5 2. c4 e6 3. Nc3 Nf6` ke baad hai. Black ko aggressive attack chase karne ke bajad apna undeveloped bishop settle karna hai; `...Be7` ek simple, repeatable principle-based move hai. Is exercise mein deep Queen's Indian theory nahi, defensive development aur king safety ka order check karna hai.

![Chess position 5: defensive development](/diagrams/chess/phase-3/phase-3-opening-05.svg)

**Side to move:** Black

**FEN:** `rnbqkb1r/pp3ppp/4pn2/8/2PP4/2N5/PP2PPPP/R1B1KB1R b KQkq - 2 4`

**Time limit / task:** 90 seconds. `...Be7`, `...dxc4`, aur `...O-O` jaise options ke short purposes compare karo; ek defensive development move choose karo.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `3... Be7!`

**Short line:** `3... Be7 4. Bg5 O-O`

**Reasoning:** Bishop f8 `e7` par undeveloped defender ko settle karta hai aur future `...O-O` ke liye f8 square khaali karta hai. Isse Black development aur king safety ke plan ko saath build hota hai. `...dxc4` bhi material change la sakta hai, lekin `...Be7` ke liye clear reason aur future structure plan hai.

**Common mistake:** `...dxc4` ko isliye turant “best” samajh lena kyunki woh capture karta hai, aur bishop development ko ignore karna. Immediate capture se pehle piece development aur future break compare karna chahiye.

</details>

## Position 6 — Trap or just a position?

Aakhri position `1. e4 e5 2. Bc4 Nc6 3. Qh5 Nf6` ke baad hai. White ko ek tempting-looking queen capture mil raha hai, lekin answer tabhi strong hai jab check, protection, aur king escape verify kiye jayein. Yeh trap recognition ka exercise hai, iska matlab nahi ki `3. Qh5` har game mein best opening move hai.

![Chess position 6: scholar's mate pattern](/diagrams/chess/phase-3/phase-3-opening-06.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `r1bqkb1r/pppp1ppp/2n2n2/4p2Q/2B1P3/8/PPPP1PPP/RNB1K1NR w KQkq - 1 4`

**Time limit / task:** 2 minutes. Queen capture ko check aur mate notation ke saath test karo; ek alternative safe move bhi socho, phir common beginner error explain karo.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `4. Qxf7#`

**Short line:** `4. Qxf7#`

**Reasoning:** Queen h5 f7 pawn ko diagonal par capture karti hai aur king e8 ko check deti hai. Bishop c4 f7 ko protect karta hai, aur black king ke escape squares ya occupied hain ya queen control mein hain. Isliye `...Nf6` ne queen ko capture hone se rokne ke bajay trap complete kya; pattern ka reason samajhna zyada important hai.

**Common mistake:** Queen capture ko sirf isliye choose karna kyunki woh free piece lag rahi hai, protection aur king escape verify kiye bina. Is exercise ka lesson yahin hai: trap ko attractive move aur real tactic bechkar confirm karo.

</details>

## What It Is NOT

Is pack mein “best move” ka matlab har position ka only theoretically perfect move nahi; kamal minimum clarity aur ek board-level opening reason hai. `Bc4`, `...a6`, aur `Qxf7#` jaise examples apne plan ko illustrate karte hain, koi fixed rating result promise nahi karte.

Opening principles ko ratna kaafi nahi hai. Agar tum move ka reason nahi bata sakte, to use copy mat karo; diagram dobara dekh kar development, centre, safety, ya structure ke evidence se apna explanation banao.
