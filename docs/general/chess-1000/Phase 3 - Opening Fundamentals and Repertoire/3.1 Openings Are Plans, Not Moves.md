# 3.1 Openings Are Plans, Not Moves

**Opening** wo shuruaati sequence hai jismein tum apne pieces ko battlefield ke liye tayyar karte ho, center par control banate ho, aur king ko early danger se door rakhte ho. **Opening plan** ka matlab hai ek practical purpose jo tumhare next do moves, yaani tumhare agle do decisions, ko direction deta hai. **Repertoire** ka simple matlab hai tumhare liye tayyar kuch opening choices ka selected set.

Opening ko ratna aur opening ko samajhna alag hai. Ratne wala player `1. e4` ke baad har move ek fixed sequence ki tarah copy karta hai; samajhne wala player dekh leta hai ki us move se center, development, king safety, ya space mein kya badla. 10+0 mein fixed theory ki stack se zyada practical, repeatable plan chahiye.

## Opening ke teen immediate kaam

Pehla kaam **development** hai: knights aur bishops ko un squares se laana jahan woh active kaam kar sakein, aur unke saath opening ke baad rook ko support mil sake. Doosra kaam **centre control** hai: central files aur ranks par control banana; centre par pawn khade hona zaroori nahi, centre ko influence karna bhi useful hai. Teesra kaam **king safety** hai: king ko early attack se bachana, jisse castling ya equivalent safe setup jaldi mile.

In teen kaam ko ek hi move mein achieve karna har baar possible nahi hai. Isliye jab bhi koi move khud se yeh poochho: “Yeh move development, centre, king safety, ya future flexibility mein se kaun sa kaam behtar karta hai?” Jawab ek hi ho, to move ka practical purpose clear hai.

## Plan sentence likho

Opening ko memorize karne se pehle ek **plan sentence** likho. Ismein apna immediate goal, next do moves ka role, aur opponent ke likely response ka ek basic handle hona chahiye. Yahan “next do moves” se tumhare apne next do decisions matlab hain, ek White move aur ek Black move nahi. Example sentence aise ho sakta hai: “White `1. e4` ke baad `2. Nf3`, phir `3. Bc4`, aur development ke baad kingside castling karega; Black centre ko challenge kar sakta hai.”

Plan sentence fixed truth nahi hai. Agar opponent ne `2... Nc6` nahi khela, to tumhara bishop route badal sakta hai, as long as development aur king safety ka larger goal maintain rahe. Yahi flexibility deep opening theory se pehle aati hai.

## 45-minute routine

Opening study ko ratne ki cram session mat banao. 10 minutes ek opening position ka board set karo, 15 minutes do possible plans likho, 10 minutes Lichess analysis board par moves test karo, aur 10 minutes apne notes mein ek “if opponent does this, I do that” line likho. Rating target ek direction hai, guarantee nahi; repeatable study aur real games dono feedback dete hain.

## What It Is NOT

**Opening plan** ka matlab ek exact move sequence ya har opponent move ki book line nahi. Plan aise decisions guide karta hai jo position ke goals se match karein.

**Repertoire** ka matlab “sab famous openings yaad rakhna” nahi. Chhoti repertoire ka matlab hai kam options ko samajhna, kuch clear responses practice karna, aur baaki situations mein principles par laut aana.

## Checkpoint: apni opening sentence

Abhi apne liye ek White aur ek Black candidate opening family choose karo, lekin poori theory yaad karne ki koshish mat karo. Har colour ke liye ek hi sentence likho: “Main yeh goal achieve karna chahta hoon, yeh opening move next step hai, aur likely response ke baad development, centre, king safety, ya future flexibility mein se kaunsi value maintain karunga?” Is sentence ko dobara padho; agar opponent ke har move ke exact reply yaad nahi aa rahe, to tum plan ke bajay sirf sequence rat rahe ho.
