# 3.5 Quiz - Opening Fundamentals and Repertoire

## What It Is NOT

Yeh quiz opening ko engine-level perfect karke prove karne ki test nahi hai. Iska matlab hai fundamentals samajh aaye hain, common choices predictable hain, aur unexpected reply par development, centre, safety, ya structure ke principles use kar sakte ho. Rating target ko guarantee mat samajhna.

3. Pawn structure ne tumhari move evaluation ko kaise badla?

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Sample Answer:** Weak pawns, open files, aur supported/unsupported squares future targets aur routes banate hain. Main structure ko immediate capture ke saath evaluate karta hoon, taaki move ke baad ka board sirf superficially attractive na lage. Isse development aur future activity ka connection samajh aata hai.

</details>

4. Early trap ko memorize karne se kaise bachte ho?

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Sample Answer:** Main sequence ke saath reason bhi store karta hoon: exposed king, undefended target, ya forced capture ka pattern. Naya move aate hi protection aur escape verify karta hoon. Isse pattern transfer hota hai, sirf same moves repeat nahi hote.

</details>

## MCQ

Q: Opening plan ka sabse practical definition kya hai?
A. Har opponent move ka exact reply ratna
B. Ek goal aur uske practical moves ka connected explanation, jo unexpected replies par adjust ho sakta hai
C. Sirf engine ki pehli recommendation copy karna
D. Opening ke pehle teen moves ke baad pawn push karna
> B. Plan ek direction aur purpose deta hai; move sequence fixed nahi hoti. Isliye opponent unexpected reply de to fundamental principles ke saath plan adjust ho sakta hai.

---

Q: `O-O` ka main opening purpose kya hai?
A. King ko permanently attack-free banana
B. Centre ke sab pawns ko develop karna
C. King ko safer area le jaana aur future rook coordination ke liye setup karna
D. Opponent ka right se attack karna
> C. Castling king ko safer area aur rook ke saath future coordination deta hai. Yeh permanent shield nahi, isliye weak squares aur open files baad mein bhi monitor karne padte hain.

---

Q: Deliberately small repertoire ka fayda kya hai?
A. Har opening par expert-level theory load karna
B. Kam choices ko samajhkar time aur decision clarity save karna
C. Har opponent ko ek hi move sequence mein beat karna
D. Opening ko skip karna
> B. Kam, well-understood choices 10+0 mein familiar decision aur plan follow karne mein madad karte hain. Result phir bhi opponent aur execution par depend karega.

---

Q: `2. e4` ke baad `2. Nf3` jaise move ko sabse practical kyun samajh sakte hain?
A. Kyunki woh hamesha engine best hai
B. Kyunki woh knight ko active karke future castling aur development support karta hai
C. Kyunki pawn move hamesha better hota hai
D. Kyunki bishop ko immediately capture karta hai
> B. Knight development, centre plan, aur king-safety setup ko saath support karta hai. Yeh particular line ka universal guarantee nahi, lekin board-level reason clear aur repeatable hai.

## TrueFalse

Q: Chhoti repertoire ka matlang hai tum sirf ek line repeat karo aur opponent ke unexpected move par principles ignore karo.
- True
- False
> False. Chhoti repertoire limited choices ko rehearse karti hai, lekin unexpected reply par development, centre, king safety, aur structure ke principles par lautna zaroori hai.

---

Q: Pawn structure ka practical use sirf labels yaad rakhna hai.
- True
- False
> False. Structure ka use weak squares, open files, future targets, aur king-safety consequences ko board par evaluate karna hai. Labels sirf pehchaan shuru hain, decision nahi.

## ShortAnswer

Q: Apni chosen White repertoire ka plan explain karo: first move, common reply, next development idea, aur unexpected reply par kya principle use karoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> Main White ke liye `1. e4` ko primary first move rakh sakta hoon. Common reply `1...e5` ke baad `2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4` jaise development aur `O-O` ka plan note karunga. Agar opponent koi different reply kare, to exact book line ke bajad active pieces, centre control, king safety, aur pawn structure evaluate karke plan adjust karunga. Yeh small repertoire ka repeatable reason hai, engine ki guarantee nahi.

</details>

Q: Development, centre control, king safety, aur pawn structure ko ek practical opening loop mein kaise use karoge?

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> Main har move par poochhta hoon kaun sa undeveloped piece active ho sakta hai, centre ka kaun sa square influence hoga, aur king ka next safe square kya hai. Pawn move ke baad weak squares, open files, aur possible opponent target bhi note karta hoon. Is loop se move ka immediate aur future purpose dono evaluate hote hain, jabki long theory memorize karne ki zaroorat nahi padti.

</details>
