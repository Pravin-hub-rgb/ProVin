# 0.3 Daily Routine aur Game Log

## 45-minute routine

Roz 45 minute ko paanch focused blocks mein todna, 45 minute ki scattered study se zyada useful hota hai. Har block ka ek clear purpose hoga, aur har block ke end mein tumhe pata chalega agla kya karna hai. Isse har game ke baad “ab kya karoon?” wala moment kam hota hai.

Pehle paanch minute board-scan warm-up ke liye rakho. Kisi bhi position par opponent ke king, checks, captures, aur threats ko dhoondho; abhi calculation kiye bina bhi ek immediate observation likhne ki koshish karo. Is habit ko practice games mein automatically call karna hai.

Agle das minute focused tactics ke liye hain. Aaj ka pattern choose karo — jaise knight capture, pin, ya back-rank idea — aur 3–5 positions solve karo. Har answer ke saath ek reason likho, taaki tum sirf move guess na karo.

Phir 25 minute practical games ke liye rakho. Lichess par ek ya do 10+0 games khelo, aur agar game length se 25 minute cross ho jaayen to us session ko review ke saath next day complete karo. Chhathe minute mein ek chhota log likho: result, ek critical moment, reason, aur kal ka ek action.

## Game log kyun zaroori hai

Jaise cricket mein sirf score dekhne se pata nahi chalta kaunsi shot costly thi, waise chess mein sirf win/loss dekhne se pata nahi chalta kaunsa move future games ka ban ban gaya. Log tumhare brain ko “maine ek game khel li” ke bajaye “maine ek specific problem observe ki” ka evidence deta hai. Wohi evidence weekly changes banane layak hota hai.

Har game ke liye chhota entry rakhna kaafi hai, essay nahi likhna. Lichess ke game review mein move number aur ek screenshot dekh sakte ho, lekin ek board se tumhara decision clear likhna sabse zyada reusable feedback hota hai. Example entry: “12th move queen ko free piece diya, maine opponent ka rook capture nahi dekha; kal har exchange pehle recapture check karunga.”

Log ka purpose blame nahi hai. “Galat move khela” likhne se agla step clear hota hai, lekin “main weak player hoon” likhne se kuch nahi hota. Har mistake ko ek observable action aur ek counter-action mein badlo, jaise “board scan nahi kiya” ko “opponent ka last move padh kar move choose karunga” se replace karo.

## Ek practical log shape

Log format simple rakho taaki daily fill karna possible ho. Har game ke baad ek minute se zyada likhne ki zaroorat nahi, lekin reason aur next action blank na chhodein.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Result | Loss, 10+0 |
| Position or move | 12. Nf6, board mein queen ka attack miss hua |
| Reason | Main opponent ka rook capture nahi check kiya |
| Habit | Hanging piece ko free dena |
| Next action | Exchange se pehle recapture aur king safety scan |

Week ke end mein entries ko do categories mein baanto: “mujhe baar-baar kya dikha” aur “kal kis action ko test karna hai.” Agar har game alag story ho, to current phase ki koi specific gap shayad clear nahi hai. Repeated phrase ko ek weekly goal bana lo.

## Benefits aur tradeoff

Routine tumhe daily entry karke “aaj kuch nahi hua” feeling se bachati hai, kyunki 5-minute drill aur 5-minute log bhi counted progress hain. Consistency se tactical recognition improve hoti hai aur real games mein decision routine strengthen hoti hai. Iska fayda long-term hai: rating ka momentary number badhne ki zaroorat nahi, decision habits reliable ho rahi hain.

Tradeoff yeh hai ki routine boring lag sakti hai, aur ek game ka detailed analysis tumhe 45-minute session se bahar le sakta hai. Isliye har game ka full chapter likhne ke bajaye ek critical moment choose karo. Har week ek deeper review karo, baaki days quick log rakho; quality aur quantity dono zaroori hain.

## What It Is NOT

Daily routine ka matlab har din maximum mushkil problem solve karna nahi. Iska matlab hai ek small, repeatable system ko preserve karna — aur us system ke andar difficulty badhana.

Game log analysis replacement nahi hai. Agar log ke bina tum move ka reason nahi samajhte, to engine ya phase notes se concept dobara seekho; sirf result likhne se tactical gap fix nahi hota.

## Seven-day checkpoint

Agle saat din yeh routine poora karke dekho: kam se kam paanch logged games, do positions ke written reasons, aur ek repeat hui mistake ki clear counter-action. Agar routine miss hui, blame ke bajaye agle din half-size session karke chain wapas laao. Target consistency hai, perfection nahi.
