# 2.1 Candidate Moves Kaise Bante Hain

Chess mein **calculation** ka simple matlab hai apne move ko dekhna aur samajhna ki opponent ke liye sabse zyada important reply kya ho. **Candidate move** wo move hai jo tumhare position ke plan aur board ki geometry dono se fit hota hai. Tumhare dimag mein 5–10 realistic candidates rakhna beginner ke liye kaafi hai; har possible move ko list karna zaroori nahi.

Board par ek familiar routine jaise traffic dekhne wala rasta use karo. Pehle aage ka immediate danger dekho, phir tumhare pawns aur pieces ke saamne ka raasta dekho, aur phir ek move jo future threat banaye us par socho. Is order ko bahar yaad karne ki zaroorat nahi; practice ke saath natural ho jayega.

## Pehle teen filters

Pehla filter **check** hai. Agar koi move check deta hai, to opponent ko legal reply dena padta hai, isliye tumhara dimag us move par focused ho sakta hai. Doosra filter **capture** hai, lekin har capture automatically accha nahi hota; calculate karo ki capture ke baad tumhara piece bachega ya nahi.

Teesra filter **threat** hai. Threat matlab tumhara move aage kisi capture, check, ya piece-entry ka possibility banata hai. Agar teenon filters se koi move clearly standout karta hai, us par short line calculate karo; baaki candidates ko turant discard mat karo, bas priority do.

## Practical 30-second routine

Position aate hi pehle apne king ko scan karo. King ko koi immediate attack mil raha hai? Agar haan, to escape ya block karna pehla kaam hai. Agar nahi, to aapke available checks, captures, aur attacks note karo.

Ab do ya teen candidates ko board par compare karo. Har candidate ke liye bas teen sawal poochho: opponent ka best reply kya hai, meri move ke baad kaamyaab piece kahan jayega, aur kya maine apne king ko ya kisi critical piece ko extra danger mein daal diya? Iske baad best-looking candidate par ek ya do forcing moves tak jaao.

Ek practical line aise likh sakte ho:

`1. Qxf7+ Kd8 2. ...`

Yahan `Qxf7+` check aur capture dono hai, isliye opponent ke legal replies limited hain. `...` ka matlab hai tum abhi poori game calculate nahi kar rahe; tum sirf next decision ke liye position ko reopen kar rahe ho. 10+0 mein itna time kaafi hota hai jab position mein ek clear tactical moment milta hai.

## Candidate list ko chhota kaise rakhein

Har position mein roshni jaisi sab directions ek saath dekhne ki koshish mat karo. Paanch candidates ke liye board par tick-mark type ka mental note rakho: `Check`, `Capture`, `Threat`, `Safe`, `Improve`. Isse tumhara focus badhta hai aur calculation random tree nahi banti.

Ek move ko discard karne ka reason bhi yaad rakho. Agar `f3` bishop ko open karta hai lekin king unsafe chhod deta hai, to woh move abhi candidate list se hat jata hai. **Quiet move** ka matlab hai woh move jo check nahi deta aur capture nahi karta; phir bhi uska immediate positional effect important ho sakta hai. Quiet move ka future value compare karna padega, aur woh detail hum `2.4 Quiet Moves Ka Evaluation` mein dekhenge.

## What It Is NOT

**Candidate moves** ka matlab sirf “jo moves legal hain” nahi hai. Har legal move aapka serious consideration nahi hai; candidate move board ki current need se relevant hona chahiye.

**Calculation** ka matlab yeh bhi nahi ki aapko 15 plies, yaani dono players ke kareeb 15 individual moves, tak har possible reply yaad rakhni hai. Yeh course ke liye calculation chhoti, practical lines par based hai: move, opponent reply, immediate result, aur ek basic safety check.
