# 2.5 Quiz - Calculation and Position Choice

## MCQ

Q: Tumhare position mein 20 legal moves hain. Phase 2 ke liye sabse practical approach kya hai?
A. 20 moves ko equal importance ke saath 10 plies tak calculate karna
B. Checks, captures, threats, king danger, aur important improvements se 2–5 realistic candidates shortlist karna
C. Sirf ek engine recommendation blindly follow karna
D. Sirf woh move chunna jo opponent ko check deta hai
> B. Calculation ka beginner-friendly target limited candidate list aur short replies hota hai. Har legal move ka equal weight se calculation waste ho sakti hai; safety aur position ki need ke hisaab se priority better hai.

---

Q: Kaun si baat move ko forcing hone ke liye sabse zaroori hai?
A. Woh check, capture, ya threat hona
B. Opponent ke meaningful legal replies materially restrict hona
C. Uske baad sabse lambi engine line yaad hona
D. Woh har possible position mein engine best hona
> B. Check, capture, ya threat forcing move ko describe kar sakte hain, lekin automatically forcing nahi banate. Defining test hai ki opponent ke meaningful choices kitne kam aur kitne clearly defined ho gaye.

---

Q: `Qe5` check block karta hai, lekin rook ke paas `Rxe5+` hai. Tum kya karoge?
A. `Qe5` isliye khologe kyunki woh legal hai
B. Block ko safety ke saath evaluate karoge aur queen ko capture hone se bachane wala legal move choose karoge
C. King ke current check ko ignore karoge
D. Opponent ka reply dekhne ke liye move ke baad wait karoge
> B. Legal block sufficient safety nahi hai. `Rxe5+` counterattack dikhata hai ki blocker unprotected hai, isliye best move ko immediate risk ke bina choose karna hoga.

---

Q: Quiet move ka primary purpose kya ho sakta hai?
A. Turant opponent ko check dena
B. Piece ki activity, safety, ya structure ko future ke liye behtar karna jab move check ya capture na kare
C. Board par har square ko ek jaisa banana
D. Long calculation ko hamesha avoid karna, chahe opportunity mile
> B. Quiet move slow-looking ho sakta hai, lekin piece ko active square, king ko safety, ya pawns ko stable shape de sakta hai. Uska value future board snapshot mein dikhta hai.

## TrueFalse

Q: Yeh course ke liye calculation ka matlab hai ki humans ko har baar long engine lines calculate karni chahiye.
- True
- False
> False. Practical calculation candidate moves, short forcing lines, safety, aur immediate evaluation par focused hai. Long exhaustive lines 10+0 ke beginner decision ke liye zaroori nahi hain.

---

Q: Koi forcing move hamesha best move hota hai, chahe woh piece ko free capture de de.
- True
- False
> False. Forcing move opponent ke choices ko restrict karta hai, lekin safety ya material evaluation ke bina best nahi hota. Move opponent ke forcing counterattack ko expose kar sakta hai.

## ShortAnswer

Q: Apne words mein Phase 2 ka practical calculation routine explain karo: candidate, opponent reply, safety, aur evaluation ka order kya hai?

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> Main pehle king scan karta hoon aur immediate danger note karta hoon. Phir checks, captures, threats, aur important improvements se do ya teen realistic candidates choose karta hoon. Har candidate ke baad opponent ke strongest reply dekh kar moved piece, material, aur future activity evaluate karta hoon. Long line tabhi extend karta hoon jab forcing sequence ka result clear ho; warna pause karke nayi position par wapas shortlist banata hoon.

</details>

Q: Forcing move aur quiet move ke beech practical difference samjhao, aur ek-ek example do.

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> Forcing move opponent ke legal replies ko meaningfully restrict karta hai; `1. Nd6+ Kf8 2. Nxc8` mein check se Black ke king replies limited hain, aur har reply ka material result verify karna padta hai. Quiet move check nahi deta aur capture nahi karta, lekin activity, safety, ya structure improve kar sakta hai; `1. Kd6` pawn-endgame example mein king ko key square par rakhta hai. Dono ke baad safety aur position evaluation zaroori hai.

</details>
