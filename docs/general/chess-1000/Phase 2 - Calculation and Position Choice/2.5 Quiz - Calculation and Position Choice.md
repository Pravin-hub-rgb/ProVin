Yeh quiz calculation ki engine accuracy test nahi hai. Iska matlab hai tumne short, practical lines aur safety questions ko samajha hai, na ki har position ka full theory proof yaad hai. Answer chhootne se pehle apna reason mentally bol lo, phir option select karo.

## MCQ

Q: Tumhare position mein 20 legal moves hain. Phase 2 ke liye sabse practical approach kya hai?
A. 20 moves ko equal importance ke saath 10 ply tak calculate karna
B. Checks, captures, threats, king danger, aur important improvements se 2–5 realistic candidates shortlist karna
C. Sirf ek engine recommendation blindly follow karna
D. Sirf woh move chunna jo opponent ko check deta hai
> B. Calculation ka beginner-friendly target limited candidate list aur short forcing replies hota hai. Har legal move ka equal weight se calculation waste ho sakti hai; safety aur position ki need ke hisaab se priority better hai.

---

Q: `1. Nd6+` ke baad opponent ke legal replies dekhna forcing line ka kaunsa hissa hai?
A. Sirf visual aesthetic check karna
B. Sirf capture count karna, king safety ignore karna
C. Opponent ke limited replies enumerate karke check, capture, aur next decision ka result evaluate karna
D. Har possible future move ka engine-level proof likhna
> C. Forcing move ke baad opponent ke legal replies count hote hain, aur tum unke immediate results compare karte ho. Yeh calculation ko short aur decision-oriented rakhta hai.

---

Q: `Qe5` check block karta hai, lekin rook ke paas `Rxe5+` hai. Tum kya karoge?
A. Sirf isliye `Qe5` khologe kyunki woh legal hai
B. Block ko safety ke saath evaluate karoge aur unsafe piece ko protect karne wala move choose karoge
C. King ka current check ignore karoge
D. Opponent ka reply dekhne ke liye move ke baad wait karoge
> B. Legal block sufficient safety nahi hai. `Rxe5+` jala counterattack dikhata hai ki blocker unprotected hai, isliye best move ko immediate risk ke bina choose karna hoga.

---

Q: Quiet move ka primary purpose kya ho sakta hai?
A. Sirf opponent ko check dena
B. Piece ki activity, safety, ya structure ko future ke liye behtar karna jab immediate check/capture nahi hai
C. Board par har square ko ek jaisa banana
D. Long calculation ko hamesha avoid karna, chahe opportunity mile
> B. Quiet move slow-looking ho sakta hai, lekin piece ko active square, king ko safety, ya pawns ko stable shape de sakta hai. Uska value future board snapshot mein dikhta hai.

## TrueFalse

Q: Yeh course ke liye calculation ka matlab humans ko har baad long engine lines calculate karni chahiye.
- True
- False
> False. Practical calculation candidate moves, short forcing lines, safety, aur immediate evaluation par focused hai. Long exhaustive lines 10+0 ke beginner decision ke liye zaroori nahi hain.

---

Q: Koi forcing move hamesha best move hota hai, chahe woh piece ko free capture de de.
- True
- False
> False. Forcing move opponent ke choices kam karta hai, lekin safety ya material evaluation ke bina best nahi hota. Aapka move opponent ke forcing counterattack ko expose kar sakta hai.

## ShortAnswer

Q: Apne words mein Phase 2 ka practical calculation routine explain karo: candidate, opponent reply, safety, aur evaluation ka order kya hai?

Q: Forcing move aur quiet move ke beech practical difference samjhao, aur ek-ek example do.

<details>
<summary>Sample Answer</summary>

> Forcing move check, capture, ya direct threat se opponent ke choices ko seedha restrict karta hai; example `1. Nd6+ Kf8 2. Nxc8` mein knight king aur rook target karta hai. Quiet move immediate check ya capture nahi karta, lekin piece activity, king safety, ya pawn structure improve karta hai; `1. Kd6` key endgame example mein king ko future ke liye better square deta hai. Dono ke baad safety aur position evaluation zaroori hai.

</details>
