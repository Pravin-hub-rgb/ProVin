Jab `2.4 Quiet Moves Ka Evaluation` mein tumne candidate moves ko future board snapshot ke saath compare karna seekha, ab exercise pack unhi skills ko chhoti legal positions par lagayega. Har position mein engine-style lambi calculation nahi hogi; tumhe candidate, opponent reply, safety, aur ek practical evaluation likhna hai.

Har position ko 60–90 seconds ke liye solve karo. Pehle apna best move note karo, phir move ke baad ka ek opponent reply socho, aur tabhi neeche wala answer accordion kholna. Isse answer dekhna active practice nahi, pattern recognition nahi banega—exact reason.

Har position ka FEN us board ka text description hai jise diagram mein White ki perspective se dikhaya gaya hai. FEN ko aise padhna: piece placement, side to move, castling, en passant, aur move counters. Abhi focus piece placement par rakho; exercise ke liye counters ko yaad rakhne ki zaroorat nahi.

# 2.5 Exercise Pack

Is pack ki positions dheere-dheere mushkil hoti hain. Pehle do mein ek clear tactic hai, beech ke do mein candidate ko safety ke saath check karna hai, aur aakhri do mein fork ke baad material evaluate karna ya quiet endgame move chunna hai. Yeh progression calculation ko engine line nahi, practical decision-making banati hai.

## Position 1 — Protected checkmate

Yeh position beginner ka first forcing-move test hai. White ke paas ek protected queen move dikh raha hai, lekin tumhe pehle verify karna hai queen capture ke baad black king ke paas koi escape square bacha ya nahi. Diagram ko dekh kar pehle tumhara move likho, phir checkmate ko king ke possible escape squares se verify karo.

![Chess position 1: protected queen mate](/diagrams/chess/phase-2/phase-2-calculation-01.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `3rk3/5p2/4P1Q1/8/8/8/6K1/8 w - - 0 1`

**Time limit / task:** 60 seconds. Do best-move candidates likho, opponent ke saare possible replies check karo, aur best move ke saath ek sentence reason do.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `1. Qxf7#`

**Short line:** `1. Qxf7#`

**Reasoning:** Queen g6 se f7 pawn capture karti hai aur king e8 ko diagonal check deti hai. White pawn e6 queen ko protect karta hai, isliye king `Kxf7` nahi kar sakta; d8 par black rook, aur baaki escape squares queen control karti hai. Yeh forcing line ko pehle safety se verify karna ka example hai.

**Common mistake:** Black rook d8 ko “capture ka escape” samajh kar queen ko bina protection check ke de dena. Aisa nahi karna—rook already apni jagah escape block kar raha hai.

</details>

## Position 2 — Knight fork

Ab forcing move opponent ke king aur ek important piece ko ek saath target karega. Is position mein tumhe pehle candidate list banaani hai, phir `check` ke baad black ke replies dekhne hain. Fork ka matlab samajh lo: ek piece ek move mein do zaroori targets par attack kar rahi hai.

![Chess position 2: knight fork](/diagrams/chess/phase-2/phase-2-calculation-02.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `2r1k3/p4p2/8/8/2N5/8/6K1/8 w - - 0 1`

**Time limit / task:** 90 seconds. `Nd6+` ke saath king ke replies enumerate karo aur rook ke capture ka best follow-up choose karo.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `1. Nd6+`

**Short line:** `1. Nd6+ Kf8 2. Nxc8`

**Reasoning:** Knight d6 se king e8 par check deta hai aur rook c8 ko bhi attack karta hai. Black ke legal replies ko dekhne ke baad, `Kf8` jaise king move ke baad knight rook capture kar leta hai. Fork mein forcing reply ko list karna, phir material result compare karna, beginner ke liye practical calculation hai.

**Common mistake:** Sirf `Nd6+` dekh kar turant click karna aur rook ko bache hue opponent ko de dena. Check aur fork dono verify kiye bina aage badhna evaluation ko adhoora karta hai.

</details>

## Position 3 — King safety first

Yahan position mein White ko initially check face karna hai. Is exercise ka point candidate move ki “looks good” feeling nahi, balki immediate king danger ko solve karna hai. Check ke baad tumhare paas ek block bhi dikh sakta hai, lekin block ke baad rook ne tumhari piece capture kar sakti hai.

![Chess position 3: king safety check](/diagrams/chess/phase-2/phase-2-calculation-03.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `4r2k/8/8/Q7/8/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1`

**Time limit / task:** 60 seconds. Paanchon legal king escapes ko scan karo, un mein se ek practical best move chuno, aur ek tempting block ka consequence likho.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `1. Kd1!`

**Short line:** `1. Kd1 Rd8+ 2. Kc1`

**Reasoning:** King e1 rook ke open e-file par check face kar raha hai. `Kd1` king ko file se nikalta hai aur central, usable square deta hai; jab rook `Rd8+` check de, king `Kc1` ke safe area mein ja sakta hai. `Qe5` block bhi legal dikhta hai, lekin rook `Rxe5+` se queen le sakta hai—yahi safety check ka practical kaam hai.

**Common mistake:** `Qe5` ko isliye chun lena kyunki woh line ko block kar raha hai. Block sirf tab helpful hai jab blocker protected ho aur opponent ke baad ke counterattack ko survive kar sake.

</details>

## Position 4 — Pinned piece ko remove karna

Is position mein bishop ke diagonal par black ka rook aur king nahi, balki knight blocker ke saath ek important relationship hai. Tumhe bishop ka move se check dene ke saath protected piece aur king safety evaluate karni hai. Yeh position forcing capture ko “sirf dekhne layak” nahi, follow-up ke saath samajhne layak banati hai.

![Chess position 4: bishop removes a blocker](/diagrams/chess/phase-2/phase-2-calculation-04.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `1r2k3/8/2n5/1B6/8/8/6K1/8 w - - 0 1`

**Time limit / task:** 90 seconds. Bishop ke checks identify karo, capture ke baad black ke legal king replies enumerate karo, aur knight capture hone ke baad material result likho.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `1. Bxc6+`

**Short line:** `1. Bxc6+ Kf8`

**Reasoning:** Bishop b5 ka diagonal c6-d7-e8 par tha, lekin black knight c6 us line ko block kar raha tha. `Bxc6+` blocker ko capture karta hai aur king e8 ko check deta hai; knight free capture nahi ho sakta. Black ke king escape ko dekhne ke baad material gain clear hai, aur tumhara bishop central, active square par aata hai.

**Common mistake:** `Bd7` jaise quiet move karke knight ko free dena. Move legal lag sakta hai, lekin is position ka immediate tactical gain `Bxc6+` se zyada practical hai.

</details>

## Position 5 — Two targets, one move

Ab target rook ki jagah black queen hai. Is baar tumhein pehle se pata hai knight ka fork pattern, isliye exercise ka naya part hai candidate safety aur follow-up capture ko bina over-calculation ke evaluate karna. Opponent ka har reply alag future position banata hai; un sab par equal time mat lagao.

![Chess position 5: knight forks king and queen](/diagrams/chess/phase-2/phase-2-calculation-05.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `2q1k3/8/8/8/2N5/8/6K1/8 w - - 0 1`

**Time limit / task:** 90 seconds. Forking move ko do targets ke naam ke saath identify karo, king ke replies check karo, aur queen capture wale follow-up ko explain karo.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `1. Nd6+`

**Short line:** `1. Nd6+ Kf8 2. Nxc8`

**Reasoning:** Knight d6 par black king e8 aur queen c8 dono ko attack karta hai. King move ke baad queen capture mil jata hai, isliye black ko knight se free nahi bachana. Yeh position dikhati hai ki check aur fork ko ek hi candidate mein combine kiya ja sakta hai, lekin follow-up result phir bhi count karna hota hai.

**Common mistake:** `Ne5` jaise bishop-free knight move ko choose karna aur black queen ko escape karne dena. Move legal hai, lekin position ka forcing target miss ho jata hai.

</details>

## Position 6 — Quiet king move

Aakhri position quiet hai: koi check ya capture nahi hai. Yahan tumhe pawn ke liye key square, opposition (dono kings ke aamne-saamne control), aur future approach ka simple evaluation karni hai. Pawn endgame ke deep formulas abhi nahi chahiye; bas dekho king ka next square kya future ko better banata hai.

![Chess position 6: king reaches a key square](/diagrams/chess/phase-2/phase-2-calculation-06.svg)

**Side to move:** White

**FEN:** `2k5/8/2K5/2P5/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1`

**Time limit / task:** 2 minutes. Chaar legal king moves ko compare karo, `Kd6` se pawn ko key square par le jaane wala plan likho, aur immediate danger ke liye dekhna na bhoolein.

<details>
<summary>Answer and reasoning</summary>

**Best move:** `1. Kd6!`

**Short line:** `1. Kd6 Kd8 2. c6`

**Reasoning:** White king c6 se d6 par jaakar pawn c5 ke aage key square b6-c6-d6 mein enter karta hai. King pawn ko protect bhi karta hai aur black ko future promotion path par zyada resistance deta hai. `Kc?` ya pawn ko dheere push karne wale moves distance ke hisaab se kam practical hain; full endgame theory ki zaroorat nahi hai.

**Common mistake:** `Kb5` ya `Kd5` jaise king retreat ko turant “safe” samajh lena. Safety ke saath key square aur pawn distance dono evaluate karne hote hain; ek retreat future ko slow kar sakta hai.

</details>

## What It Is NOT

Exercise pack ka answer koi engine certificate nahi hai; woh sirf ek practical next move aur uska reason dikhata hai. `Qxf7#`, `Nd6+`, ya `Kd6` jaise answers is position ka clearest learning example hain, iska matlab har future game mein wahi move compulsory nahi.

Checkpoint ka score bhi rating guarantee nahi hai. Agar kuch positions galat hui hain to reason likhne ki practice repeat karo, aur agar sahi hain to bhi next real game mein move ke baad ek short evaluation note karo.
