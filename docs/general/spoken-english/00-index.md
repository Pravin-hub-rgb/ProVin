# Spoken English Course — Vinowski Edition

## Your Goals
- Get a call center job
- Speak confidently in meetings
- Explain projects and ideas clearly
- Clear job interviews in English

## How This Course Works

No grammar rules. No textbooks. You learn the way a child learns — by hearing, repeating, and using. The method is called **shadowing** and it is the fastest way to get fluent.

Every phase has:
- A clear goal
- Explanation of concepts
- Example sentences and dialogues
- Practice exercises
- Shadowing video recommendations
- A recording exercise — you speak, you listen back, you improve

**One rule: always practice out loud. Never in your head.**

## The PREP Framework — Quick Reference

Use this for any unexpected question in any situation.

- **P — Point** — Say your answer directly
- **R — Reason** — Say why
- **E — Example** — Give a real example
- **P — Point** — Repeat your answer to close

## Quick Phrase Card

| Situation | What to say |
|---|---|
| Didn't understand | "Could you please clarify that?" |
| Need time to think | "That's a good question. Let me think for a moment." |
| Agreeing | "That makes a lot of sense." |
| Disagreeing | "I see your point, however..." |
| Explaining | "Basically what this means is..." |
| Closing a call | "Is there anything else I can help you with?" |
| Filler replacement | Pause. Silence sounds more confident than "uh." |

## How Long Will This Take?

There is no fixed timeline. Each phase should be practiced until it feels comfortable — not just read and moved on from. A rough guide:

- Phases 1–2: 2–3 days each
- Phases 3–6: 3–5 days each
- Phase 7: Ongoing, runs parallel to everything else
- Phase 8: One full day, then repeat weekly

Start with Phase 1. Do not skip ahead.
