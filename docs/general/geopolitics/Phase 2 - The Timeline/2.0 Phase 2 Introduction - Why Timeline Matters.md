Phase 1 is finished. You now know India's shape, its seven neighbours, the five red lines, the water borders, the world's chokepoints, and the Chicken's Neck.

But one question has been sitting underneath all of it.

**Why is it like this?**

The Radcliffe Line is from 1947. The McMahon Line is from 1914. The Durand Line is from 1893. The Chicken's Neck dates from the 1950s. The Himalayas have always been there — but the *lines drawn across them* did not exist until somebody drew them.

You have learned **space**. Now you learn **time**.

But first, something that stops most people before they start.

# Phase 2 - Waqt Ka Naksha (The Timeline)

## Why people think timelines are boring, and why they are wrong

Most people's reaction to the word "timeline" is: *oh, dates to memorise.* That is a completely mistaken idea, and it is the reason most people never build a real understanding of history.

Here is a better way to think about it.

Imagine you get a new phone. You start using it straight away — it works fine. Now imagine you are also given the **complete manual**: what shipped in 2020, what changed in 2023, what was added in 2025, what broke in 2027.

Read that manual once. After that, whenever the phone does something strange — the battery drains fast, a feature is missing — you will immediately know *why*, because you know what changed in that year.

**A timeline is the manual for the world.**

And that is exactly what Phase 2 is for you. Because right now you have all the geography of Phase 1 — and geography without history is just a picture. **Every line and every conflict you learned about has a date attached to it, and that date is the "why."**

## How to read this phase

One rule, and it applies to every file in Phase 2.

**Never read a timeline as a list of things that happened. Read it as a list of *interests colliding*.**

Every event in this phase will involve at least two parties who wanted different things. Your job is to notice what each of them wanted, and what they were each afraid of.

Once you do that, dates stop being dates and start being **reasons**.

Because a timeline is not a list of facts. **A timeline is a list of decisions, made by people with interests, which is exactly what Phase 3 will formalise.**
